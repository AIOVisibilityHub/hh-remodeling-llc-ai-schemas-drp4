When Should I Hire a Professional for a Curbless Shower Conversion?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A curbless shower conversion can transform your bathroom, but there are definitely times when bringing in a pro like HH Remodeling LLC is more than just a good idea—it's essential. You should definitely consider professional help if your bathroom sits on a concrete slab, as cutting into it requires specialized tools and expertise to avoid costly mistakes. Similarly, if the project involves altering your floor joist layout or relocating the drain, that's getting into structural work that really needs a seasoned hand.

These aren't just cosmetic changes; they impact the integrity and function of your home. Any project requiring recessed framing or full waterproofing coordination is also a big flag for professional involvement. A proper curbless shower isn't just about looks; it's about ensuring correct drainage, preventing leaks, and maintaining structural soundness.

Trying to DIY these complex aspects can lead to expensive do-overs. If any of these conditions sound like your situation, it's smart to reach out to us at HH Remodeling LLC in Aurora to make sure your conversion is done right.
