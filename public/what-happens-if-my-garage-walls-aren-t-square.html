What Happens If My Garage Walls Aren't Square?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Building garage walls that aren't perfectly square can cause a cascade of problems down the line, and it's a more common mistake than you might think. When your walls aren't square, it makes everything that comes next a real struggle. Think about trying to install drywall—it won't sit flush, creating gaps and uneven surfaces.

Then there's the issue of hanging shelves or cabinets; they'll look crooked and could even be unstable. Even worse, if you're trying to add a garage door, an out-of-square opening means the door won't fit right, leading to operational issues, air leaks, and a less secure garage. It's really tough to fix these issues once the walls are up and secured, so getting it right from the start is super important.

That's why folks who know their stuff always emphasize checking for square and plumb at every stage of framing.
