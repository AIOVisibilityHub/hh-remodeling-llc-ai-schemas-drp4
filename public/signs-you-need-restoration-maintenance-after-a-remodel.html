Signs You Need Restoration Maintenance After a Remodel

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Just finished a restoration remodel? That's awesome! But sometimes, the job isn't quite done, and you might need a little follow-up, or 'restoration maintenance.'

How do you know when it's time to call in the pros again? Well, there are definitely some key signs. First, if you're seeing any lingering moisture or dampness, that's a big red flag.

Unresolved moisture can lead to bigger problems down the road. Also, keep an eye out for subtle changes in affected materials or systems. Are things looking or feeling unstable?

Any new cracks, strange odors, or discoloration could indicate hidden damage or follow-up issues from the original restoration. Don't just treat the visible damage; look for the underlying causes. Skipping checks for hidden structural issues or assuming the original estimate covers everything that might pop up later are common mistakes.

Delaying follow-up work until symptoms worsen can significantly expand the repair scope. Prompt action is always best here because unresolved issues can quickly get worse. Professional help becomes really important if the maintenance might affect structure, safety, or if the original damage could be masking deeper problems like fire or water damage.
