Choosing the Right Door Casing Style for Your Home

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Picking out new door casings can feel like a small detail, but it really makes a huge difference in your home's overall look and feel. You're probably wondering what size and style will best complement your existing decor, and whether you need to yank out all your old trim first. When you're choosing, think about the material, the profile (that's the shape of the trim!)

, and the finish. Do you want something sleek and modern, or a more traditional, ornate look? It’s also important to consider how well the new casing will flow with your door itself and any other trim in the room.

You also need to think about how well the material can be cut cleanly, especially if your door opening isn't perfectly square. Sometimes, if walls are a bit uneven, or the opening is irregular, custom fitting is key. A professional can help guide you through these choices and ensure the installation looks seamless, whether you're in Highlands Ranch or Boulder.

It's about getting that perfect, tailored finish that makes your house truly feel like a home.
