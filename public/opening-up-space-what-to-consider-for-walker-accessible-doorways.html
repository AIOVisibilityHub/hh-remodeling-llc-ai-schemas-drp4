Opening Up Space: What to Consider for Walker Accessible Doorways

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Making your doorways friendly for a walker user involves more than just thinking about width, although that's super important too! When you're assessing a doorway, first, measure the usable opening to make sure there's enough room not only for the walker but also for the person's shoulders and hands as they navigate through. Typically, 32-36 inches of clear clearance is a good range for comfortable navigation.

But don't stop there! You also need to inspect thresholds; even a slight bump can be a tripping hazard or make pushing a walker difficult. Door hardware matters too – is it easy to grasp and operate with one hand?
