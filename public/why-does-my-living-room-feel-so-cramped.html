Why Does My Living Room Feel So Cramped?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ever walk into your living room and feel like you're playing a game of human Tetris just to get to the couch? It's a super common problem, especially in Denver homes where space can be at a premium. The good news is, a lot of the time it's not about the size of the room, but how you've set up your pathways and furniture. We've all been there, trying to squeeze past a coffee table or navigate a tight corner.

First, think about your main traffic route. Where do people naturally walk to get from one part of your home to another through the living room? This path needs to be prioritized.

Aim for about 36 inches wide for this main thoroughfare; it really makes a difference. Then, tackle secondary paths, like getting to a specific chair or a window – these can be a bit narrower, around 30 inches. Next, consider your biggest furniture pieces.

Place your sofa and main seating first, then build around them. It's easy to block doorways or create awkward zigzags if you don't map this out early. Finally, don't forget the small but mighty coffee table.

You want about 14 to 18 inches between it and your sofa for comfortable access, but not so much that it feels miles away.
