Avoiding Common Ramp Repair Mistakes

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When it comes to ramp repair, it's easy to make a few common blunders that can turn a small issue into a bigger headache. First off, a frequent mistake is treating surface wear as purely cosmetic. You might think a little scuff or faded paint is no big deal, but it could be a sign of deeper issues, especially if the surface is losing its slip resistance.

Another common oversight? Ignoring loose fasteners. A wobbly ramp might just need some tightening, but delaying that simple fix can lead to structural damage over time.

It's like ignoring a small crack in your windshield – it usually just gets worse! Also, delaying repairs after you spot visible damage or notice a wobble is a huge no-no. Small issues often become bigger, more expensive problems, and more importantly, they become safety risks.

Periodic checks are your best friend here, helping you catch things before they escalate.
