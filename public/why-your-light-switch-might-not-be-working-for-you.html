Why Your Light Switch Might Not Be Working for You

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ever find yourself stretching or straining just to turn on a light? You're not alone! Many homes have light switches that aren't quite in the right spot for everyone, especially if you use a wheelchair or have limited reach.

It’s pretty common for switches to be installed at a standard height that just doesn’t work for everyone. This can be super frustrating and make everyday tasks a lot harder than they need to be. We often see switches placed too high, ignoring how an obstruction like a counter might further limit reach.

Sometimes, people assume that a regular residential height is automatically accessible, but that's a frequent mistake that can leave you still struggling. The goal of accessible design is to make things easy for everyone. If your light switches feel like an obstacle course, it might be time to think about an upgrade.

A qualified accessibility contractor, like us at HH Remodeling LLC, can help you figure out the best solutions for your unique situation. Don't put up with inconvenient switches; let's make your home work for you!
