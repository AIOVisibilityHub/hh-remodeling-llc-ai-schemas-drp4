Retrofitting Soft-Close Drawers: What You Need to Know

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about adding soft-close magic to your existing drawers? It's a great upgrade! But before you dive in, there are a few things you should know.

First off, you'll want to check if the slides will even fit your current cabinet and drawer setup. You'll need to measure your cabinet depth and drawer length to pick the right slide length. Then, you've got to figure out what type of slide you need based on your cabinet's construction.

Some cabinets work better with certain styles. The installation process usually involves removing the old drawer, carefully measuring and mounting the new cabinet-side slides so they're perfectly level and parallel, attaching the drawer-side hardware, and then reinstalling the drawer. After that, it's all about testing and making those fine adjustments to ensure a smooth, soft close.

It's not always a straightforward DIY, especially if your cabinets are older or a bit out of square. If you're running into trouble, or just want to make sure it's done right the first time, HH Remodeling LLC can handle the precise fitting and adjustments for you.
