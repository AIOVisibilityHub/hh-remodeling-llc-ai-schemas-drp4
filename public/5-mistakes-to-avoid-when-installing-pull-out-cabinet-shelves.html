5 Mistakes to Avoid When Installing Pull-Out Cabinet Shelves

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing pull-out cabinet shelves can be a game-changer for organization, but there are a few common mistakes that can turn it into a headache. Here's how to steer clear of them:

  • Choosing the Wrong Size: Don't just eyeball it! Measure the interior width, depth, and height of your cabinet precisely, accounting for any hinges or face frames. A shelf that's too wide or too shallow won't function properly.
  • Mounting Slides Unevenly: If your slides aren't perfectly level, your shelf won't glide smoothly. Always use a level to ensure both sides are installed at the exact same height.
  • Ignoring Door Swing and Obstructions: Make sure the shelf can fully extend without hitting the cabinet door or any internal elements like plumbing. Plan your hardware placement carefully.
  • Overloading the Shelf: Every pull-out shelf has a weight capacity. Piling on too much can damage the slides and make the shelf difficult to operate. Know your shelf's limits and stick to them.
  • Not Considering Cabinet Framing: If your cabinet has uneven or old framing, it can make a clean installation tricky. Sometimes you need to add shims or reinforcements.
