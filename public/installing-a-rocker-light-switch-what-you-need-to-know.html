Installing a Rocker Light Switch: What You Need to Know

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing a rocker light switch can be a great way to update your home's functionality and aesthetic. So, how does it all work? First off, you'll want to select the perfect rocker switch style for your space – there are tons of options out there!

Once you've got your new switch, the critical first step is always to turn off the power to that circuit at your breaker box. Safety first, right? Then, we carefully remove the old switch, making sure to disconnect the wiring properly.

Next, the new rocker switch goes in, along with its cover plate. It's super important to confirm that the finished height and operability of the control meet your needs, especially if you're aiming for an accessible setup. Remember, even with a modern rocker switch, if it's part of an accessibility upgrade, it still needs to be within that 15 to 48-inch reach range above the floor, considering any obstructions.

Sometimes, the existing electrical box or wiring might need a little tweaking, or you might be adding a new circuit. In those cases, having a licensed electrician or experienced contractor like HH Remodeling LLC step in is a really smart move to ensure everything is safe and up to code.
