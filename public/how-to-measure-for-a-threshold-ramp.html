How to Measure for a Threshold Ramp

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about adding a threshold ramp to make those small bumps in your home a breeze? It’s a great idea, and getting the measurements right is key to a perfect fit. Here’s how you can do it.

First, measure the exact rise at your doorway threshold. This is the vertical height difference you need the ramp to bridge. Threshold ramps are usually designed for small rises, typically in the one to three-inch range.

Once you have that measurement, you'll want to confirm the width of your doorway and make sure there's enough clearance for the ramp to sit properly without obstructing the door. Consider if the ramp will be used indoors or outdoors, as this will influence the best material choice – rubber for indoor use often, or aluminum for more weather resistance. You’ll also want to think about the surface grip and how smoothly the ramp transitions.

With these measurements, you can select a ramp that fits snugly and securely, ensuring a stable and level approach. If your threshold is a bit unusual or you're just not sure, a professional from HH Remodeling LLC can help you get it right, ensuring safety and an optimal fit.
