Why Is My New Night Light Not Turning On Automatically?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It can be frustrating when you install a new night light, expecting it to just work, and then it doesn't turn on when you need it. Often, this isn't a wiring issue but rather about the type of sensor or mode your particular unit uses. Many modern night lights come with photosensors that detect ambient light, meaning they'll only activate when the room gets dark enough.

If your night light stays off, first check if there's too much ambient light in the area. Sometimes, even light from a hallway or another room can keep it from activating. You also want to confirm if your specific night light has a 'nightlight mode' or a reduced-light setting, as these features influence operation, not necessarily the installation itself.

If you've got a hardwired unit, you should also double-check that the power is fully restored at the breaker and that all connections are secure. If you've checked these basic things and it's still not working, especially if it's a hardwired unit, it might be time to call in an expert.
