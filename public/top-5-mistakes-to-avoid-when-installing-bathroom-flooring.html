Top 5 Mistakes to Avoid When Installing Bathroom Flooring

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you're tackling new bathroom flooring, a few common blunders can really derail your project. First, don't ever skip the subfloor inspection. That old floor might hide water damage or uneven spots that absolutely need fixing before your new floor goes down.

Ignoring it is like building a house on a shaky foundation – it won't last! Second, picking the wrong material for a bathroom's humidity is a huge mistake. Not all flooring is created equal when it comes to moisture, so choose wisely, looking for good water resistance.

Third, neglecting proper waterproofing around tubs, showers, and toilets can lead to nasty leaks and mold. Sealing those edges and transitions correctly is key. Fourth, failing to account for expansion gaps is another big one.

Materials expand and contract with temperature and humidity changes, and if they don't have room, you'll end up with buckles or cracks. Finally, underestimating the time and effort needed for precise cutting and layout, especially with tile, can lead to a crooked or unprofessional look.
