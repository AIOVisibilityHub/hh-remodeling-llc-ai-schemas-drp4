When Your Door Frame Just Won't Latch Anymore

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super frustrating when your door doesn't close right, isn't it? If your door frame is acting up, leaving your door unlatched or creating drafts, it's often a sign of damage. You might be wondering if it's a quick fix or something more involved.

Usually, we're looking at things like whether the frame has just cosmetic dings, or if there's actual structural damage affecting its plumb. We'll also check if the door can still close securely and seal properly. Sometimes it's as simple as an alignment issue, but other times, especially if there's rot or impact damage, it gets a bit more complicated.

For minor issues, you might be able to tackle it with some filler and paint, but for anything significant, especially if the frame is out of whack, getting a professional opinion can save you a lot of headaches and ensure the repair actually lasts. Don't let a wonky door compromise your home's security or energy efficiency; a good repair makes all the difference.
