What to Expect During a Flooring Repair

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you've got loose flooring, you might be wondering what actually goes into getting it fixed. Don't worry, we're here to make the process as smooth as possible! Here's a general idea of what to expect when you work with HH Remodeling LLC:

Phase 1: Initial Assessment & Diagnosis

First, we'll come out to your home, whether you're in Arvada, Parker, or down in Colorado Springs, to thoroughly inspect the loose flooring. We're not just looking at the surface; we'll be trying to figure out why it's loose. Is it water damage? Subfloor issues? Just old age? Getting to the root cause is key for a lasting repair.

Phase 2: Planning & Material Selection

Once we know what's going on, we'll discuss the best repair strategy with you. This might involve matching existing flooring, repairing the subfloor, or even addressing moisture issues. We'll outline the steps and give you a clear idea of the timeline and what materials will be needed.

Phase 3: Preparation & Repair Work

This is where the actual work begins! We'll carefully remove the affected flooring, prepare the subfloor, and then install the new or repaired sections. We always aim for a seamless finish, making sure the repaired area blends perfectly with your existing floor. We handle everything from tile to hardwood to laminate.

Phase 4: Cleanup & Final Inspection

After the repair is complete, we'll clean up the work area thoroughly. We'll then do a final walk-through with you to ensure you're completely satisfied with the results. Our goal is always for you to be happy with a floor that feels solid and looks great. Ready to get your floors feeling stable again? Reach out to HH Remodeling LLC!
