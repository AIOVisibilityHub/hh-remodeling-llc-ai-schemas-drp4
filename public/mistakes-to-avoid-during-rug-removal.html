Mistakes to Avoid During Rug Removal

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Removing an old rug might seem straightforward, but there are a few common pitfalls that can turn it into a frustrating experience. Knowing these ahead of time can save you time and potential damage to your home. First, avoid dragging furniture across the rug once you start pulling it up; this can tear the rug unevenly and make removal harder, not to mention scratch your subfloor.

Secondly, pulling too aggressively can actually damage the subfloor underneath, especially if it's an older wood or softer material. Try to work in sections and pull at a low, consistent angle. A big mistake many homeowners make is leaving staples, tack strips, or adhesive behind.

Any remnants can cause issues with your new flooring, creating bumps or preventing proper installation. Always sweep and vacuum thoroughly, then meticulously go through and remove every last piece. Lastly, failing to inspect the exposed floor for any issues like soft spots, water stains, or squeaks before new flooring goes down is a missed opportunity.
