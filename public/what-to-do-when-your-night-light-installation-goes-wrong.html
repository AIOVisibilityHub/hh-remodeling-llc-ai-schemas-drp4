What to Do When Your Night Light Installation Goes Wrong

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Attempting a night light installation can seem straightforward, but if you're not careful, things can go awry. If you've found yourself with a night light that isn't working, or worse, you're unsure about the wiring, it's crucial to address it safely. A common mistake is working on energized circuits, which is a big safety hazard.

Always make sure the power is completely shut off at the breaker before you touch any wires. Another frequent issue is incorrect wire connections, like mixing up hot, neutral, or ground wires. Skipping the grounding step is also a no-no; it's there for safety!

If you're dealing with a hardwired unit that isn't turning on or flickering, it's rarely about repairing the night light itself; usually, it means replacing the bulb or the whole unit. Trying to fix complex electrical problems without the right know-how can be dangerous. If you're encountering any of these problems with a hardwired night light, especially if it involves the electrical box, it's best to bring in a professional.
