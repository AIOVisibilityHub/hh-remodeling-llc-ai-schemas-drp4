Why Do My Stair Lights Flicker (And How to Fix It)

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Flickering stairway lights can be more than just annoying; they can be a sign of underlying electrical issues or improper installation. Sometimes it's as simple as an incompatible bulb or a loose connection, but other times it points to more complex problems with the driver or even the mains connection. If your stairway lighting, whether it's sleek LED strips or classic wall sconces, starts to act up, you'll want to address it quickly.

Persistent flickering or lights that trip breakers definitely signals it's time to call in the pros, especially since staircase lighting is often a hard-wired circuit. A qualified professional can help you troubleshoot persistent issues like flickering or breaker trips, ensuring your stairway lighting is both functional and safe. They can also review your setup to confirm it meets local lighting and stair safety requirements, giving you peace of mind that your home is up to code.

Don't let flickering lights be a hazard; get them checked out to keep your home bright and secure.
