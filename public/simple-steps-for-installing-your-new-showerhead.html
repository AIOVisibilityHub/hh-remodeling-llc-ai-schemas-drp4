Simple Steps for Installing Your New Showerhead

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Putting in a new showerhead, whether it’s a standard adjustable one or a handheld unit, is a pretty common DIY task. But to make sure it works perfectly and doesn't leak, following a few simple steps makes all the difference.

Step 1: Remove the Old Showerhead. Grab a wrench (if needed, but often hand-loosening works) and twist off your old showerhead. Be careful not to damage the shower arm.

Step 2: Clean the Threads. This is super important! Make sure there's no old thread tape residue or mineral buildup on the shower arm threads. A clean surface ensures a good seal for your new unit.

Step 3: Apply the Right Seal. Check your new showerhead's instructions. Some units need new thread seal tape wrapped clockwise around the shower arm threads. Others rely on a rubber washer inside the showerhead or bracket. For handheld units, make sure any included diverter or bracket is installed correctly here with its seals.

Step 4: Install the New Showerhead. Carefully screw on your new showerhead (or the bracket for a handheld). Hand-tighten it first, and only use a wrench gently if the instructions say so and it’s still loose. Overtightening is a common mistake! For handhelds, connect the hose, making sure any washers are in place and the vacuum breaker (if included) is oriented correctly.

Step 5: Test for Leaks and Functionality. Turn on the water and check thoroughly for any drips. If it's an adjustable showerhead, cycle through all the spray settings to ensure they work.
