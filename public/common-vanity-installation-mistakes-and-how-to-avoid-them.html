Common Vanity Installation Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing a new bathroom vanity might seem straightforward, but there are some classic slip-ups that can turn your project into a real headache. One of the biggest gotchas is poor measurements. You've really got to be precise with your space and make sure the new vanity fits, both in width and depth.

Also, forgetting to account for door and drawer clearances is a big one. You don't want your new vanity blocking a doorway or another cabinet! Another frequent issue is ignoring where your plumbing rough-ins are.

If your new vanity's sink doesn't line up with the existing pipes, you're looking at extra plumbing work, which means more time and money. We often see people fail to check for damage behind the old vanity, too. Walls can hide all sorts of surprises, like water damage or mold, that need to be addressed before the new unit goes in.

Lastly, poor sealing around the sink and countertop is a common culprit for future water damage. It's crucial to seal everything properly to prevent moisture from seeping into your new investment.
