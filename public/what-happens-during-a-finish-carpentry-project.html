What Happens During a Finish Carpentry Project?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ever wonder what goes into making your home's trim and molding look so good? A finish carpentry project with HH Remodeling LLC follows a pretty clear timeline to get those perfect results. Phase 1 is all about planning and preparation.

We'll measure the areas, discuss material options like wood species and profiles that match your home's style, and finalize the layout. This ensures everything is mapped out for accuracy before we even make a cut. Phase 2 involves the actual installation.

Our team will make precise cuts, fit each piece meticulously, and securely fasten them into place, paying close attention to details like miters and transitions. This is where the craftsmanship really shines. Phase 3 focuses on the finishing touches.

We'll meticulously fill all nail holes, caulk any joints for a seamless look, and then sand the surfaces to create a smooth, paint-ready finish. This final stage is crucial for that clean, professional appearance that makes all the difference.
