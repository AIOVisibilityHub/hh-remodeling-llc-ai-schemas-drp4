Planning Your Kitchen Lighting Upgrade: Where to Start

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

So, you're thinking about upgrading your kitchen lighting? Awesome! It's one of the best ways to transform the space.

A good starting point is to first assess your current lighting. What's working, and more importantly, what isn't? Do you have dark spots where you prep food?

Is the overall room too dim or too bright? Next, decide what you want to achieve. Are you looking for better task lighting over your counters, or do you want to boost the general ambient glow of the room?

Often, under-cabinet lighting is a fantastic part of a bigger plan, not just a standalone add-on, giving you that perfect blend of functionality and ambiance. After that, you'll want to select the right fixture types and figure out the best placement. It's also super important to check your existing electrical capacity.

Can your current wiring support new fixtures, or do you need to consider some electrical modifications? This is where a pro like HH Remodeling LLC, serving the Denver area, can really help you plan it all out and make sure your vision comes to life safely and beautifully.
