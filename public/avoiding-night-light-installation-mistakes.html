Avoiding night light installation mistakes

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing a bedroom night light seems simple enough, right? But folks often make a few common blunders that can turn a helpful light into a headache. One big one is placing the light in the wrong spot, like directly in your line of sight when you're lying in bed.

That's a surefire way to get glared at! Always think about how you'll be using the light and from what angles. Another mistake is mounting it at the wrong height; too high and it's not effective path lighting, too low and it might not spread light where you need it.

People also often ignore their home's electrical capacity, leading to tripped breakers or worse. It’s crucial to check your existing circuit to make sure it can handle the new fixture. And if you're swapping out a dimmer, make sure it's compatible with your new LED night light – not all dimmers play nice with LEDs!

Poor wire connections or shoddy junction-box work are also big no-nos. If you're not confident about the wiring, or if it involves anything more complex than a simple plug-in, it's always smarter to bring in a pro.
