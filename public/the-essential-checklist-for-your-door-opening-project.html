The Essential Checklist for Your Door Opening Project

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Planning a new door opening? A little preparation goes a long way to ensure everything goes smoothly. Here's a quick checklist to guide you:

  • Determine Door Type and Size: What kind of door are you installing (pre-hung, slab, bifold)? What are its exact dimensions? This is your starting point for the rough opening size.
  • Calculate Rough Opening: Remember the rule of thumb: door width + 2 inches, door height + 2.5 inches. Write this down!
  • Identify Load-Bearing Status: Is the wall you're cutting into load-bearing or non-load-bearing? This dramatically impacts the framing requirements, especially for header size. If you're not sure, get a pro to check it out.
  • Gather Your Materials: You'll need king studs, jack (or trimmer) studs, a header, and potentially cripple studs. Make sure you have enough lumber of the correct size and grade.
  • Tools Ready: Tape measure, level, saw, hammer/nail gun, and shims are your best friends here.
  • Plan for Utilities: Check for any electrical, plumbing, or HVAC lines in the wall before you start cutting. You definitely don't want to hit those!
  • Consider Finishes: How will the door opening look with existing trim, drywall, and flooring? Plan ahead to ensure a seamless transition.

Following this checklist can prevent common missteps. However, if you're dealing with a load-bearing wall or have any doubts, don't hesitate to call a professional like HH Remodeling.
