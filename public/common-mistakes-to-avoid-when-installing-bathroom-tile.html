Common Mistakes to Avoid When Installing Bathroom Tile

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's easy to get excited about new bathroom tile and overlook some critical details, but trust me, avoiding common pitfalls will save you a ton of grief (and money!) down the road. One of the biggest mistakes we see is using the wrong type of adhesive, like mastic, in wet areas.

Mastic just isn't designed for constant moisture, and it can lead to tile failure. Another huge one? Skipping waterproof membranes.

Just because tile and grout are on the surface doesn't mean your shower or bathroom floor is magically waterproof. Water can still seep through and cause damage underneath. People also often forget about movement joints.

These are essential for allowing your tile to expand and contract with temperature changes, preventing cracks. And finally, poor thinset combing, which leaves air pockets under the tile, can lead to hollow spots and weak points. To avoid these issues, always verify the correct materials for wet applications, ensure proper waterproofing systems are in place, and confirm that movement joints are included where needed.
