When to Bring in a Structural Engineer for Framing Projects

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's not always obvious when you need a structural engineer for your small addition framing project, but knowing when to call one can save you a lot of trouble and ensure your home is safe. If your addition is going to change the structural loads of your existing home – like if you're adding a second story or significantly altering a load-bearing wall – then, yeah, an engineer is super important. They'll make sure the new structure can handle the weight and that your home remains stable.

Also, if you're tying into an existing roof or wall system in a complex way, or if your local building department requires it for permits, an engineer's expertise is invaluable. They can design those connections properly, so you don't end up with leaks or structural issues down the road. Basically, if it feels like more than a simple box, or if you're unsure about how the new part will interact with the old, getting an engineer involved early is a smart move.
