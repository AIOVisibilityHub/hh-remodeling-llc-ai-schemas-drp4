When Your Door Just Won't Close Right

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super frustrating when a door in your home starts acting up, right? Maybe it's sticking, rubbing against the frame, or just refusing to latch properly. Often, these issues aren't just annoying; they can be signs of underlying problems like hinge wear, foundation shifts, or even just old, tired weatherstripping.

We often find that folks assume it's just the latch, but that's rarely the full story. Sometimes, simple adjustments can make a world of difference, like tightening hinge screws or tweaking the strike plate. But other times, especially if the problem changes with the seasons or after a big home project, it might point to something deeper.

Knowing whether the frame is plumb and level is a great starting point. If you've tried the basics and your door is still misbehaving, or if you're seeing repeated issues, it's a good idea to consider getting some professional eyes on it. We've seen it all in Denver and the surrounding areas, and sometimes a pro can spot things you might miss, saving you headaches down the road.
