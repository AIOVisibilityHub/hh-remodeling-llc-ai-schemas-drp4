Why Your Outdoor Lighting Project Might Not Look Right

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ever installed outdoor lighting only to find it doesn't quite hit the mark? It's a common frustration, and usually, a few missteps are to blame. One big issue is picking low-quality fixtures or components.

You might save a buck upfront, but these often won't stand up to Colorado's weather and will fail sooner rather than later. Another frequent mistake is not confirming that all your parts are truly waterproof and outdoor-rated. It sounds obvious, but it's easy to overlook, and water damage is a real headache.

Plus, skipping a proper design phase that considers fixture placement and how you might want to expand in the future can leave you with a hodgepodge look instead of a cohesive, beautiful display. Think about where you want light, what you want to highlight, and how the system can grow with your needs. When you're dealing with hard-wiring, transformer sizing, or anything at the main electrical panel, that's when a qualified professional becomes essential.
