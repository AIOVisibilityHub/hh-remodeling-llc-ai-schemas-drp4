Under Cabinet Lighting Mistakes to Avoid

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you're diving into under cabinet lighting, there are a few common slip-ups that can turn your bright idea into a dim outcome. First, don't measure too late! Always get your measurements right before you buy anything to ensure your lights fit perfectly.

Another frequent mistake is not leaving enough wire reach between cabinets, which can make routing a headache. For LED strips, mixing up positive and negative polarity is a big no-no – they simply won't work. Also, watch out for weak connections; make sure everything is snug and secure, or you'll have flickering or non-functional lights.

Poor surface prep is a real buzzkill; if your cabinets aren't clean, the adhesive won't stick, and your lights will sag. And please, don't leave wires visible! The whole point of sleek under cabinet lighting is a clean look.

Finally, choosing an incompatible dimmer or control method can mess up the whole system. If you're feeling overwhelmed or want to avoid these pitfalls, HH Remodeling LLC is here to help plan and install your lighting right the first time, especially in areas like Denver, Centennial, or Aurora.
