Can You Make Your Current Bathroom Floor Less Slippery?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

You've got an existing bathroom floor, and you're wondering if you can make it safer without a full replacement. Good news – sometimes you can! There are a few strategies you can explore to increase the slip resistance of your current flooring. It often depends on the material you already have.

One option is chemical etching, which can subtly alter the surface of certain tiles to create more microscopic grip. There are also anti-slip coatings available that can be applied to your existing floor, though you'll want to make sure they're durable and compatible with your current material. For quick fixes, anti-slip tapes or strips can be added to specific high-traffic wet areas, and good old-fashioned bath mats with rubberized backing are always a simple, effective solution.

However, it's important to be realistic about expectations. While these methods can help, they might not achieve the same level of slip resistance as a purpose-built non-slip floor.
