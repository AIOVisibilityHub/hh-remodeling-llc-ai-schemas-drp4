Cabinet Not Big Enough for a Pull Down Shelf? Here's What to Check

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about adding a pull-down cabinet shelf but worried your cabinet might not have enough space? It's a common concern! Before you buy, you'll definitely want to measure the interior carefully.

Don't just measure the opening; make sure to check the overall width, depth, and height of the cabinet's interior. You also need to confirm there's enough clearance around the mechanism, especially for any nearby plumbing, wiring, or existing shelf supports. These things can interfere with the shelf's movement, causing binding or uneven operation if they're in the way.

Also, consider the strength of your cabinet's structure. These shelves carry a fair bit of weight, and you want to ensure your cabinet can handle the bracket load without issues.
