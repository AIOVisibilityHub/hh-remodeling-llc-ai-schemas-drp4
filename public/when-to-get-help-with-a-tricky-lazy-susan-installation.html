When to Get Help with a Tricky Lazy Susan Installation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing a Lazy Susan can significantly improve your kitchen's organization, but sometimes the DIY path hits a snag. Knowing when to call in a professional can save you a lot of headache and potential damage. Here are some key indicators:

  • Irregular Corner Cabinets: If your corner cabinet isn't a standard size or shape, getting a pre-made Lazy Susan to fit perfectly can be a real challenge.
  • Uneven Surfaces: If your cabinet box or the floor within the cabinet is out of level, it can cause the Lazy Susan to rub, wobble, or bind during rotation. A professional can correct these issues.
  • Custom Fitting Needed: Sometimes, a Lazy Susan unit needs trimming or custom adjustments to fit snugly and operate smoothly, which requires specific tools and expertise.
  • Obstructions: If you have existing plumbing, wiring, or other fixed obstructions inside the cabinet that the Lazy Susan needs to work around, a pro can navigate these complexities safely.
