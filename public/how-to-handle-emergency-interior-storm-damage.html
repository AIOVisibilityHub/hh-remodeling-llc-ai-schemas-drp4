How to Handle Emergency Interior Storm Damage

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Dealing with emergency interior storm damage can feel like a whirlwind, but breaking it down into steps makes it more manageable. Here’s a process that usually works best:

Step 1: Secure the Area and Document. As soon as it's safe, your first move is to make the area secure. This might mean shutting off utilities if there’s a risk, but only if you know how to do it safely. Then, grab your phone or camera and thoroughly document all the damage with photos and videos. Don't touch anything yet; just get clear evidence for your insurance company.

Step 2: Prevent Further Loss. This is where temporary mitigation comes in. Think tarps over a damaged roof, boarding up broken windows, or using a wet vac to remove standing water. The goal here is to stop the problem from escalating. This isn’t the permanent fix, just a hold-over.

Step 3: Contact Your Insurer and a Professional. As soon as these first two steps are handled, reach out to your insurance company. Many policies require timely reporting, often within 24-48 hours. Then, it's smart to call in a qualified professional. Especially if there's significant water, structural concerns, or potential electrical issues, you'll want someone experienced.
