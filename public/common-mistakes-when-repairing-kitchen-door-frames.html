Common Mistakes When Repairing Kitchen Door Frames

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Tackling a kitchen door frame repair can seem straightforward, but it's easy to make a few common blunders that can lead to more headaches. One frequent mistake is trying to use wood filler for structural problems like severe rot or a deeply split jamb. Filler is great for cosmetic fixes, but it won't provide the strength needed for structural integrity.

Always address the underlying issue first! Another common error is not properly preparing the surface; make sure the area is clean, dry, and free of loose debris before applying any materials. Failing to check the door's alignment and latch after the repair is another big one.

Even a small repair can throw things off, so always test that the door swings freely, latches securely, and seals properly. Don't forget to use appropriate screws or reinforcements if you're replacing sections, ensuring they're long enough to hold everything firmly. Lastly, rushing the finishing steps like sanding, caulking, priming, and painting can leave a noticeable repair.
