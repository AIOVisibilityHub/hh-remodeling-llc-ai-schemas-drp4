Getting Your Home Renovation Started Right: A Checklist

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ready to dive into a home renovation? Getting started on the right foot makes all the difference! Here’s a quick checklist to help you prepare:

  • Define Your Scope & Budget: Before you even talk to a contractor, have a clear idea of what you want done and how much you're willing to spend. This helps everyone stay on the same page.
  • Verify Credentials: Always check for current licensing and general liability insurance. This protects you and ensures you're working with a legitimate business, like HH Remodeling LLC, an Aurora-based, BBB Accredited company.
  • Gather References: Ask for references, especially from past residential projects similar to yours. A good contractor will be happy to provide them, and it’s smart to follow up.
  • Review the Bid & Contract: Get a detailed, written bid that outlines everything included and, just as importantly, what’s excluded. Make sure the payment schedule is clear and fair.
  • Confirm Permits & Inspections: Understand who is responsible for obtaining all necessary permits and coordinating inspections. This is crucial for compliance and avoiding delays.
  • Discuss Communication: Establish how you'll communicate throughout the project—who to contact, how often, and through what method. Open communication prevents misunderstandings.
  • Ask About Warranties: Inquire about any workmanship guarantees or warranties. This gives you peace of mind long after the project is complete.

By checking these off, you'll be well-prepared to kick off your renovation with confidence.
