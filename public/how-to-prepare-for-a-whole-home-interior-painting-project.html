How to Prepare for a Whole-Home Interior Painting Project

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting ready for a full interior paint job can feel like a big undertaking, but a little preparation on your end makes the whole process smoother and faster. Here's a checklist to help you get organized: 1. Declutter and clear pathways: Move smaller items, décor, and wall hangings out of the rooms that will be painted.

This gives the painters clear access to the walls and helps protect your belongings. 2. Address any major repairs: While painters handle minor wall imperfections, significant cracks or holes should be discussed beforehand.

If there's underlying moisture or structural issues, it's best to fix those first. 3. Finalize your color choices: Having your paint colors and finishes selected before the project starts avoids delays.

Consider swatches on your walls to see how colors look in different lighting. 4. Discuss furniture moving: Clarify with your contractor who is responsible for moving larger furniture pieces.

Often, they'll assist, but knowing upfront prevents surprises. 5. Plan for pets and kids: Think about where pets and children will be during the painting process to ensure their safety and minimize disruptions.

A clear plan ensures a beautiful, hassle-free painting experience.
