When Your Trim Paint Just Won't Stick

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ever painted your interior trim only to have it peel or chip way too soon? It’s super frustrating, right? A lot of times, this happens because of some common mistakes in the prep work.

For example, if you paint over a waxy residue or a really glossy surface without giving it a good sand, that new paint just won't have anything to grab onto. Another big one is skipping the degreasing and cleaning step. All those fingerprints and everyday grime prevent proper adhesion.

And if you're trying to put water-based paint over old oil-based trim without a bonding primer, that's a recipe for disaster. The two simply won't play nice together. Sometimes, folks also leave masking tape on until the paint is fully cured, and when they pull it off, it takes a chunk of paint with it!

Avoiding these blunders can make a huge difference in how long your paint job lasts. If you're dealing with heavily damaged trim, or if you know it's got an old oil-based finish, getting a professional opinion can save you a lot of headache (and repaint jobs!) down the line.
