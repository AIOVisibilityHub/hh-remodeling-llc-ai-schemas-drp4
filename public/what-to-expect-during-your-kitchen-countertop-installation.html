What to Expect During Your Kitchen Countertop Installation?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

So, you’ve picked out your gorgeous new kitchen countertops, and now you’re wondering what happens on installation day. It’s a pretty straightforward process, but knowing what to expect can ease your mind! First off, we’ll make sure your cabinets are in place and properly supported – this is crucial for a stable, long-lasting countertop.

Then, we’ll take precise measurements or create a template to ensure everything fits perfectly. Once your new countertops are fabricated off-site, the installation itself can often happen in about a day for simpler jobs. Our crew handles the careful placement, securing everything, and making sure all the seams, sinks, and overhangs look just right.

Sometimes, we might need access to plumbing for sink cutouts, so it's a good idea to have those areas cleared. Before we arrive, it's super helpful if you clear out your cabinets, protect your floors, and remove any valuables from the work area. This helps us work efficiently and keeps your home safe.

After we're done, we’ll do a final check to ensure everything is perfect and answer any questions you have. We want you to be absolutely thrilled with your new kitchen!
