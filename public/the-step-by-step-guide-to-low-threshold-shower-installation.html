The Step-by-Step Guide to Low-Threshold Shower Installation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing a low-threshold shower can really upgrade your bathroom's accessibility and style. Here's a general idea of the process we follow to ensure a smooth and successful installation. Step 1: Removal and Subfloor Prep.

We start by carefully removing your old shower or tub. Once that’s out, we inspect the subfloor for any damage and make necessary repairs, ensuring a solid, level base for your new shower. This is super important for preventing future issues!

Step 2: Drain and Base Installation. Next, we verify the drain's location and fit for your new low-threshold base. The base is then precisely installed, making sure the slope is correct so water flows perfectly to the drain.

This step is critical for preventing standing water and ensuring proper drainage. Step 3: Wall Panels and Waterproofing. With the base in, we install the wall panels.

This is where meticulous waterproofing comes into play – sealing all seams and corners to protect your home from moisture. We use high-quality materials and techniques to create a watertight enclosure. Step 4: Final Plumbing and Curing.

We connect all the plumbing fixtures and then thoroughly test everything to ensure it’s working correctly and leak-free. After that, sealants and adhesives need time to cure, typically at least 24 hours, before the shower can be used. It’s a bit of a wait, but totally worth it for a durable, leak-proof finish!
