Common Drywall Remodeling Mistakes to Steer Clear Of

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Embarking on a drywall remodeling project? Great! But watch out for these common blunders that can derail your efforts and leave you with less-than-perfect results.

A big one is not clearly specifying the finish level in your project bid. Are you aiming for a perfectly smooth, level-five finish, or is a more basic level acceptable for areas that will be hidden? This impacts time, cost, and ultimately, your satisfaction.

Another frequent mistake is ignoring moisture sources before closing up walls, especially in bathrooms or basements. You absolutely need to address any leaks or dampness to prevent future mold or damage. Using the wrong type of drywall in damp areas, like regular drywall instead of moisture-resistant green board, is another common error.

Underestimating drying time for joint compound is also a classic; rushing this step leads to cracks and uneven surfaces. Finally, failing to confirm how texture matching and cleanup will be handled can lead to unexpected costs or a patchy look. Make sure these details are squared away upfront.
