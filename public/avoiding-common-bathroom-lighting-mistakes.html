Avoiding Common Bathroom Lighting Mistakes

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It’s easy to get excited about new bathroom lights, but some common pitfalls can lead to less-than-ideal results. Nobody wants a beautiful new fixture that doesn’t actually light the room properly or creates annoying shadows! Let's chat about a few things to watch out for to ensure your bathroom lighting truly shines.

A big mistake we often see is choosing lights purely for their appearance without considering their actual function. A gorgeous fixture that's too small for the space or doesn’t provide enough light for your daily tasks won’t serve you well. Always balance aesthetics with practicality.

Another common error is relying on a single light source, especially an overhead fixture. This often creates harsh shadows on your face, making grooming activities like shaving or applying makeup much harder. The key is to layer your lighting, combining ambient, task, and accent lights to create a well-rounded and shadow-free environment.

Ignoring the placement and scale of fixtures relative to your mirror and vanity is another one. Lights placed too high, too low, or too far apart can cast awkward shadows. Vanity lights, for example, should ideally be positioned at eye level or along the sides of the mirror to provide even illumination.

Also, forgetting about dimming compatibility can be a bummer. Dimmers offer flexibility, allowing you to adjust the mood and brightness as needed. Finally, make sure any fixtures installed near the tub or shower are rated for damp or wet locations for safety.
