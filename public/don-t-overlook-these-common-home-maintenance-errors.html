Don't Overlook These Common Home Maintenance Errors

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

We all want to keep our homes in great shape, but it's surprisingly easy to make some common maintenance mistakes that can lead to bigger problems. One of the biggest culprits is skipping routine filter changes for your HVAC system. A dirty filter makes your system work harder, driving up energy bills and potentially leading to a breakdown when you need it most.

Another frequent oversight is forgetting to replace the batteries in your smoke and carbon monoxide detectors. These are literally lifesavers, and they need fresh batteries regularly to do their job. Leaving small water issues unresolved is another classic mistake; that tiny drip can quickly become a huge headache, causing rot, mold, and expensive repairs.

And waiting until your systems fail during peak use, like your AC in summer or furnace in winter, instead of getting them serviced ahead of time, is a recipe for discomfort and emergency costs.
