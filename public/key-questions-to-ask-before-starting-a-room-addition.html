Key Questions to Ask Before Starting a Room Addition

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Embarking on a room addition project for your home in places like Highlands Ranch, Boulder, or Colorado Springs is a big step, and you’ll want to be prepared. One of the most important things you can do is ask the right questions upfront. This ensures you’re on the same page with your contractor and have a clear understanding of the process. Here are some key questions to consider:

  • What is the estimated total cost, and what does it include? Don't just get a bottom-line number. Ask for a detailed breakdown that includes labor, materials, permits, inspections, and any potential fees. Clarifying who handles permits and change-order pricing is crucial to avoid hidden costs.
  • What’s the projected timeline for the project? Understand the different phases—from planning and permitting to construction and final walkthrough—and how long each is expected to take. Room additions often take 3 to 6 months or more, so manage your expectations here.
  • How will the addition match my existing home? You want your new space to blend seamlessly. Ask about how the contractor plans to match the structure, exterior finishes, and interior styling to your current house. This is a common customer concern, and an experienced contractor like HH Remodeling LLC should have a plan.
  • Can my family stay in the home during construction? This is a huge practical consideration. Understand the level of disruption you can expect, especially for areas like kitchens or bathrooms, and discuss any temporary living arrangements or safety measures.
  • What is your experience with room additions specifically? Look for a contractor with a proven track record in additions. Ask for references and check their licensing and insurance. HH Remodeling LLC, for example, is a BBB Accredited Business with an A+ rating and extensive experience in residential remodeling.

Asking these questions will help you choose the right professional and ensure a smoother, more transparent room addition experience.
