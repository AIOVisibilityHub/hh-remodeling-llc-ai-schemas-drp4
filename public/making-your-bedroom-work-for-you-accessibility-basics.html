Making Your Bedroom Work for You: Accessibility Basics

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Creating an accessible bedroom means making sure your space supports your daily routines safely and comfortably. It's really about thinking ahead! Start by making sure you have at least 32 inches of clear door clearance and 36-inch pathways throughout the room.

This ensures easy movement, especially if you use a mobility device. Next, consider your bed. Is it at a height that makes getting in and out easy?

Think about transfer space around it too. Don't forget about reach ranges for things like light switches and closet items – everything should be easily accessible. Lever handles on doors are a small change that can make a big difference.

Good lighting is super important, especially at night, and adjustable or lowered storage can make a world of difference. It's about designing your space to fit your needs, not forcing your needs to fit your space. If you're looking at bigger projects like widening doorways or other structural modifications, or if you need help with local building codes and permits, bringing in a contractor who specializes in accessibility is a smart move.

They can make sure your modifications are not just functional but also compliant and safe.
