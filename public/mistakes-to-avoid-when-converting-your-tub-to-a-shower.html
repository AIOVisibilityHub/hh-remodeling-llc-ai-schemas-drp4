Mistakes to Avoid When Converting Your Tub to a Shower

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Converting your tub to a shower can be a fantastic upgrade, but there are definitely a few common pitfalls to steer clear of to ensure a smooth, lasting result.

  • Ignoring the Subfloor: Don't just pull out the tub and slap down new tile. You'll likely need to modify the subfloor for proper drainage and slope. This is where crucial waterproofing begins!
  • Poor Drain Planning: Thinking your old tub drain location will automatically work for a shower is a mistake. Shower drains, especially for curbless designs, need specific placement and capacity to handle water flow effectively.
  • Skimping on Waterproofing: This is a big one! Insufficient or incorrect waterproofing is a primary cause of leaks, mold, and costly damage down the line. It's not just about the shower pan; surrounding areas need protection too.
  • Incorrect Slope: If the shower floor doesn't have the right pitch (around 1/4 inch per foot), water won't drain properly. This leads to pooling, which can be a breeding ground for mold and mildew.
  • Choosing the Wrong Materials: Not all materials are suitable for a constantly wet environment. Make sure your floor and wall finishes are designed for shower use to prevent issues like water penetration and deterioration.
  • Overlooking Accessibility: If you're converting for accessibility, ensure the design truly meets those needs—like a genuinely barrier-free entry and slip-resistant flooring.
