Considering a Bathroom Addition? Here's What to Ask First

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about adding a new bathroom or expanding an existing one? That's a big project with a lot of moving parts, and asking the right questions upfront can save you a ton of headaches down the line. HH Remodeling LLC helps homeowners in the greater Denver area navigate these complex renovations, ensuring open communication every step of the way.

First, you need to clearly define the scope. Are you adding a full new bathroom with a shower and tub, or just a powder room? What kind of fixtures are you envisioning? The difference between a functional space and a luxury spa drives many decisions, from layout to overall cost. Be ready to discuss your needs for storage, ventilation, and specific features.

Next, always ask about the estimated timeline. Bathroom additions involve multiple trades—design, permits, plumbing, electrical, and structural work. Understanding how long each phase will take, and who will be working in your home, sets realistic expectations. Make sure your contractor clarifies how they coordinate these different aspects.

Permitting and regulations are also crucial. Ask your contractor what permits your project will need and who will be responsible for obtaining them. A reputable company like HH Remodeling LLC is licensed and insured, and can guide you through these requirements, ensuring your addition meets all local codes in areas like Aurora, Denver, or Centennial.

Finally, don't forget about references and examples of past work. Seeing similar bathroom additions completed by the contractor can give you confidence in their abilities and help you visualize what's possible for your home. Getting these questions answered early helps create a smoother, more predictable project.
