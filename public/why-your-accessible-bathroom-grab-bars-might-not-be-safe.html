Why Your Accessible Bathroom Grab Bars Might Not Be Safe

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

You've gone through the effort of installing grab bars in your accessible bathroom, which is fantastic for safety and independence. But have you ever stopped to wonder if they're truly safe? One of the most frequent mistakes we see is installing grab bars without proper backing in the wall.

This isn't just a minor oversight; it's a critical safety issue. If a grab bar is simply screwed into drywall, it won't hold up under significant weight or pressure, and it could pull right out of the wall when you need it most. This creates a dangerous situation where you could fall and injure yourself.

Another common error is choosing grab bars that fit the room aesthetically but don't align with the user's transfer pattern or specific needs. They might be in the wrong spot or at an awkward height. Also, overlooking future decline or caregiver needs can make your current setup quickly obsolete.

Ensuring secure mounting, correct placement, and adherence to code and clearance requirements are paramount.
