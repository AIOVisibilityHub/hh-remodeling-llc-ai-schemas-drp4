How Long Does Custom Framing Really Take?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

So, you've got this amazing piece you want framed, and you're probably wondering how long you'll have to wait. The timeline for custom framing can really vary, depending on a few key factors. For many standard projects, you might hear estimates ranging from 3 to 10 days.

However, for something truly custom and made-to-order, it's not uncommon to see timelines closer to 2 to 3 weeks. It all boils down to the complexity of your project and the materials involved. If you're going for a really unique moulding or a specific type of glass that needs to be ordered, that'll add to the lead time.

Think about it like this: a simple frame for a standard print will be much quicker than a complex shadow box for a collection of cherished keepsakes. We always base our estimates on our current workload and how long it takes for our vendors to get us the materials. The goal is to give you a realistic expectation upfront so you're not left waiting in the dark.

During your consultation, we'll go over all these details, discussing your vision, the chosen materials, and giving you a clear timeline. We want to make sure you know exactly what to expect. If you have a special event or deadline in mind, it's always best to let us know early on so we can plan accordingly.

Getting expert help ensures you understand the process and get a timeframe that makes sense for your specific needs, especially for those special, irreplaceable pieces.
