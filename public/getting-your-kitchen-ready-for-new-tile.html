Getting Your Kitchen Ready for New Tile

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Prepping for new kitchen tile might seem straightforward, but a little planning goes a long way to ensure a beautiful, lasting finish. Here’s a quick checklist to help you get ready. First, assess your existing subfloor or substrate.

Are there any loose, chipped, or uneven sections? These need to be addressed to provide a stable base for your new tile. If you’re unsure how to level it, that’s a good sign to consider professional help.

Next, clean the area thoroughly. Dust, grease, and debris can prevent adhesive from bonding properly, so make sure the surface is spotless. Also, think about waterproofing, especially in high-splash zones around sinks or dishwashers.

If your kitchen setup involves these areas, a waterproofing membrane might be crucial. You’ll also want to confirm the removal and disposal plan for your old tile and any debris. Finally, discuss the layout plan with your installer.
