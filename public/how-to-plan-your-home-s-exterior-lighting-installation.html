How to Plan Your Home's Exterior Lighting Installation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting new exterior lighting for your home is an exciting project! To make sure it goes smoothly and you get the results you're dreaming of, a good plan is key. Here's a step-by-step look at how we approach it, ensuring your outdoor spaces in Denver and surrounding areas truly shine.

Phase 1: Initial Consultation & Site Walk-Through. We start by walking your property with you. This is where we learn about your vision, what areas you want to illuminate, and any specific features you want to highlight. It's also our chance to assess the property's unique characteristics and electrical needs.

Phase 2: Layout & Design Planning. Next, we'll work with you to create a detailed layout and design plan. This includes proposing fixture types, placement, and control options. We can even offer a night-time demonstration in some cases so you can visualize the effect. This phase also accounts for future expansion.

Phase 3: Fixture and Control Selection. Once the design is set, we'll help you select the right fixtures and controls. We'll discuss options that fit your aesthetic, budget, and durability needs, ensuring they are all outdoor-rated and high-quality for our Colorado climate.

Phase 4: Professional Installation. Our team then takes care of the complete installation. This involves careful wiring, transformer sizing (if needed), proper fixture mounting, and ensuring all connections are waterproof and safe. We prioritize efficiency and minimal disruption to your home.

Phase 5: Post-Installation Support & Maintenance. After installation, we'll walk you through your new system and answer any questions. We can also discuss ongoing maintenance or future expansion possibilities. For any work involving your home’s main electrical panel, we always recommend a licensed electrician to ensure everything is up to code and safe. Ready to brighten your home?
