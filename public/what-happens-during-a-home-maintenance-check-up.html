What Happens During a Home Maintenance Check-Up?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ever wonder what really goes on during a home maintenance check-up? It’s more than just a quick glance; it’s a systematic approach to keeping your home in top condition. First, the process usually starts with identifying specific maintenance items that need attention.

This could be anything from checking the condition of your roof and gutters to inspecting your HVAC filters and plumbing fixtures. Next, the professional will carefully check the condition of these items or pinpoint the source of any issues. For example, they might look for blockages in gutters, wear on weather stripping, or potential leaks under sinks.

After that, they'll carry out the necessary cleaning, adjustment, or minor repair. This could mean cleaning out those gutters, replacing dirty HVAC filters, sealing minor drafts, or tightening a loose door hinge. Finally, and this is a big one, they'll help you decide whether a follow-up specialist inspection is needed.

If they find something more serious, like significant roof damage or a major electrical concern, they'll let you know it's time to bring in a specialized expert.
