Signs Your Door Frame Damage Needs a Pro

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Sometimes, a little dent or scratch on your door frame is no big deal, but other times, that damage signals something more serious that really needs a pro's touch. Here are a few key signs:

  • Rot or persistent moisture damage: If you see spongy wood or discoloration, it’s not just cosmetic; you’ve got a moisture problem that needs to be found and fixed, not just covered up.
  • Significant impact damage: A big crack or a visibly splintered frame means the structural integrity is compromised, affecting how the door hangs and closes.
  • Hidden fastener damage: If the door wobbles or feels loose in the frame, there might be issues with the fasteners you can't see, which could be a safety concern.
  • Frame misalignment: If your door doesn't close evenly, drags, or has huge gaps, the frame itself might be out of plumb or square, making proper operation impossible.
  • Full jamb or threshold replacement needed: If these critical components are beyond repair, it's a complex job requiring precise measurements and installation to ensure security and weather sealing.
