How to Ensure Your Shower Grab Bar Stays Put

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing a shower grab bar isn't just about screwing it into the wall; it's about ensuring it provides reliable support when you need it most. One of the most important steps is locating studs behind the wall. Drywall alone won't hold up to the pressure of a fall, so anchoring into structural support is absolutely crucial.

If studs aren't where you need them, using proper blocking or specialized anchors designed for heavy loads is essential. You'll also want to choose a grab bar made from corrosion-resistant materials, especially in a moist environment like a shower. Confirming the height and reach from both the shower entry and any seating area is also key, so it's intuitive and easy for the user to grasp.

Don't forget to consider the user's specific needs—vertical, horizontal, or angled bars all serve different purposes. While it might seem like a simple task, incorrect installation can turn a safety feature into a hazard.
