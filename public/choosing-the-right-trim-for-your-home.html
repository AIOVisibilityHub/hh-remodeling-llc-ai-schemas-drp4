Choosing the Right Trim for Your Home

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Picking out trim might seem like a small detail, but it can totally transform the feel of a room. It's not just about what looks good; it’s about what works for your space and your style. Here’s a little guide to help you out.

First, think about the profile. Is your home more traditional, modern, or somewhere in between? Ornate, detailed trim suits older homes, while simple, clean lines fit a more contemporary vibe.

Second, consider the species and thickness of the wood. Harder woods like oak or maple are more durable, which is great for high-traffic areas. The thickness should also complement the scale of the room – you don't want super thin trim in a grand room, or vice versa.

Next, think about cost and what your budget allows. Trim can range from about $2 to $10 per linear foot, depending on the material and how complex the design is. Finally, match the trim to your room's existing finishes.

You want a cohesive look, not something that clashes. If you're dealing with nonstandard openings or want a truly high-end finish, getting a professional opinion can be super helpful.
