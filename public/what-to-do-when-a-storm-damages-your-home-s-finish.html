What to Do When a Storm Damages Your Home’s Finish

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Dealing with storm damage to your home’s interior or exterior finish can be overwhelming. It's not just about aesthetics; it’s about protecting your home from further issues. Here’s a phased approach to getting things back to normal:

Phase 1: Initial Damage Assessment and Safety

First things first, ensure your home is safe. Once it is, conduct an initial assessment of the damage. Note any visible issues like water stains, peeling paint, or compromised surfaces. Don't touch anything structural without professional advice. This step is crucial for both your immediate safety and for documenting everything for insurance purposes.

Phase 2: Moisture Checks and Temporary Fixes

It's absolutely critical to ensure that any damaged surfaces are completely dry before you even think about painting. Painting over dampness is a recipe for disaster, leading to peeling, blistering, and potential mold growth. We use moisture meters to confirm dryness. If there are ongoing leaks, prioritize temporary repairs to stop further water intrusion. This might involve tarping roofs or sealing broken windows.

Phase 3: Cleanup and Substrate Repairs

Once everything is dry, it’s time to clean up debris and address the underlying damage. This could mean replacing rotten wood, repairing compromised drywall, or patching holes. Don't skip these foundational repairs; they’re essential for a lasting finish. HH Remodeling LLC excels at these repair-led prep tasks.

Phase 4: Priming, Sealing, and Finish Painting

After repairs, priming and sealing damaged areas are often necessary to prevent stains from bleeding through and to ensure uniform paint adhesion. Then comes the finish painting. Matching existing finishes can be tricky, so we take care to blend new work seamlessly with older sections.

Phase 5: Final Inspection and Touch-ups

Once the finish work is complete, do a thorough final inspection. Look for any missed damage, areas that need touch-ups, or spots where the finish doesn't quite match. Open communication with your contractor throughout this process is key for your satisfaction. When storm damage involves extensive repairs, recurring leaks, or structural concerns, it’s definitely time to bring in experienced professionals like HH Remodeling LLC. We can coordinate with other trades and ensure a comprehensive restoration.
