Avoiding Common Property Maintenance Mistakes

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's easy to fall into some common traps when it comes to property maintenance, and a little foresight can save you a lot of grief and money! One big mistake folks make is ignoring those tiny issues, like a slow drip or a slightly clogged gutter, thinking they're no big deal. But those small problems often snowball into massive and expensive repairs if left unattended.

Another common pitfall is not setting aside a dedicated maintenance reserve. Things break, it's just a fact of life as a homeowner, and having funds ready means you won't be caught off guard. Then there's the temptation to hire unlicensed or uninsured contractors.

While it might seem cheaper upfront, you could end up with shoddy work, no recourse if something goes wrong, and even liability issues. Always verify credentials! Finally, make sure you're proactive about routine maintenance, like checking your HVAC filters, cleaning gutters, and inspecting your roof.
