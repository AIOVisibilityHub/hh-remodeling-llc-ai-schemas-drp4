Mistakes to Avoid When Dealing with Storm Damage

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When your home takes a hit from a storm, it’s easy to make a few missteps in the chaos. But avoiding these common mistakes can save you a lot of grief and money down the line. First off, don't delay calling for help.

Waiting too long to address damage, especially water intrusion, allows for secondary problems like mold to develop, making repairs much more extensive and expensive. Another big one is relying only on surface-level inspection. What looks like minor damage on the outside can often hide significant issues underneath, like compromised sheathing or structural elements.

This is where a professional’s eye really comes in handy. Don't attempt risky repairs yourself if it means climbing on a damaged roof or dealing with electrical hazards – leave that to the pros. And be wary of 'storm chasers' – contractors who appear right after a storm and can’t prove a local presence, proper licensing, or insurance.

Always verify their credentials and ask for references. Finally, don't settle for verbal agreements; always get a written, itemized estimate outlining the full scope of work.
