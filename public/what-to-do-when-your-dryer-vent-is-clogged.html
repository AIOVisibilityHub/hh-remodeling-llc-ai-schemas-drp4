What to Do When Your Dryer Vent is Clogged

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A clogged dryer vent isn't just inefficient; it's a real fire hazard. You know how important it is to clean your lint screen after every single load, but that's only part of the battle. Lint can build up in the dryer vent tube itself, and that's where things get dangerous.

First, always make sure you're using fire-resistant venting material, not those flimsy plastic flex hoses. If you notice your clothes are taking forever to dry, or the dryer feels super hot to the touch, those are big red flags. You should also periodically check the vent tube for any kinks, crimps, or disconnections, especially where it attaches to the dryer and exits your home.

Keeping the outdoor lint trap clear is crucial too. If you suspect a serious clog or any damage, it's really best to stop using the dryer and call in a professional. HH Remodeling LLC can help inspect your venting and ensure it's up to par, keeping your home safer and your dryer running more efficiently.

Don't wait until it's too late; a clear vent is a safe vent.
