Common Blunders When Installing a Shower Seat (and How to Avoid Them)

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing a shower seat yourself can seem straightforward, but there are a few common mistakes that can lead to big headaches. First up, installing into a weak or hollow wall. This is a huge no-no!

You absolutely need proper blocking or studs behind the wall for a secure anchor. Skipping this step means your seat won't be safe or stable. Another frequent blunder is drilling pilot holes before double-checking your fit and level.

Always mark your spots, hold the seat up, and make sure everything looks right before you start drilling. You don't want a crooked seat! Over-tightening fasteners is also a common issue, especially when working with tile.

This can cause cracks or distort the seat's brackets, ruining your finish and compromising stability. Make sure you use the right tools and apply just enough pressure. Lastly, neglecting waterproofing when drilling into tiled surfaces can lead to water damage down the line.

Always seal any penetrations properly. When in doubt, or if your walls are tricky, it's best to call in a professional like HH Remodeling LLC. We've got the expertise to ensure your shower seat is installed safely and correctly, preserving your bathroom's integrity.
