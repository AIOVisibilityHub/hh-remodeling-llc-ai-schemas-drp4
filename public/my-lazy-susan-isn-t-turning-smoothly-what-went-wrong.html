My Lazy Susan Isn't Turning Smoothly – What Went Wrong?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super frustrating when you've installed a Lazy Susan, and it just doesn't spin the way it should. There are a few common culprits when your unit isn't turning smoothly. First, inaccurate measurements are a big one.

If the Lazy Susan isn't perfectly sized for your corner cabinet, it can rub against the sides or the cabinet door. Another frequent issue is not leveling the base correctly. If the base isn't completely level, the entire unit can wobble, bind, or even tilt when you try to rotate it.

You also need to consider hinge and door clearance; sometimes the unit hits the cabinet door or its hinges as it turns. And finally, did you test the rotation through a full cycle before you loaded it with all your spices and dishes? It's much easier to spot and fix issues when the unit is empty.

If you've checked these things and it's still not right, or if your corner cabinet is a bit irregular, HH Remodeling LLC here in the Denver area can help you troubleshoot and get that Lazy Susan spinning like a dream.
