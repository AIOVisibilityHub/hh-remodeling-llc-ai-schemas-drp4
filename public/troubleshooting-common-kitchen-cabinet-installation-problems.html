Troubleshooting Common Kitchen Cabinet Installation Problems

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing kitchen cabinets can sometimes throw a curveball or two, even for the pros! One common mistake folks make is skipping the crucial step of confirming where those wall studs are. If you don't hit a stud, your cabinets won't be as secure as they should be.

Another frequent misstep is not continuously checking for level and plumb throughout the entire installation. It's not a one-and-done check; things can shift, and you want that perfect alignment for your finished kitchen. Also, some people forget to remove cabinet doors and shelves before installation to lighten the load, which can make the process unnecessarily difficult and increase the risk of damage.

And here's a big one: not accounting for backsplash and countertop clearances. You need to make sure there's enough room for everything to fit together seamlessly once those elements are added. If you're encountering any of these issues, or if your walls and floors are seriously uneven, bringing in an experienced installer can save you a lot of headaches and ensure a beautiful, lasting result.

Don't hesitate to reach out for professional help if you're feeling overwhelmed.
