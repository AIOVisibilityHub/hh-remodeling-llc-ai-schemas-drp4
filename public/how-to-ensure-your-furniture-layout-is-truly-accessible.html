How to Ensure Your Furniture Layout is Truly Accessible

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Making sure your living room furniture layout is truly accessible goes beyond just making it look good; it's about ensuring everyone can move through and use the space comfortably and independently. Here at HH Remodeling LLC, serving Aurora and the wider Denver area, we believe accessible design should be seamless and thoughtful. If you're planning an accessible space, here's how to ensure your layout hits the mark.

First up, prioritize clear pathways. The golden rule for a straight accessible path is generally a minimum of 36 inches, but honestly, 42 inches or more offers much better comfort, especially if someone uses a mobility aid. Don't forget that turning areas need significantly more room than straight paths, so account for wide arcs.

Second, measure everything. And we mean everything! Know the exact footprint of your furniture, and compare it to the available space.

A piece that looks right in the showroom might be too bulky in your specific room. Third, consider doorway swings and transitions. Make sure doors can open fully without obstruction and that there are no sudden changes in floor level that could be a tripping hazard or barrier for a mobility device.

Finally, and this is key, think about the daily routine. Does the layout support how a person will actually live in the space? Can they easily reach light switches, open blinds, or access storage?

Don't just arrange furniture for visual appeal; arrange it for real-world functionality. If these considerations feel complex, especially with tight constraints or specific accessibility requirements, our team at HH Remodeling LLC can provide the expert guidance needed to avoid costly errors.
