Common Mistakes That Delay Bathroom Remodels

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A bathroom remodel can be a fantastic upgrade, but it’s easy to hit snags that push back your timeline. One major mistake people make is choosing their finishes like tiles or fixtures before confirming their lead times. You might fall in love with a custom vanity, only to find out it won't arrive for months, grinding your project to a halt!

Another common error is starting demolition before all necessary permits are approved. Depending on whether you're moving plumbing or electrical, you might need inspections, and jumping the gun can lead to costly re-work or fines. Don't underestimate how long inspections can take or the potential for ordering delays.

These small hiccups can really add up. To avoid these issues, pick a contractor who can manage code compliance, effectively coordinate all the different trades, and clearly explain what's included in each phase, from rough-in to final inspection.
