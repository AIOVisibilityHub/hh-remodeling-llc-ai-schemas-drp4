Avoiding Common Mistakes When Installing a New Bathtub

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting a new bathtub installed might seem straightforward, but there are definitely some pitfalls that can turn a simple upgrade into a major headache. We've seen it all, and these common mistakes often lead to costly fixes down the line. Avoiding them is key to a smooth, leak-free installation that you'll be happy with for years.

One frequent mistake is poor drain alignment. If the drain and overflow aren't perfectly aligned and sealed, you're looking at leaks and water damage – fast. It's not just about getting the pipes to meet; it's about making sure everything is snug, watertight, and set according to the manufacturer's specific instructions.

Another big one is an unlevel or unsupported tub. A tub that isn't perfectly level or properly supported can rock, creak, and even crack over time. This isn't just annoying; it can compromise the seals and lead to leaks.

Always ensure the tub is securely shimmed and fastened as recommended.

Then there's insufficient waterproofing. The area around your tub needs to be properly waterproofed, especially if you're installing tile or a surround. Skipping this crucial step or doing it improperly is an open invitation for mold, mildew, and structural damage behind your walls. You really need to follow those manufacturer guidelines for sealing and protecting the surrounding areas.

Finally, many folks forget about permits. If your installation involves new drain rough-in, drain relocation, new supply lines, or any structural work on the subfloor, you might need a permit. Failing to get the necessary permits can cause delays, fines, and even require you to tear out work and start over. If you're unsure about the local requirements in Aurora, Denver, or surrounding communities, it's always best to check with a professional. We're here to help you avoid these common missteps and ensure your bathtub installation is done right the first time.
