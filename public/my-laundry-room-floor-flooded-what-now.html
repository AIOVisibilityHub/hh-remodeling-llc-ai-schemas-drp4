My Laundry Room Floor Flooded – What Now?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A flooded laundry room can be a real headache, and it often leaves homeowners wondering what went wrong and how to fix it. The key to preventing future issues and fixing current ones usually comes down to making sure your flooring is truly equipped for the job. It's not just about a quick cleanup; it's about making sure your subfloor is sound and that water can't get to it again.

First, assess the damage and identify the leak's source. Once the immediate issue is handled, you'll want to focus on prevention. Make sure your chosen flooring is 100% waterproof. Options like porcelain tile or rigid-core waterproof vinyl (LVP/SPC) are ideal because they can withstand significant moisture. If you have a floor drain, ensure it’s clear and functional; tile should be sloped about 1/4 inch per foot towards it to guide water away. For LVP around a drain, remember to leave a perimeter gap instead of floating it directly over.

One common mistake is choosing merely “water-resistant” flooring instead of truly waterproof. Also, don't overlook the importance of detailing seams and perimeters to prevent water from seeping underneath. Consider adding a washer pan, upgrading to braided stainless steel hoses, or even installing an automatic shut-off valve for extra protection. When the subfloor condition is uncertain, or if you're dealing with a leak over a living space, that's definitely a time when a qualified professional can help ensure everything is correctly installed and protected.
