When to Bring in the Pros for Your Shower Seat Installation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Sure, a shower seat seems like a simple addition, but there are definitely times when calling in a professional like HH Remodeling LLC just makes sense. You should seriously consider it if your wall doesn't have obvious backing. Trying to install a seat into drywall or a hollow wall is a recipe for disaster; it needs to be anchored into studs or solid blocking, and if you're not sure where those are, a pro can help find them or add them.

Another big signal is if you're dealing with tile. Drilling into tile without the right tools and technique can lead to cracks, which means a much bigger repair bill than the cost of a professional installation. Also, if your shower is already waterproofed, drilling into it means you'll need to carefully preserve that waterproofing, which is a specialized skill.

Finally, if you're installing a shower seat to meet accessibility or building code requirements, like for an ADA-compliant bathroom, you really need someone who knows the rules to ensure it's done right and safely. Don't take chances with safety and structural integrity—it's worth it to get expert help.
