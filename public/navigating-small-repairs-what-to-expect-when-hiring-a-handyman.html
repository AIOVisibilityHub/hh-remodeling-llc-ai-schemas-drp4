Navigating Small Repairs: What to Expect When Hiring a Handyman

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you have a list of small repairs or odd jobs around the house, hiring a handyman can be a fantastic solution. But if you haven't done it before, you might wonder what the process looks like. Typically, it starts with an assessment of the problem – you explain what needs doing, and the handyman evaluates the scope.

This leads to defining the scope of work, so everyone is on the same page about what will be done. Next, it's about parts and materials. Sometimes you provide them, sometimes the handyman sources them; it's always good to clarify this upfront.

Once that's sorted, scheduling the work happens, usually based on urgency and parts availability rather than strict deadlines, though permits can still be needed for certain modifications. After the work is completed, there's usually a final walkthrough to make sure you're satisfied with the repair. It’s important to avoid common mistakes like having an unclear scope or underestimating parts and labor.
