What to Expect During Your Kitchen Addition: A Timeline

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about adding onto your kitchen? It's a big project, and knowing what to expect can really help manage stress. Generally, a kitchen addition can take anywhere from 4 to 15 months from start to finish, with the actual construction portion often lasting 10 to 16 weeks, or even 5 to 7 months for larger jobs.

This timeline includes everything from the initial design and survey work to getting all your permits and finally the actual build. Don't forget, there are also things like lead times for materials and builder availability that can push start dates back, so planning ahead is key! You'll kick things off with a brief and budget discussion, then move into surveys and drawings.

After that comes the crucial step of getting all your permits and approvals sorted. Once those are in hand, it's time for demolition and site setup, followed by the foundations and structural work. Then, the first and second fix trades (plumbing, electrical, etc.)

come in, leading to the exciting part: finishes! Finally, you'll have a handover and receive all your certificates. It's a journey, but with the right team coordinating everything from drawings to structural work, it can be a smooth one.
