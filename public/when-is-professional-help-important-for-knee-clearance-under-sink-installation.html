When is professional help important for knee clearance under sink installation?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Figuring out when to bring in a pro for knee clearance under sink installation can save you a lot of frustration and potential issues. If you're undertaking a bathroom remodel that involves significant plumbing changes, or if the sink is specifically part of an accessibility-sensitive design—whether it’s for a home modification or a commercial space—that’s a strong signal to get expert help. These types of projects often have specific clearance dimensions that need to be met, like the 30 inches wide by 17 to 25 inches deep knee/toe space with a minimum 27 inches of knee height.

Mistakes in these measurements can lead to compliance failures and costly rework, especially since inspectors will look at the installed condition. A professional team, like HH Remodeling LLC, can ensure that the plumbing is routed correctly to keep the space unobstructed and that the overall design supports seated use and proper faucet reach. Don't risk cutting corners when accessibility is key; it's better to get it right the first time.
