How to Ensure Your New Vanity Plumbing is Leak-Free

How to Ensure Your New Vanity Plumbing is Leak-Free

Read article

Nobody wants a leaky vanity, especially after all the effort of a bathroom remodel! Ensuring your new plumbing is completely leak-free is crucial, and it requires a methodical approach. First, always make sure you shut off the main water supply and relieve any pressure before disconnecting old lines.

When you're connecting the new supply lines and P-trap, ensure all connections are hand-tightened first, then gently snugged with a wrench—don't overtighten, or you risk stripping threads or cracking pipes. Pay close attention to the alignment of your drain and supply routing; everything should fit naturally without forcing. Once everything is connected, the most important step is a thorough leak test.

Turn the water back on slowly and let it run for several minutes, checking every single connection point with a dry paper towel. Run water in the sink for an extended period, fill it up, and drain it a few times to simulate real-world use. If you spot even a tiny drip, address it immediately.

If you're unsure about any part of this process, or if you encounter persistent leaks, it's definitely time to call in a professional plumber to make sure everything is sealed tight and working correctly.
