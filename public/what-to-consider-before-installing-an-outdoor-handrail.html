What to consider before installing an outdoor handrail?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Before you install an outdoor handrail, there are a few important things to think about to make sure it's safe and effective. First, inspect the layout of your stairs or ramp. You need to confirm safe and secure mounting points for the handrail; it's not just decorative, it's safety hardware!

Think about the graspable profile of the handrail itself—is it easy to grip for the people who will be using it? You want a comfortable and secure hold. Then, verify that the rail's placement works with the width of your steps, the layout of your landings, and the user's reach and balance needs.

You might need a handrail on one side, or both, depending on the situation and local building codes. These codes often specify placement, continuity, and dimensions, so it's smart to be aware of them. Choosing weather-resistant materials is also key, especially in Colorado's climate, to ensure the rail stands up to the elements and remains sturdy over time.

When in doubt, especially with masonry or concrete anchoring, a qualified professional can make sure your handrail is installed correctly and safely.
