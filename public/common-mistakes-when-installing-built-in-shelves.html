Common Mistakes When Installing Built-In Shelves

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing built-in shelving can significantly upgrade your space, but there are a few common pitfalls to steer clear of. One frequent mistake is not planning for outlet access. You don't want your beautiful new shelves to block essential power!

Another big one is relying solely on drywall anchors for heavy loads. Drywall anchors are fine for light decor, but for books or substantial items, you absolutely need to anchor to studs for real support. Failing to anchor tall units to studs is a safety hazard; these can tip over, which is a scary thought.

And finally, don't forget to leave service access for future repairs, especially if you're building around plumbing or electrical. Burying access can make future fixes a nightmare. If any of this feels overwhelming, or if you're unsure about structural changes or utility relocation, that's where a qualified professional comes in handy.
