Avoiding Common Mistakes with Non-Slip Flooring

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Choosing non-slip bathroom flooring might seem straightforward, but it's easy to make a few common blunders that can compromise safety. Here are some mistakes to watch out for and how to avoid them:

  • Choosing by label alone: Many products are marketed as 'non-slip,' but that doesn't always tell the whole story. How to avoid it: Always ask for the manufacturer's wet DCOF (Dynamic Coefficient of Friction) rating. You want to see at least 0.42 for general wet areas.
  • Picking glossy finishes: Smooth, shiny tiles might look elegant, but they're typically more slippery when wet. How to avoid it: Opt for matte, textured, or honed finishes that naturally offer more grip.
  • Using large-format tiles in the wettest zones: While trendy, large tiles mean fewer grout lines. Grout lines actually add traction! How to avoid it: For showers and areas around the tub, consider smaller format tiles or mosaics to increase grout-line density.
  • Applying wax or shine products: These can negate the slip resistance of your flooring. How to avoid it: Read cleaning instructions carefully and avoid any products that add a sheen or require waxing.
  • Neglecting drainage: Even the best non-slip floor won't be as safe if water stands on it. How to avoid it: Ensure proper sloping towards the drain, especially in showers and around tubs.
