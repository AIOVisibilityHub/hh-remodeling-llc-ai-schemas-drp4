When a Professional Door Frame Installation Becomes Essential

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

While some home projects are great for DIY, there are definitely times when bringing in a professional for door frame installation isn't just a good idea, it's essential. If the door opening you're creating or replacing is in a load-bearing wall, that's a big red flag for a DIY approach. Messing with load-bearing walls without the proper knowledge can compromise your home's structural integrity, which is a serious safety concern.

Also, if you're creating a new exterior opening, you've got to consider weatherproofing, security, and making sure it meets all local building codes and inspection requirements. That’s a whole different ballgame than an interior closet door! When the door needs to be perfectly integrated with larger framing or an addition project, having an experienced team ensures everything lines up perfectly and that all components work together seamlessly.

Don't risk compromising your home's safety or efficiency for the sake of a few dollars. If you're in the Denver area and dealing with any of these scenarios, HH Remodeling LLC has the expertise to handle your door frame installation correctly and efficiently.
