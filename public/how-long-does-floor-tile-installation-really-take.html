How Long Does Floor Tile Installation Really Take?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting new floor tiles isn't just a snap-your-fingers kind of job; there's actually a bit of a process involved that takes some time. It's not just about laying the tiles down. First, if you're replacing old flooring, that needs to be removed, and the subfloor often needs prepping and leveling.

This can add a significant amount of time, especially if there are any surprises underneath. Then, the actual tile setting takes place, making sure everything is perfectly spaced and aligned. After that, the grout goes in.

But here's the thing: you can't just walk all over it right away! There's a crucial drying and curing period for both the mortar and the grout. Depending on the size of your room and the type of tile, this can easily add several days to the project.

For example, a marble floor install often needs more careful sealing steps, both before and after grouting, which extends the overall timeline. So, when you're planning, remember to factor in not just the installation time, but also the essential drying and curing phases for a long-lasting, beautiful floor. Communication about the timeline is key with your installer.
