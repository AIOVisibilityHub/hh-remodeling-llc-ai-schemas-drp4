What to Do When Your Foreclosed Property Bathroom Has Hidden Damage

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's a common scenario: you're excited about refreshing a foreclosed property's bathroom, and then demolition reveals hidden issues like mold, outdated plumbing, or structural problems. Don't panic! The key here is to go into the project expecting the unexpected.

Always start with a detailed inspection and document everything with photos. This helps you define a clear scope of work from the get-go. If you uncover something new, pause and reassess.

You'll want to address any structural, plumbing, electrical, or water intrusion issues before you even think about new tiles or fixtures. Trying to cover up hidden damage is a recipe for disaster and will cost you more in the long run. Be sure to build a contingency into your timeline and budget, maybe 15-25% extra, to account for these surprises.

And definitely, if the issues are beyond a cosmetic fix, it’s time to call in a licensed bathroom professional. They can ensure everything is up to code and structurally sound, giving you peace of mind.
