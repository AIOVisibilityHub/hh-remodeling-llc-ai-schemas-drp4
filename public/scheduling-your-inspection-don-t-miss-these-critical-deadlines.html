Scheduling Your Inspection: Don't Miss These Critical Deadlines

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting your project's inspections scheduled efficiently is key to keeping things on track. But it's easy to miss crucial deadlines if you're not aware of how each jurisdiction operates. For example, some counties require you to submit next-work-day inspection requests before a specific time, like 5:00 p.

m. If you miss that cutoff, you're looking at a delay. It's not just about submitting the request, though.

You also need to confirm the actual inspection window to make sure someone is available on-site when the inspector arrives. Failing to have the required documentation ready or not clearly identifying who will be responsible for the onsite walkthrough are also frequent mistakes that can cause unnecessary reschedules. It's really about knowing the specific rules and processes for each Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) involved in your project.

When your project involves different jurisdictions or particularly intricate coordination with these authorities, professional help can be invaluable. A service like HH Remodeling LLC can manage these updates, communicate directly with homeowners and AHJs, and track permit statuses, ensuring that your inspections are scheduled and completed without a hitch right up to the final closeout.
