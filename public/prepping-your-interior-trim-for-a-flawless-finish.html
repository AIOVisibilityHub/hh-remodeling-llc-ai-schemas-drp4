Prepping Your Interior Trim for a Flawless Finish

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting your interior trim ready for paint is probably the most important part of the whole process – seriously, don't skip this! First up, you've gotta clean that trim. We're talking about removing all the dirt, grime, and any waxy buildup.

Just imagine trying to paint over that, it'd be a mess. Once it's clean, you'll want to lightly sand or at least 'scuff-sand' the surface. This creates tiny abrasions that the new paint can really grip onto.

After sanding, make sure to wipe away all that dust. A damp cloth usually does the trick. If your trim has seen better days, or if you're changing from a really dark color to a light one, a good primer can be your best friend.

It helps with adhesion and makes sure your final coats look smooth and even. Oh, and if you're painting just the trim and not the walls, don't forget to protect those adjacent walls with painter's tape! It'll save you from a lot of touch-ups later.

If you're ever unsure about the best way to prep, especially with older, more damaged trim, it might be time to call in the pros. We can help you get it just right.
