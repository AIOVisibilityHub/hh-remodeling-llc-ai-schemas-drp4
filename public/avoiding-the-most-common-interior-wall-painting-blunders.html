Avoiding the Most Common Interior Wall Painting Blunders

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Painting interior walls seems straightforward, but it’s easy to make mistakes that can impact the final look and longevity of your paint job. Avoiding these common blunders can save you time and frustration.

One frequent mistake is choosing paint from a brand name without specifying the exact product line. Not all paints are created equal, even within the same brand! Always confirm the specific product for the best results.

Another big one is skipping proper wall prep. We all want to get to the fun part, but failing to clean and patch walls means imperfections will show through your new paint. Take the time to do it right!

Not confirming whether primer is included in the scope can also lead to issues, especially with color changes or stain coverage. Primer is often crucial for good adhesion and an even finish.

Many folks also don't state which rooms, closets, trim, or ceilings are excluded from a quote. This can lead to misunderstandings and unexpected costs down the line. Get everything in writing!

Finally, rushing the process – not allowing proper drying time between coats or trying to finish too quickly – often results in an uneven or streaky finish. Patience is key for a truly professional look.
