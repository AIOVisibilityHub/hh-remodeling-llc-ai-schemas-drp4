When to call in a pro for bedroom handrails

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A bedroom handrail can be a lifesaver for balance and mobility, but a poorly installed one can be just as dangerous. Knowing when to wave the white flag and call in a professional is key. Here are the times you definitely want an expert on the job:

  • Uncertain Wall Construction: If you're not sure what your wall is made of behind the drywall or plaster, or if it feels a bit flimsy, a pro can determine the best way to secure the handrail without damaging your walls or, worse, having the rail pull out.
  • Weight-Bearing Support: If the handrail is truly meant for meaningful weight-bearing, like assisting someone with mobility challenges, its installation needs to be absolutely rock-solid. A professional ensures it can handle the load safely.
  • Fall Prevention is Paramount: For situations where fall prevention is the main goal, you simply cannot compromise on the reliability of the installation. A qualified installer will make sure it's firmly fixed and won't budge.
  • Complex or Unique Wall Materials: Some wall types, like old plaster or certain types of masonry, require specialized tools and techniques for proper anchoring. Don't risk cracking or damaging your walls – let an expert handle it.
  • Ensuring Code Compliance: If your installation needs to meet specific accessibility requirements or local building codes, a professional knows the ins and outs to ensure everything is up to snuff.
