A Simple Plan for Home Upkeep

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Keeping your home well-maintained might seem like a huge task, but it's totally manageable with a simple, consistent plan. Think of it like a routine check-up for your house! On a monthly basis, you'll want to test your smoke and carbon monoxide detectors and quickly check for any visible leaks under sinks or around appliances.

Every quarter, it's a good idea to clear out your gutters, which prevents water damage, and check your water heater for any sediment buildup. These quick checks can prevent major issues later on. Annually, consider a more thorough inspection of your home, re-caulk any areas that need it, and, importantly, have your HVAC system professionally serviced.

This keeps your heating and cooling running efficiently and catches potential problems before they become emergencies.
