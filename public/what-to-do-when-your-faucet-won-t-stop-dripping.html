What to Do When Your Faucet Won't Stop Dripping

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A dripping faucet might seem like a small nuisance, but it can waste a lot of water and money over time. Here's a quick guide on how to approach it. First, identify the root cause.

Is it a loose handle, a worn-out washer, or something more complex with the cartridge? Document the issue with photos, as this can be helpful if you end up needing professional help. Next, compare your repair options; sometimes a simple washer replacement is all it takes, but other times, a new faucet might be more cost-effective in the long run.

If you're comfortable tackling it yourself, make sure to turn off the water supply before you start. However, if the issue persists or seems beyond a simple fix, it’s smart to get written estimates from a few professionals. Trying to fix a complex plumbing issue without the right tools or experience can lead to bigger problems.
