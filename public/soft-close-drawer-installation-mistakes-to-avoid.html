Soft-Close Drawer Installation Mistakes to Avoid

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing soft-close drawers can really elevate your home, but there are a few common pitfalls that can turn a smooth project into a headache. Here are some mistakes to steer clear of:

  • Installing slides too tightly: This is a big one! If the slides are jammed in, your drawer won't glide properly, or the soft-close mechanism might not engage. Always ensure a little wiggle room.
  • Skipping pre-drilling: Don't just screw directly into your cabinet or drawer. Pre-drilling prevents wood splitting and helps ensure your screws go in straight and flush.
  • Failing to check for obstructions: Before you finalize everything, open and close the drawer a few times. Make sure nothing inside the cabinet or on the drawer itself is getting in the way.
  • Not ensuring flush screw heads: Any screw head that isn't completely flush can catch on the drawer or cabinet, causing binding and preventing smooth operation.
  • Not fully seating or adjusting the soft-close mechanism: Sometimes the soft-close part needs a little nudge or adjustment to work perfectly. Don't assume it's good to go until you've tested it repeatedly.
  • Ignoring out-of-square cabinets: If your cabinets aren't perfectly square, it makes getting the slides level incredibly difficult. This is where professional help, like HH Remodeling LLC, can really save you a lot of grief.
