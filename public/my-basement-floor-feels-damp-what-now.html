My Basement Floor Feels Damp – What Now?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Discovering a damp basement floor is definitely a red flag, and it's smart to address it before considering new flooring. Active moisture is the archenemy of most basement flooring, so figuring out the 'why' is step one. Here’s a quick guide on what to do when you find dampness.

First, assess the source. Is it a persistent dampness, or does it only appear after heavy rain? This can indicate anything from condensation due to humidity to more serious water intrusion through cracks in your foundation. Sometimes, poor exterior drainage or even a leaking pipe can be the culprit. Knowing the source is crucial for effective remediation.

Next, perform a moisture test. You can do a simple tape-down plastic sheet test for a few days to see if condensation forms underneath, indicating moisture rising from the slab. This helps confirm if the concrete itself is a source of moisture. However, for a truly accurate picture, professional moisture meters and calcium chloride tests provide more precise data on moisture levels.

Then, address the root cause. If it's condensation, improving ventilation and potentially adding a dehumidifier can help. If it's water intrusion, you'll need to look at exterior grading, sealing foundation cracks, or even interior drainage systems.

Don't just cover it up; that's a common mistake that leads to bigger problems later on, like mold or flooring failure.
