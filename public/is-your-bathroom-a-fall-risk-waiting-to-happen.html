Is Your Bathroom a Fall Risk Waiting to Happen?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

The bathroom is one of the riskiest rooms in any home for falls, and it's something many people don't fully realize until an incident happens. You might be wondering what really matters most to prevent falls in this space, and if basic changes are enough. Common issues include slippery floors, lack of support when getting in or out of the shower or off the toilet, and even poor lighting that makes it hard to navigate.

Prioritizing this room makes a lot of sense, as the combination of water and hard surfaces creates a particularly hazardous environment. Simple changes like using non-slip mats, ensuring good lighting, and removing clutter are a great start. However, if you or a loved one use a cane or walker, or if falls have become a recurring problem, more robust solutions are often needed.

This could mean installing sturdy grab bars around the toilet and in the shower, or even considering a tub-to-shower conversion for easier, safer access. When looking for modifications, always prioritize durability and ease of use. If you're unsure where to begin or if significant changes are needed, a professional assessment can help ensure all potential hazards are addressed effectively and safely.
