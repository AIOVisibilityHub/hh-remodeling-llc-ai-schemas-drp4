What to Consider for an Accessible Kitchen Layout

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about making your kitchen more accessible? It's a fantastic way to make your home more comfortable and functional for everyone. When you're planning these updates, you'll want to focus on a few key areas to ensure the layout really works.

First, consider reach ranges and sight lines – you want frequently used items to be easy to grab without stretching or bending too much. Then, think about traffic paths; clear floor space is crucial, especially if you're using a wheelchair. Don't forget storage zones, either; pull-out shelves or lowered cabinets can make a huge difference.

Lighting is another often-overlooked aspect that can greatly improve usability. Finally, review appliance placement to make sure everything is easily reachable and usable.
