How Long Does it Really Take to Install a Kitchen Backsplash?

So, you're wondering about the timeline for a kitchen backsplash? It's not usually an overnight job, and a lot of it comes down to cure times. Generally, a standard backsplash install can take anywhere from one to three days from start to finish. Day one might involve prep, layout, and setting the tiles. Then comes the waiting game.
