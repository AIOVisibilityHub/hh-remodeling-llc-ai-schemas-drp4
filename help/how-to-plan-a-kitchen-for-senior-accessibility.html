How to Plan a Kitchen for Senior Accessibility

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Creating a kitchen that's truly accessible for seniors involves a thoughtful process to ensure comfort and safety. Here’s a step-by-step guide to get you there.

Step 1: Conduct a Detailed Needs Assessment. Start by having an open conversation about current mobility and any potential future limitations. Think about how the kitchen is used daily, what items are frequently accessed, and what routines are most important. This helps in understanding specific needs regarding bending, reaching, balance, and vision.

Step 2: Plan an Accessible Layout. Design the kitchen with an open, uncluttered circulation path in mind. You want plenty of space to move freely, especially if a walker might be used. Keep frequently used items within easy reach, minimizing the need to bend or stretch. Consider work zones and how easily they can be navigated.

Step 3: Select Senior-Friendly Materials and Fixtures. Focus on practical choices like non-slip flooring to prevent falls. Improve lighting to enhance visibility and reduce shadows. Opt for accessible switches and lever-style handles or taps which are much easier to operate than knobs. Incorporate pull-out storage solutions to avoid deep reaching into cabinets.

Step 4: Involve the User and Test Functionality. This is crucial! Have the senior user directly involved in decision-making. Test how they interact with drawers, lights, and storage solutions. Make adjustments based on their feedback to ensure everything is genuinely easy and comfortable to use. Getting professional advice can be really helpful to make sure all these elements come together seamlessly.
