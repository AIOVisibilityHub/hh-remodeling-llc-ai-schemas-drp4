When Should You Call a Pro for Your Shower Tile Installation?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

While DIY projects can be fun, some home improvements are best left to the experts, especially when it comes to shower tile installation. There are definitely clear signs that indicate it's time to bring in a professional like HH Remodeling LLC. Skipping professional help when it's needed can lead to costly mistakes and headaches down the road.

Here are some signals that it's time to call a pro

  • Your walls aren't perfectly flat: If the shower walls aren't plumb and flat, achieving a professional-looking tile job is incredibly difficult. Professionals have the tools and expertise to properly prepare and flatten the substrate, ensuring a smooth, even surface for your tiles.
  • You're unsure about waterproofing: This is probably the biggest reason to call a professional. Waterproofing errors in a shower can lead to serious, hidden water damage, mold, and costly repairs. A pro knows the correct waterproofing systems, how to apply them meticulously, and how to seal all critical junctions, corners, and penetrations.
  • The layout seems complex: If your shower involves intricate patterns, multiple tile sizes, niches, or challenging angles, planning the layout can get complicated fast. Professionals can design a layout that minimizes awkward cuts and ensures a visually appealing finish.
  • You want to ensure longevity: Shower installations are sensitive to proper technique and material compatibility. Professionals understand which adhesives and grouts are best suited for wet areas and how to apply them for maximum durability. They also ensure adequate cure times, which is vital for preventing premature failure.
  • You simply want it done right the first time: If you value your time and want the peace of mind that comes with a perfectly installed, waterproof shower, a professional is your best bet.
