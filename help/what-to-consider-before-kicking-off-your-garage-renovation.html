What to Consider Before Kicking Off Your Garage Renovation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about giving your garage a serious facelift? That's awesome! Before you dive in, let's chat about a few key things.

First off, what do you really want to use this space for? Parking your car, a killer workshop, a home gym, or maybe just super organized storage? Your answer here is going to guide almost every decision you make, from the layout to the kind of flooring and even where you put electrical outlets.

It’s a big deal, so take some time to really nail this down. Next, take a good, honest look at your current garage. What’s working, what’s not, and what do you already have stored in there?

This inventory check helps you figure out exactly what kind of storage solutions you’ll need. Finally, let’s talk materials and finishes. Think about how durable you need things to be and what kind of look you’re going for.
