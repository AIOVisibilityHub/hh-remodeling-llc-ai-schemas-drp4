When to Hire an Expert for Lever Handle Upgrades

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing a new lever handle can be a quick DIY, but sometimes it's really best to call in a professional. So, when should you pick up the phone? Here are some key indicators:

  • New Drilling Required: If your new lever handle doesn't match the existing holes, and new drilling is needed, a pro ensures precision and avoids damaging your door.
  • Damaged Lock Areas: If the area around the old lock is damaged, warped, or split, we can repair it properly so the new handle has a solid, secure foundation.
  • Latch/Strike Alignment Issues: If the latch isn't catching cleanly or the door isn't closing smoothly, there's often an alignment issue with the strike plate. An expert can quickly diagnose and correct this, preventing rubbing or latch drag.
  • Fire-Rated or Security Hardware: For these specialized installations, precise fit and function are absolutely critical for safety and security. Improper installation can compromise their effectiveness.
  • Integration with Smart Home Systems: If you're going high-tech, having a professional handle the installation ensures seamless integration and functionality.

We have the experience to tackle these trickier situations, making sure your new lever handle isn't just installed, but installed perfectly for long-term smooth operation.
