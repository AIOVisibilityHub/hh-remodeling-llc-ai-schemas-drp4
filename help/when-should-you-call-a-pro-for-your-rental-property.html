When Should You Call a Pro for Your Rental Property?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Knowing when to tackle a repair yourself versus when to call in a professional for your rental property can save you time, money, and headaches. Here are some clear signs it's time to bring in the pros:

  • Plumbing Issues: If you're dealing with anything beyond a simple clogged drain, like a persistent leak, low water pressure, or a water heater problem, it's best to call a licensed plumber. Water damage is no joke!
  • Electrical Concerns: Sparks, flickering lights, or outlets that don't work? These are serious. Electrical work can be dangerous and should always be handled by a qualified electrician to ensure safety and prevent fire hazards.
  • HVAC Malfunctions: If the furnace isn't heating or the AC isn't cooling, especially during extreme weather, you need an HVAC technician. They can diagnose and repair complex systems efficiently.
  • Gas-Related Work: Any issues involving gas lines, appliances, or heating systems that use gas absolutely require a professional. This isn't a DIY job, ever, due to safety risks.
  • Tenant Access Issues: If a tenant is preventing necessary inspections or safety checks, and you've provided proper notice, a professional property manager or guidance might be needed to navigate the situation.
