How to Make Your Kitchen Work for You as You Get Older

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

As we get a little older, our priorities shift, and sometimes our homes need to shift with us. Your kitchen, often the busiest room in the house, should be a place of joy and ease, not a source of frustration. If you’re finding yourself reaching for things that are just a little too high, or struggling with tight corners, it might be time to think about a thoughtful remodel.

It's all about making smart changes now that will keep your kitchen comfortable and functional for many more years, allowing you to age in place gracefully and independently. HH Remodeling LLC specializes in these kinds of updates across the greater Denver area, including Englewood and Highlands Ranch, ensuring your kitchen truly supports your lifestyle.

Start by assessing your everyday activities. Where do you prep food? Where do you store your most-used items? Often, simple adjustments can make a significant difference. Consider installing pull-out drawers and pantry shelves to eliminate deep reaching, or repositioning frequently used items to waist height. We can also look at features like accessible sinks, perhaps a single-lever faucet that’s easier to operate, or even toe-kick drawers at the bottom of cabinets for extra storage that's easy to access. These small but mighty changes can improve workflow and reduce strain.

Another key area is flooring and pathways. Slippery floors can be a hazard, so opting for non-slip materials like matte tiles or certain engineered wood can boost safety. Also, ensure there's ample space to move around freely, especially if you anticipate needing a walker or wheelchair in the future. Removing unnecessary obstacles and widening doorways to at least 36 inches can be transformative. Think about countertop materials too; smooth, durable surfaces that are easy to clean are always a good choice, and a contrasting edge can be helpful for those with vision changes.

Don't forget about task lighting! As our eyes age, we often need more light to see clearly. Strategically placed under-cabinet lighting, brighter overhead fixtures, and even pendant lights over work areas can illuminate your space beautifully and functionally, making cooking and reading recipes much safer. It's about layering your lighting to eliminate shadows and provide clarity. We can help design a lighting plan that's both practical and aesthetically pleasing.
