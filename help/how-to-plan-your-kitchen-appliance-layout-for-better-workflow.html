How to Plan Your Kitchen Appliance Layout for Better Workflow

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Planning a kitchen appliance layout that actually works for you is all about strategy. You want to make sure your kitchen isn't just pretty, but super functional too. Here's a step-by-step approach we use to get it right:

Step 1: Gather Appliance Specs. Before you even think about where things go, get the exact model numbers and spec sheets for all your appliances. This tells you their precise dimensions and any special clearance requirements.

Step 2: Test the Workflow. Mentally walk through your cooking process. Where will the fridge be in relation to your prep area? Does the dishwasher door swing clear of traffic paths? You'll want to ensure good clearance around oven doors, fridge doors, and other appliances.

Step 3: Confirm Utility Routing. This is a big one. You need to know where your water lines, gas lines, and electrical outlets are, or where they can realistically be moved. This dictates where certain appliances can go. Make sure you have dedicated circuits for things that need them.

Step 4: Account for Storage & Counter Space. Think about where your pots and pans will live relative to the stove, and make sure there's enough counter space around your main work zones. You don't want to be chopping vegetables on the other side of the kitchen.

Step 5: Finalize Before Ordering. Lock down your layout before you order any cabinetry or appliances. Changes later on can be costly and delay your project. If you're moving utilities or integrating built-ins, that's definitely a point where bringing in a professional can save you a lot of headaches.
