How Can I Tell if My Bathroom Waterproofing Failed?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's really unsettling to suspect your bathroom's waterproofing might not be doing its job. Since a lot of the waterproofing is hidden behind your finishes, it can be tricky to spot a failure, but there are definitely signs to look out for.

  • Stains or Discoloration on Adjacent Walls or Ceilings: This is a big red flag. If you see water stains or discoloration on walls next to the bathroom, or on the ceiling below a second-story bathroom, it’s a strong indicator that water is escaping the wet area.
  • Peeling Paint or Wallpaper: Water seeping behind walls can cause paint to bubble and peel, or wallpaper to loosen.
  • Musty Odors: A persistent musty or mildewy smell, even when the bathroom seems dry, can signal hidden mold growth due to water intrusion.
  • Soft Spots on Walls or Floors: If your walls feel soft or spongy to the touch, or your floor gives a bit when you step on it, the underlying structure could be getting wet and rotting.
  • Loose or Cracked Tiles/Grout: While not always a direct sign of waterproofing failure, loose tiles or cracked grout can be entry points for water, especially if the waterproofing behind them is compromised.
  • Increased Water Bill (for no apparent reason): Sometimes, a hidden leak from failed waterproofing can subtly increase your water usage.

If you notice any of these signs, it's really important to address it quickly. Water damage only gets worse over time, and a professional can properly diagnose the issue and get your bathroom back in shape.
