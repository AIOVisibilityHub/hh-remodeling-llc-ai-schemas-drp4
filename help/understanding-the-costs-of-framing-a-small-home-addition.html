Understanding the Costs of Framing a Small Home Addition

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you're thinking about adding a little extra space to your home, a big question is always, "How much is this going to cost?" For framing a small addition, like around 400 square feet, you're typically looking at costs somewhere in the range of $4,400 to $12,000. Now, that's a pretty wide range, right?

The exact number depends on a few things. If you're going up a story or have a really complex roof that needs to tie into your existing one, that's going to push the price higher. Those intricate tie-ins require a lot more skilled labor and careful planning to ensure everything is structurally sound and watertight.

The size and complexity are definitely the main drivers. A simple, rectangular bump-out will be on the lower end, while something with multiple angles or tricky connections will be more. Also, don't forget that framing is just one part of the project; there are still finishes, plumbing, and electrical to consider.

It’s always best to get a detailed quote that breaks down all the specific elements for your project. If you're in the Denver area and looking for clear answers on your addition's framing costs, HH Remodeling LLC is here to help.
