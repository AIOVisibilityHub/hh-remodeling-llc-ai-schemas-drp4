Do Your Cabinets Need to Be Replaced Before New Countertops?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's a really common question: do my cabinets need to be perfect before I get new countertops? The short answer is, not always, but their condition is definitely a big factor in a successful countertop replacement. When we come in for a countertop replacement, one of the first things we do after removing the old surface is check your base cabinets.

We're looking to make sure they're strong, stable, and level. If your cabinets are wobbly, have significant water damage, or are severely out of level, they might need reinforcement or even full replacement before a new countertop goes in. Installing a heavy new countertop on an unstable base is just asking for problems down the road, like cracking or uneven surfaces.

However, if your cabinets are structurally sound but just look a little tired, sometimes a fresh coat of paint or new hardware can give them a new lease on life without needing a full swap-out. This is especially true for projects that involve a new sink or plumbing, as a strong base prevents future issues. If you're unsure about the condition of your cabinets, that's where an expert like HH Remodeling can step in.

We can assess your existing setup and advise whether repair, reinforcement, or replacement is the best path forward for your Denver-area home, ensuring your new countertops have the solid foundation they need.
