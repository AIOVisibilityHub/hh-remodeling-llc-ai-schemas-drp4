Avoiding Common Living Room Trip Hazard Mistakes

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Nobody wants a trip or a fall in their own living room, but sometimes we make little mistakes that can create big hazards. Let's talk about some common blunders and how you can avoid them. First off, leaving rugs unsecured is a classic.

That small rug by the sofa might look great, but if it slides, it's a problem. Fact: Always use non-slip backing or tape to keep rugs firmly in place. Another frequent mistake is letting cords become an obstacle course.

Running cables across walkways is just asking for trouble. Fact: Route all cords along walls or baseboards, or use cable management solutions to keep them tidy and out of the way. Using low or unstable furniture in high-traffic paths is another one.

That cute little stool might seem harmless, but if someone leans on it or bumps into it, it could tip. Fact: Ensure all furniture in walkways is stable and can support incidental contact. Finally, don't assume hazards are always obvious.

Anything below eye level, especially in dim light, can be missed. Fact: Do regular walkthroughs of your living room, even in low light, to spot potential hazards.
