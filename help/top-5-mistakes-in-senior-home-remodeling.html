Top 5 Mistakes in Senior Home Remodeling

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Making your home senior-friendly is a fantastic goal, but it's easy to overlook crucial details. Many folks wonder how to avoid common pitfalls that can make a remodel less effective or even costly to fix later. Let's look at some common mistakes and how to steer clear of them.

  • Focusing on just one room: It's tempting to tackle only the bathroom, but ignoring other areas like the kitchen or entryways can create new barriers. How to avoid it: Do a whole-home assessment to identify all potential areas for improvement, even if you only act on a few initially.
  • Ignoring future needs: What works today might not work in five or ten years. How to avoid it: Think about potential mobility changes – could a walker or wheelchair be needed? Plan for things like wider doorways and enough turning space.
  • Underestimating access details: Small things like lever hardware on doors or the type of flooring can make a huge difference. How to avoid it: Consider all touchpoints and surfaces. Non-slip flooring and reachable controls are often overlooked but vital.
  • Not checking seated reach ranges: What's accessible standing up might not be from a seated position. How to avoid it: If you or a loved one might use a wheelchair, test reach heights for cabinets, light switches, and outlets from a seated position.
  • Delaying structural changes: Widening doorways or changing a bathroom's layout is much harder and more expensive to do later. How to avoid it: If you're planning a major remodel, integrate these structural changes early on.
