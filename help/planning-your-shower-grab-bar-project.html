Planning Your Shower Grab Bar Project

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting ready to install a shower grab bar? A little planning goes a long way to ensure it's safe, secure, and perfectly suited for your needs. Here's a quick checklist to help you get started:

  • Assess User Needs: Who will be using the grab bar, and what specific movements do they need assistance with? This will dictate the best placement (vertical for entering/exiting, horizontal for standing, or angled for combined support).
  • Locate Wall Studs: This is critical! Grab bars must be anchored into studs or proper blocking behind the wall, not just drywall. A stud finder is your best friend here. If studs aren't in ideal spots, you might need to open the wall for backing.
  • Measure for Placement: Confirm the ideal height and reach from both the shower entry point and any seating area. The bar should be easy to grasp without straining.
  • Choose the Right Bar: Select a corrosion-resistant grab bar with appropriate length, grip texture, and weight rating. Consider how it will look with your existing bathroom fixtures.
  • Check for Obstructions: Ensure no pipes, wires, or other elements are behind the wall where you plan to drill. This is especially important in tiled showers where damage can be costly.
