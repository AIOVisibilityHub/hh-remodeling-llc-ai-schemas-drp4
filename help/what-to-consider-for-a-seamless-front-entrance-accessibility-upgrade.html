What to Consider for a Seamless Front Entrance Accessibility Upgrade

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Making your front entrance truly accessible involves a bit more than just adding a ramp. To make sure it's seamless, you'll want to think through a few key things. First, really assess your current entrance from the ground up to your door – how much of a vertical rise is there?

And how much landing space do you have available? These measurements are crucial for selecting the right components. Then, consider your door swing; you need to ensure any upgrade, whether it’s a ramp or a threshold modification, won't block the door from opening or closing properly.

Also, think about the materials – will they stand up to the weather? Durability for indoor/outdoor exposure is key, as is the hardware, which needs to be suitable for the mobility device it'll support. You don't want a solution that needs constant maintenance because of shifting or cracking!

And don't forget regulatory considerations like the 1:12 ramp slope, minimum clear widths, and limits on threshold changes. If your project involves structural elements, exterior drainage, or needs to meet specific accessibility requirements, a professional can help ensure everything is done right the first time.
