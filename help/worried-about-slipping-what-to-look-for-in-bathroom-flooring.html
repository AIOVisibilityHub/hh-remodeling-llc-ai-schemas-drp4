Worried About Slipping? What to Look for in Bathroom Flooring

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's a common concern: nobody wants a slippery bathroom floor. When you're thinking about new flooring, the key is to understand what truly provides traction in a wet environment. You'll want to dig a bit deeper than just what the label says. Look for products that have a verified wet DCOF rating of at least 0.42. This isn't just a random number; it's a standard that indicates a floor's dynamic coefficient of friction when wet, which is pretty important for preventing slips.

Another big tip is to lean towards matte or textured finishes. Those beautiful, glossy tiles might look sleek, but they often lack the grip you need when things get wet. Textured porcelain is usually a fantastic choice because it's durable and offers excellent wet traction. Also, consider smaller-format tiles or mosaics, especially in areas like around the tub or shower, because the extra grout lines can add even more grip.

Don't forget about maintenance, either. Using waxes or polishes can actually reduce the slip resistance of your floor, so make sure your cleaning routine won't accidentally make things more hazardous.
