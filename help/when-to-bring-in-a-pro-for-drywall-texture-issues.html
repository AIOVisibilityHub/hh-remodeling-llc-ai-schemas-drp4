When to Bring in a Pro for Drywall Texture Issues

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Drywall texture repairs can be a DIY project for small dings, but sometimes, you really do need a professional touch to get it right. Here are some signs it's time to call in the experts.

  • The repair is large or highly visible: If you're dealing with a big patch in a main living area, like your living room or dining room, or a highly trafficked hallway, a professional can ensure it blends perfectly. You don't want a noticeable patch staring at you every day!
  • It's on a ceiling: Ceilings are tough because gravity isn't your friend, and getting an even texture overhead is a challenge for even experienced DIYers. Plus, those ceiling textures often have unique patterns that are hard to replicate.
  • The texture has to blend seamlessly: Some textures, like popcorn or certain hand-applied finishes, are incredibly hard to match perfectly. If you want it to look like nothing ever happened, a pro has the tools and techniques to achieve that seamless look.
  • You've tried and failed (multiple times): Hey, it happens! If you've given it a shot and the patch is still obvious, or worse, you've made it bigger, that's a good indicator to bring in someone who does this all the time.
