When to Upgrade to Slip-Resistant Flooring

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Wondering if it's time to swap out your current floors for something with a bit more grip? There are a few clear signals that suggest an upgrade to slip-resistant flooring is a smart move. First off, if you've had a few near-misses or actual slips, especially in areas like the kitchen, bathroom, or entryway, that's a big red flag.

Old or worn-out flooring can become slick over time, losing its original texture and making it more dangerous. Another key indicator is if you have young kids or elderly family members living with you. Their balance might not be as stable, and a fall can have more serious consequences.

Also, if your current flooring just doesn't handle moisture well – think puddles from the shower or drips near the sink – slip-resistant options are designed to tackle that head-on. If you're doing a general remodel of a wet area like a bathroom or kitchen, it's the perfect opportunity to integrate safer flooring. Don't wait for an accident to happen; recognizing these signs early can save you a lot of trouble down the line.
