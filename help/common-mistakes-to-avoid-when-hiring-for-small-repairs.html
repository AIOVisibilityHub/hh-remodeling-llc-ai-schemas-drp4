Common Mistakes to Avoid When Hiring for Small Repairs

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you've got small repairs piling up, it's easy to just want them fixed fast. But hold on a sec! There are a few common blunders folks make when hiring for these tasks.

First, don't just assume every 'small' repair is handyman-appropriate. While many jobs like drywall fixes, minor electrical, or plumbing tasks are perfect, extensive system work often needs a licensed pro. Double-check the scope!

Another big one is not clarifying what materials are included. Does the estimate cover new parts, paint, or anything else needed? Make sure you know upfront so there are no surprises.

And here's a crucial one: always confirm whether the repair requires a licensed specialist for things like major electrical overhauls or complex plumbing issues. If it goes beyond basic repair, especially if it affects safety systems, you absolutely need to bring in a licensed expert. Don't be shy about asking if they can coordinate with licensed trades if the job requires it.
