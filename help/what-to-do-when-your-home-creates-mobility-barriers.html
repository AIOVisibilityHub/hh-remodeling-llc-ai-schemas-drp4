What to Do When Your Home Creates Mobility Barriers

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Finding yourself navigating mobility challenges at home can be frustrating, but with the right approach, you can transform your space into one that truly supports your needs. It all starts with a thorough assessment of your current and future mobility. Think about how you move now and how that might change down the road.

Next, take a walk through your home, specifically looking at entryways, doors, flooring, lighting, switches, and any safety devices you have. These are often the first places where barriers pop up. Once you've identified those tricky spots, you can start thinking about specific modifications.

Common changes include adding ramps for easy entry, widening doorways for better maneuverability, improving lighting for visibility, installing grab bars where you need extra support, opting for non-slip flooring, and even considering stair lifts if stairs are a major hurdle. Don't feel like you have to tackle everything at once, but having a clear plan is key. If you're feeling overwhelmed, or if the modifications involve structural changes, it's a good idea to chat with a professional who specializes in accessible home modifications.

They can help you create a safe and functional space that truly makes your house feel more like a home.
