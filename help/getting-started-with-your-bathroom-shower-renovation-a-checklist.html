Getting Started with Your Bathroom Shower Renovation: A Checklist

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ready to dive into a bathroom shower renovation? Here's a quick checklist to help you get organized. First, measure your space accurately.

You’ll want to know the dimensions for your shower, including height, width, and depth. This helps with choosing the right shower pan, enclosure, and fixtures. Next, think about your design preferences.

Do you want tile, a pre-fab surround, a specific showerhead style, or a built-in bench? This also includes considering drain location and shower-head height. Third, assess your current plumbing.

Are you planning to move any plumbing lines? This is a big one because it can significantly impact cost and complexity. You’ll also want to decide if you're keeping your old tub or removing it entirely.

Finally, gather bids from licensed and insured contractors. Make sure they provide a clear scope of work that includes demolition, plumbing changes, material costs, and cleanup. Don’t forget to ask about warranties!

A professional contractor, like HH Remodeling LLC, can guide you through these decisions and make sure everything is handled correctly, especially if permits are needed or if you want a custom, leak-free shower.
