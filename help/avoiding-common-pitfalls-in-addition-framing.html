Avoiding Common Pitfalls in Addition Framing

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Starting a small addition can be exciting, but it’s super easy to trip up if you’re not careful. One of the biggest headaches we see is poor layout and not getting things perfectly square from the get-go. If your foundation isn't true, everything built on it will be off, and that's a nightmare to fix later!

Another biggie is underestimating how much your new roof tie-in will affect the existing structure. It's not just slapping new wood on; you've got to consider load paths and how water will drain. People also sometimes forget to factor in how complex their existing roofline is when planning for an addition.

Changes are bound to happen on any project, but weak communication can derail everything. Make sure you and your contractor are on the same page constantly. Lastly, don’t just hire the cheapest option!

Always check their recent, similar projects, verify their insurance, and get a clear understanding of their warranty terms. It really pays to do your homework upfront to avoid major headaches and extra costs down the line.
