Mistakes to Avoid When Refinishing Your Log Home

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Refinishing your log home is a big job, and it’s easy to make some common mistakes that can really impact the final result and the longevity of your logs. One of the biggest no-nos is trying to refinish over a dirty or failing surface. If you don't properly clean and prepare the logs, the new finish won't adhere well and you'll be back to square one in no time.

Another frequent error is ignoring the end-grain and checks (those natural cracks in the logs). These areas are like sponges for water, so they need extra attention and protection. Using incompatible coatings is another pitfall; you always want to make sure the new product works well with any existing finishes.

And please, don't delay repairs! Waiting until logs are already damaged by rot or insects can turn a simple refinishing project into a much more complex and expensive structural repair. We’ve seen it all, and it's always better to be proactive.

If you’re unsure about what kind of finish to use or how to tackle existing damage, bringing in a specialist can save you a lot of headaches and ensure your log home stays protected and beautiful for years.
