What to Do When Water Damage Strikes Your Home

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Discovering water damage in your home can be pretty overwhelming, right? The first thing to do is stop the source of the damage immediately. Whether it's a leaky pipe or an overflowing toilet, cutting off the water supply is crucial.

Next, take clear photos and notes of everything. This documentation is super important for understanding the extent of the damage and for any potential insurance claims down the line. After that, you need to assess whether it's something you can handle yourself or if you need to call in the pros.

For big issues or anything that affects your home's structure or utilities, a professional is a must. We're talking about things like structural damage or electrical issues—those are definitely not DIY projects. Once you've got a handle on the situation, it's time to open a work order, order any necessary parts, and schedule the restoration work.

Think about material availability and when technicians can access your home to get the job done efficiently. Don't delay addressing active damage; it'll only get worse! If you're not sure where to start, give HH Remodeling LLC a call.
