Common Mistakes to Avoid When Installing Interior Trim

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing interior trim can seem straightforward, but there are a few common pitfalls that can derail your project and leave you with less-than-perfect results. One big mistake is assuming trim is just included in a general painting or finishing quote. Always clarify this, because trim work requires specific skills and attention, and it might be a separate line item.

Another common issue is misaligned joints, visible gaps, or uneven surfaces. These often happen from inaccurate measurements or rushed cuts. It's crucial to take your time and make test cuts to ensure everything lines up perfectly before the final installation.

Don't rely on caulk to hide poor cuts – caulk is for tiny gaps, not major errors! Also, thinking that DIY is always the best route for complex trim designs or an entire house can be a costly mistake. While simple trim might be doable, intricate profiles or stain-grade materials demand precision that's hard to achieve without professional experience.

Finally, not verifying if the person doing the work has experience with similar projects or how they handle uneven walls can lead to disappointment. Always ask to see examples of their previous work and discuss their process. Avoiding these mistakes will lead to a much smoother project and a beautiful, lasting finish.
