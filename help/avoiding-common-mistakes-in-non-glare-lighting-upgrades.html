Avoiding Common Mistakes in Non-Glare Lighting Upgrades

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Upgrading your home's lighting to reduce glare can significantly improve comfort and aesthetics, but it's easy to make missteps that leave you frustrated. Here are some common mistakes and how to avoid them:

  • Choosing fixtures by appearance alone: It's tempting to pick lights solely because they look good, but aesthetics don't always equate to glare reduction. Always prioritize fixtures with effective optics and shielding designed to minimize glare, rather than just style.
  • Underestimating the need for a detailed plan: Simply buying new bulbs or swapping out a few fixtures without a comprehensive plan is a recipe for continued glare. A professional will evaluate your space and create a layout that balances brightness, safety, and coverage.
  • Ignoring maintenance and warranty terms: Before committing to an upgrade, especially for outdoor lighting, make sure you understand the long-term maintenance requirements and what the warranty covers. This prevents unexpected costs and ensures longevity.
  • Not considering fixture optics and shielding: These are crucial for glare control. Look for features like diffusers, louvers, or recessed light sources that direct light effectively without creating harsh spots.
  • Hiring without verifying experience and qualifications: A glare-free lighting upgrade isn't just about electrical work; it's about specialized knowledge of lighting design. Always confirm your contractor has a proven track record in glare reduction and can provide a clear plan and estimate.
