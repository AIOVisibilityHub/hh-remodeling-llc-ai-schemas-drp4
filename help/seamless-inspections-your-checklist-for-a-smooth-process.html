Seamless Inspections: Your Checklist for a Smooth Process

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Making sure your project's inspections go smoothly can save you a lot of time and hassle. Here's a simple checklist to help you prepare and avoid common hiccups:

  • Confirm the AHJ's Scheduling Method: Every Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) can have a slightly different process. Double-check if you need to call, use an online portal, or submit a form. Missing a deadline, like a 5:00 p.m. cutoff for next-day inspections, can push your project back.
  • Verify the Inspection Window: Once scheduled, confirm the exact time frame the inspector is expected. This helps ensure someone is available on-site to meet them, preventing a missed visit and rescheduling.
  • Prepare Required Documentation: Have all necessary plans, permits, and supporting documents readily accessible for the inspector. This might include approved drawings or specific material specifications.
  • Ensure Site Readiness: Make sure the area to be inspected is clear, accessible, and properly lit. If the inspector needs to see specific components, ensure they aren't obstructed.
  • Designate an On-Site Contact: Clearly identify who will be responsible for meeting the inspector and answering any questions. This person should be familiar with the project details and able to walk through the completed work.
  • Confirm Closeout Procedures: Understand how the signed closeout is delivered and filed. Knowing this in advance ensures the final step is handled correctly. If you're tackling a complex project or one that spans multiple jurisdictions, coordinating all these elements can become quite involved.
