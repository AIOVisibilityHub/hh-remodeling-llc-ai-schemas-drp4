What to Consider When Adding Lighting to Your Closet

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about upgrading your closet lighting? It's more than just screwing in a bulb! First, really look at your closet layout.

Where are the shadows? Where do you need the most light – on the hanging clothes, the shelves, or both? Next, fixture choice is super important.

You'll want fully enclosed, closet-rated fixtures that provide good, even light. Don't just grab any old light; bare bulbs are a definite no-no in closets due to safety regulations. You also need to think about power.

Are you adding new wiring or just replacing an existing fixture? The power source and how you route the wiring are critical for safety and code compliance. Finally, consider your switch location and how it makes sense for using the closet.

It's all about getting even coverage and making sure you can actually see everything you own! If you're unsure about any of these steps, especially when it comes to wiring or compliance, it's always smart to consult with pros like HH Remodeling LLC.
