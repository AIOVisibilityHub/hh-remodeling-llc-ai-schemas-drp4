What's the Ideal Width for Living Room Walkways?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Figuring out the right amount of space for pathways in your living room can be tricky, and it's one of the questions we get asked a lot here at HH Remodeling LLC in Aurora. You want your room to be functional and comfortable, not a maze! The ideal width really depends on the path's purpose, but there are some excellent rules of thumb to make sure your living room flows beautifully.

For your main walkway, the one everyone uses to get from one side of the room to the other or to an adjacent room, you're generally aiming for about 36 inches. This provides a comfortable passage for most people and prevents that 'squeezed' feeling. For secondary paths, like the route to a specific armchair, a window, or perhaps a bookshelf, you can usually get away with a slightly narrower passage, around 30 inches.

Then there's the space around your coffee table; you want it to be close enough to be useful but far enough to let you pass. A good target here is 14 to 18 inches between the edge of your sofa and the coffee table. Always check clearances around door swings too – you don't want a perfectly placed piece of furniture to suddenly become an obstacle when someone opens a door.
