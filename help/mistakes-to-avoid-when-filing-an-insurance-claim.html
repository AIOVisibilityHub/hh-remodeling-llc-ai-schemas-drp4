Mistakes to Avoid When Filing an Insurance Claim

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Navigating an insurance claim for interior restoration after storm damage can be tricky, and it's easy to make a few common missteps that can complicate things. Here are some key mistakes to steer clear of:

Don't Sign Contracts Immediately. It's tempting to jump on the first offer, but avoid authorizing full replacement work before your insurance company has had a chance to inspect and agree on the scope of damage. Focus on temporary mitigation first.

Don't Skip the Documentation. Failing to thoroughly document all damage with photos and videos before any cleanup or permanent repairs is a big one. This visual evidence is crucial for your claim.

Don't Ignore Your Policy's Timeline. Many policies have deadlines for reporting damage. Don't delay; contact your insurance company as soon as it's safe to do so, usually within 24-48 hours.

Don't Toss Receipts. Keep meticulous records of any expenses related to the damage, even for temporary repairs. These can often be reimbursed.

Don't Assume Your Contractor Knows Insurance. Make sure your chosen contractor has experience working with insurance companies and can provide estimates in a claims-friendly format, like line-item pricing. We're well-versed in working with adjusters to make the process smoother for you.
