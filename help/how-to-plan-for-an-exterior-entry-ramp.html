How to Plan for an Exterior Entry Ramp

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Planning for an exterior entry ramp can seem a bit daunting, but breaking it down makes it much easier. You'll want to start by figuring out which entrance works best for your needs. Think about the space outside: Is there enough room?

Are there any obstacles or changes in ground level that might affect the design? These are common questions that come up. Next, it's all about the site assessment.

You need to measure the 'rise' – that's the height from the ground to your door's threshold – and then confirm the 'run,' which is how much length you have available. Also, make sure the landing area is firm, level, and drains well enough to support the ramp system. Don't forget about permits!

Local building codes and utility checks are super important before you even think about digging. A professional can help ensure your ramp meets all requirements, including ADA standards, which often specify a 1:12 slope ratio. This means for every inch of rise, you need 12 inches of run.
