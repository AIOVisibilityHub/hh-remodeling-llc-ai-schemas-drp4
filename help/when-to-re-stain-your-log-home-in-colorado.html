When to re-stain your log home in Colorado

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super important to keep an eye on your log home's stain, especially here in Colorado where the elements can be tough. Folks often ask how often they should re-stain, and honestly, it varies a bit. Generally, you'll need to re-stain about every 3 years, but some stains and exposures might let you go 5-7 years.

A good rule of thumb is to check if water still beads on the surface. If it soaks in, that's a clear sign you're due for a refresh. Don't wait too long after you notice the water isn't beading anymore!

Regular cleaning, at least once a year, helps a lot too, and it's a great time to inspect for any early signs of trouble. Remember, applying a quality stain specifically designed for log homes is key; generic exterior coatings just won't cut it. If you're unsure whether your existing finish can be re-coated or needs stripping, or if you spot any rot, it's a good idea to reach out to a professional.
