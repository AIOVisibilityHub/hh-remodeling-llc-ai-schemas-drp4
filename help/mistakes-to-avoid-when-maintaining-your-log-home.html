Mistakes to Avoid When Maintaining Your Log Home

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Maintaining a log home is a bit different from a regular stick-built house, and there are some common missteps folks make. Here are a few to steer clear of:

  • Relying on clear coats alone: They just don't offer enough protection from the elements.
  • Waiting too long after water stops beading: That's your signal to re-stain. Don't put it off!
  • Skipping inspections of high-exposure walls: Your south and west-facing walls take the most abuse, so give 'em extra attention.
  • Not sealing log joints with chink or caulk: Open joints are an open invitation for water damage.
  • Using generic exterior coatings: Log homes need special stains that are designed to let the wood breathe. Using the wrong product can cause big problems down the road.

Catching these things early can save you a ton of hassle and money. If you're ever in doubt about the condition of your logs, or if you've got some serious peeling or rot, it's always a smart move to bring in a professional. We can assess what's going on and help you get things back on track.
