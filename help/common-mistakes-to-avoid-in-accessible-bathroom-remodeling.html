Common Mistakes to Avoid in Accessible Bathroom Remodeling

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's easy to get excited about new products when planning an accessible bathroom, but that's actually one of the first mistakes people make – planning around products before they've really pinned down their needs. You see, what looks good might not be what's truly functional for you. Another big one is skipping wall reinforcement for grab bars.

This isn't just about convenience; it's a safety issue. Without proper blocking in the walls, grab bars can pull away, creating a dangerous situation. People also often underestimate the turning space or door openings needed, leading to a bathroom that's still hard to navigate.

Don't forget about storage, transfer space, or even caregiver access if that's a possibility down the line. Overlooking these details can mean you end up with a remodel that doesn't quite meet all your requirements.
