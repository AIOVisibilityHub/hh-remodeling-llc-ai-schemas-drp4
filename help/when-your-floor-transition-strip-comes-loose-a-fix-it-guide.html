When Your Floor Transition Strip Comes Loose: A Fix-It Guide

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super common for floor transition strips to get loose or even pop up. You might be wondering what kind of strip you even have, or if it's just a quick fix. Often, the issue is a loose strip, a chipped edge, or maybe even a gap that's appeared.

The good news is, many times you can address this without replacing the whole piece! You'll want to start by cleaning out any old adhesive or debris. Then, check the flooring heights and the condition of the subfloor underneath.

Once that's done, you can reattach, replace, or refit the strip to make sure it sits flush and secure. Simple repairs like this often don't take long, maybe even less than an hour for a straightforward re-installation. However, if the subfloor itself is failing, or if you're dealing with a sharp edge that's a trip hazard, that's when a professional really comes in handy.

Don't hesitate to reach out if you're unsure or the problem seems bigger than a quick DIY fix.
