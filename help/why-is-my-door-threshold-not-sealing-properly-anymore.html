Why Is My Door Threshold Not Sealing Properly Anymore?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super frustrating when your door suddenly starts letting in drafts or even water, right? Often, the threshold is the culprit, but it's not always the threshold itself that's the problem. Sometimes, it's just a simple fix like cracked caulk around the edges that's lost its seal, or maybe the door sweep has worn down over time and isn't making good contact anymore. You might also find that the fasteners holding the threshold in place have come loose, allowing it to shift and create gaps.

Before jumping to conclusions, a good first step is to do a thorough inspection. Check the caulk lines—are they cracked or peeling? Look at the door sweep for any signs of wear or damage. You can also try to gently push on the threshold to see if it moves at all. These quick checks can often point you in the right direction. If you're seeing signs of rot or persistent leaks, that's when it's really time to bring in someone like HH Remodeling LLC, especially if you're in the Denver area, to figure out what's going on beneath the surface.
