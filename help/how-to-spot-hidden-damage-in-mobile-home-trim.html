How to Spot Hidden Damage in Mobile Home Trim

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Hidden damage in mobile home trim can be a real headache if it's not caught early. It's not always obvious, but there are definitely signs you can look for. Start by doing a thorough visual inspection – look for subtle discolorations, swelling, or areas where the paint seems to be blistering or bubbling, especially near seams or where different materials meet.

Sometimes, what looks like just a paint issue is actually moisture trapped beneath. Give the trim a gentle push or tap; if it feels soft, spongy, or sounds hollow in places it shouldn't, that's a red flag for rot or internal damage. Also, pay attention to any persistent musty odors, which can indicate mold or mildew growth that's often associated with water intrusion.

Don't forget to check the areas where trim meets windows, doors, and the roofline, as these are common entry points for water. Catching these issues early can save you from more extensive and costly repairs down the road. If you're unsure, it's always best to have an expert take a look.
