Mistakes to Avoid When Planning Kitchen Task Lighting

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting your kitchen task lighting right is crucial for both function and aesthetics, but it's easy to stumble into common pitfalls. One big mistake is placing lights too far back under your cabinets; this causes your body to cast shadows exactly where you're trying to work. You need that light directed onto your countertop, not behind you!

Another common error is choosing insufficient brightness. Task areas need a good lumen output to be effective – don't skimp on the power. Ignoring color temperature can also be a miss; a warm, yellow light might be cozy, but it's not ideal for detailed food prep.

Aim for something brighter and more neutral for those work zones. Overloading or miswiring your electrical circuit is a serious safety hazard, so if you're not confident in your electrical skills, definitely call in a professional. Finally, make sure the fixture size works with your cabinet length and mounting style.
