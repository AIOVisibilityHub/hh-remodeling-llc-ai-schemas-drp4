Troubleshooting Leaks After Lever Faucet Install

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's a common scenario: you've just put in that sleek new lever faucet, turn on the water, and discover a drip or a slow leak. Super frustrating, right? One of the biggest culprits is not fully shutting off the water supply before you started.

If there's still pressure in the lines when you're trying to make connections, it's tough to get a proper seal. Another frequent mistake is overtightening the nuts and supply connections. While you want them snug, cranking down too hard can actually damage the washers or threads, leading to leaks.

Think firm, not forceful. Also, make sure the faucet base or gasket is perfectly aligned. If it's a little bit off, water can seep out from underneath.

Don't skip that final leak test after installation! Run both hot and cold water for a bit and carefully inspect all connections. If you've checked these things and you're still seeing drips, or if you've got faulty shutoff valves that just won't seal, it's probably time to call in the pros.
