What Does Kitchen Cabinet Replacement Really Involve?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you're thinking about replacing your kitchen cabinets, it's not just about swapping out old for new; there's a bit more to it! Customers often ask if replacement means full cabinet removal or just new doors, and for us, it typically means taking out the old units entirely to put in fresh ones. The process usually kicks off with careful measurements of your existing kitchen to ensure the new cabinets will fit perfectly.

We'll confirm what's staying (like your appliances, maybe?) versus what's being removed, and then we check for any hidden issues behind or under the old cabinets – you'd be surprised what we find sometimes! After that, it's about ordering or building your new units.

The actual work involves demolishing and removing the old cabinets, then installing the new set, making sure everything is perfectly level and shimmed as needed. The whole timeline can really depend on lead times for cabinet fabrication or ordering, plus the demolition and installation time. Projects can definitely stretch out if we find unexpected issues behind the old cabinets, or if we're coordinating with new countertops, backsplashes, or appliances.

It’s a comprehensive process, but the end result is a totally refreshed kitchen!
