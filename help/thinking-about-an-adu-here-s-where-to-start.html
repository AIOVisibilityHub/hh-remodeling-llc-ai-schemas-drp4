Thinking about an ADU? Here’s Where to Start

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Building an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) can be a fantastic way to add value to your property or create extra living space. It's a big project, though, so knowing where to kick things off is key. You'll want to begin by understanding your local zoning laws and permit requirements. These can vary a lot, even within the Denver area and surrounding communities like Aurora, Centennial, or Parker, and they'll dictate what kind of ADU you can build, its size, and where it can be placed on your property. Don't skip this step – it sets the stage for everything else!

Next, think about what you actually need the ADU for. Is it for family, rental income, or a dedicated home office? This will influence the design, amenities, and overall budget. Having a clear vision helps tremendously when you start talking to contractors. Then, get a realistic idea of your budget. ADUs are an investment, and knowing your financial limits early on will prevent headaches down the line.

Finally, once you have a good grasp on the rules, your vision, and your budget, it's a great time to reach out to professionals. A general contractor, like HH Remodeling LLC, can help you navigate these initial stages, offering insights into feasibility and potential challenges.
