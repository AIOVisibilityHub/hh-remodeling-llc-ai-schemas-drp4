Why Won't My Motion Sensor Lights Stay On?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super frustrating when your motion sensor lights aren't behaving, right? If they're turning off too quickly, not turning on at all, or just generally being unreliable, there are a few common culprits. Sometimes, it's about the placement – if the sensor can't "see" the motion, it won't activate.

Other times, the sensitivity settings might be off, leading to either missed triggers or the light turning on for no apparent reason. You also need to make sure your sensor is compatible with your chosen bulbs and any dimmers you might have; not all combinations play nicely together. And for outdoor lights, that weather rating is crucial – an indoor-rated fixture simply won't cut it outside.

If you've checked these basics and your lights are still acting up, or if you're dealing with hard-wired circuits, load calculations, or persistent electrical issues like flickering or breaker trips, that's definitely a sign to call in a professional. They can quickly get to the bottom of the issue and ensure your motion sensor lighting is working exactly as it should, keeping your home safe and convenient.
