When to Bring in a Pro for a Tricky Bedroom Layout

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Sometimes, you hit a wall (literally!) with your bedroom layout, and that’s when a professional can really make a difference. How do you know when it’s time to call in the experts?

Well, if your bedroom is particularly small or has a bunch of doors and windows that make furniture placement a nightmare, a designer or space planner can work wonders. They’re great at figuring out how to balance safety, access, and furniture dimensions in challenging spaces. If you or someone in your household uses mobility equipment, then an accessibility specialist becomes super important.

They'll ensure your pathways are wide enough – thinking about those 36 inches or more for main walkways – and that the room is genuinely safe and functional. They can help you meet those minimum clearance targets, like having at least 18 inches in super tight spots, or ideally 24-36 inches for comfortable movement. Essentially, if you're struggling to achieve a safe, functional, and comfortable layout on your own, don’t hesitate to reach out.

HH Remodeling LLC specializes in accessibility modifications and can help you create a bedroom that works for everyone.
