Choosing the Right Paint for Your Garage

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Picking the perfect paint for your garage can make a huge difference in both its look and durability. It's not just about color; you'll want to think about what the paint needs to stand up to. Are you looking for something super washable because of potential spills and grime?

Or is scuff resistance a bigger priority since it's a high-traffic area with tools and maybe even car doors bumping against walls? Many homeowners also appreciate brighter light reflection to make the space feel less like a cave. For garage drywall, it’s usually best to start with a good drywall or PVA-type primer to ensure everything adheres well.

Then, for the topcoat, a durable interior paint, often a satin or semi-gloss latex, is recommended because it’s easier to clean and holds up better to wear and tear. You really want something that can handle the temperature swings and general rough-and-tumble of a garage. If your garage walls are part of a larger renovation, getting professional advice can help ensure the surface prep and paint choice align perfectly with the overall project, giving you a lasting finish.
