Getting Your Interior Doors Ready for a New Coat of Paint

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Prepping your interior doors for painting is a game-changer for a smooth, lasting finish. First things first, you've got to give those doors a good clean. Dirt, grime, and any polish residue can seriously mess with paint adhesion.

After cleaning, a light 'scuff-sand' is key. This roughs up the surface just enough for the new paint to grab on, especially important for glossy doors. Don't forget to wipe away all the dust afterward!

If your doors have repairs or are super glossy, a primer will be your best friend. It helps create an even base for your finish coats. If you're also doing trim, a common sequence is to paint the trim first, then the doors, and finally the walls, making sure to carefully protect any finished surfaces as you go.

Remember to confirm if the quote you get includes sanding, priming, and even removing or taping off the hardware. These small steps make a huge difference in the final look and feel of your painted doors. For detailed doors or those with old oil finishes, a professional painter can ensure a crisp, consistent finish without the headaches.
