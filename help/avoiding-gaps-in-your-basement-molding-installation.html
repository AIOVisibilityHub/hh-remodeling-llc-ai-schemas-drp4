Avoiding Gaps in Your Basement Molding Installation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Nobody wants to see noticeable gaps in their freshly installed basement molding, right? It totally ruins the clean look you're going for. One of the biggest culprits is rushing the job and skipping some crucial steps.

First, always make sure your molding material has had time to acclimate to your basement's environment for a few days. This prevents it from moving around too much after installation. A frequent mistake is not properly dry-fitting each piece before fastening.

This is your chance to catch any fit issues and make those small adjustments, especially for corners. If you're trying to nail down molding that doesn't quite fit, you're pretty much guaranteeing a gap. Also, consider your nail spacing.

If your nails are too far apart, the molding might not pull tight to the wall or floor. Sometimes just adding an extra nail can resolve a small gap. Don't forget the caulk and wood filler!

These are your best friends for getting a truly seamless finish. Fill those nail holes and caulk any small seams to make everything disappear.
