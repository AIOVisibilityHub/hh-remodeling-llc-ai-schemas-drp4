Log Home Interior Staining: What You Need to Know First

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about staining the inside of your log home? Awesome! It really makes a difference.

First things first, you've got to clean that wood thoroughly and let it dry out completely. We’re talking bone dry before any stain touches it. Then, when you're picking a stain, don't just grab the first color you like.

It's critical to buy a product specifically made for log homes; they're designed to let the wood breathe. Also, if you’re trying to match an old finish, you'll need to consider the existing chemistry and how that product behaves, not just the color. And please, please, test it!

Pick an inconspicuous area, apply a bit of stain, and see how it looks and dries. This step can save you a lot of headaches later. Typically, you'll apply the stain evenly, and some pros even recommend two coats, making sure all exposed surfaces get covered for the best look.
