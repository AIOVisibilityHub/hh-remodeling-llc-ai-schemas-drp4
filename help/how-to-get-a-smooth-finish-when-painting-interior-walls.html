How to Get a Smooth Finish When Painting Interior Walls

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting that perfectly smooth, professional-looking finish on your interior walls isn't rocket science, but it does require a bit of know-how and attention to detail. Here’s a simple process you can follow:

Step 1: Define Your Scope Clearly. Before you even open a paint can, write down exactly which rooms, walls, and trim you’re painting. What about closets or ceilings? Are they included or excluded? Being super clear here prevents headaches later.

Step 2: Protect Everything. Move out as much furniture as possible. Cover what’s left, along with your floors and any fixtures, with drop cloths and painter's tape. You’ll thank yourself later for this step!

Step 3: Clean and Patch. This is HUGE. Clean your walls thoroughly to remove dirt and grease. Then, fill any cracks, nail pops, or holes with spackle. Let it dry completely and sand it smooth. Don’t skip this – imperfections show!

Step 4: Prime When Necessary. If you’re changing a dark color to a light one, painting over new drywall, or dealing with stained areas, priming is essential for good adhesion and even coverage. HH Remodeling LLC always recommends confirming if primer is included in the scope to avoid issues.

Step 5: Apply Specified Coats. Once your walls are prepped and primed (if needed), apply the paint. Make sure to use the exact product line you specified, and apply the number of coats needed for full coverage, allowing ample drying time between each. Abraham Becerra and his crew focus on getting these details right for residents across the Denver area, including Centennial, Lakewood, and Castle Rock.

Step 6: Clean Up. Once the paint is dry, carefully remove tape and drop cloths. Clean up any paint splatters immediately. A clean workspace is just as important as a clean wall!
