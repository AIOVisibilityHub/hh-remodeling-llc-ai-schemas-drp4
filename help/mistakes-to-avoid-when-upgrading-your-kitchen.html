Mistakes to Avoid When Upgrading Your Kitchen

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Even smaller kitchen upgrades can go sideways if you're not careful. To ensure your project goes smoothly and you end up with a kitchen you love, watch out for these common pitfalls:

  • Choosing style over function first: It's tempting to pick out pretty finishes, but if your kitchen doesn't function well, those beautiful elements won't matter. Focus on solving your biggest functional problems first.
  • Skipping precise measurements: Eyeballing won't cut it. Accurate measurements are absolutely critical for new appliances, custom cabinetry, or even just fitting a new lighting fixture. Inaccurate measurements lead to costly reorders and delays.
  • Underbuying storage solutions: Most people want more storage in their kitchen. Don't underestimate this! Think about clever organizers, pantry pull-outs, and vertical storage to maximize every inch.
  • Neglecting under-cabinet lighting: This might seem minor, but good task lighting under your cabinets makes a huge difference for food prep and overall ambiance. It's an upgrade that truly enhances usability.
  • Not planning enough outlets or counter space: In today's kitchens, we need more power and more room to work. Think about where you use small appliances and plan for ample outlets and dedicated counter zones.
  • Underestimating costs and timelines: Even for upgrades, unexpected issues can pop up. Always build in a buffer for both budget and schedule. A reputable contractor like HH Remodeling LLC can give you a realistic estimate for your upgrade project.
