Avoiding Glare and Shadows in Your Kitchen Lighting

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting your kitchen lighting just right means more than just brightness; it's about avoiding annoying glare and pesky shadows that can make cooking and daily tasks a real pain. One common mistake is using the wrong color temperature for the room. A light that's too cool or too warm can feel off and contribute to a less inviting atmosphere.

Another big one is placing lights where they create glare directly in your eyes or cast shadows exactly where you need light, like over your chopping board. This often happens when task lighting isn't properly aimed or isn't strong enough. People also frequently underestimate the need for dedicated task lighting at counters; ambient light alone won't cut it.

Lastly, failing to plan for wire routing can lead to an unsightly mess, and mixing incompatible dimmers, drivers, or fixture types can cause flickering or simply not work together.
