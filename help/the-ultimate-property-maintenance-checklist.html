The Ultimate Property Maintenance Checklist

Keeping up with property maintenance can feel like a never-ending task, but a solid checklist makes it much more manageable! Here’s a rundown of what you should be looking at to keep your home in tip-top shape.

1. Seasonal Exterior Checks: Twice a year, walk around your home. Look at your roof for loose or damaged shingles, clear out your gutters and downspouts, and check your foundation for any new cracks. Don't forget to trim back any trees or bushes that are getting too close to your house.

2. HVAC System Tune-Up: Schedule professional heating and cooling system checks annually. This isn't just about comfort; it helps with efficiency and extends the life of your units. Remember to change your filters monthly, too!

3. Plumbing Inspections: Periodically check all your faucets, toilets, and exposed pipes for any drips or leaks. Even small leaks can waste water and cause damage over time. Test your water heater's pressure relief valve once a year.

4. Electrical System Overview: Look for any flickering lights, warm outlets, or frequently tripped breakers. If you notice these, it’s a sign to call an expert. Also, test your GFCI outlets every month.

5. Appliance Maintenance: Clean your refrigerator coils, check your washing machine hoses for bulges or cracks, and clean your dryer vent to prevent fire hazards. Each appliance has its own needs, so consult their manuals.

6. Emergency Preparedness: Check smoke and carbon monoxide detectors monthly and replace batteries yearly. Know where your main water shut-off valve is and how to use it!

Sticking to a routine like this can catch issues before they become major problems.
