What to Do When Your Basement Drywall Has Water Damage

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's a bummer to find water damage on your basement drywall, right? But don't panic! The first thing you absolutely have to do is find out where that moisture is coming from and stop it.

Seriously, don't even think about patching until that's handled, or you'll be right back where you started. Once the leak is stopped, you need to get rid of any loose or soggy drywall. You can't just cover it up; mold is a real concern here, so act quickly!

For smaller spots, you might be able to patch things up, but if it's a big area, replacing the whole section is probably the better move. After removing the damaged bits, you'll patch or replace, then apply joint compound, sand it smooth, and get ready for priming and painting. For really extensive damage, or if you suspect hidden issues like mold or electrical problems, it's a good idea to bring in a pro.
