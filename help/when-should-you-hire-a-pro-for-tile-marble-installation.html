When Should You Hire a Pro for Tile & Marble Installation?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Deciding whether to DIY your tile and marble installation or call in a professional can be a tough call. While some smaller, straightforward projects might seem doable on your own, there are definitely times when bringing in a pro is not just helpful, but truly essential for a beautiful, long-lasting result. Here are some signs it's time to call in the experts:

  • Natural Stone Projects: If you're working with natural stone like marble, it's a whole different ballgame. Marble is porous and requires specific methods, like white, non-staining thin-set, careful sealing, and unsanded grout for narrow joints. Pros know these nuances.
  • Large-Format or Complex Layouts: Big tiles or intricate patterns are much harder to get perfectly aligned and flat. A small mistake can be very noticeable. Professionals have the tools and experience for precision.
  • Uneven Substrates: If your floor or wall isn't perfectly level, a pro can properly prepare and flatten the surface, which is crucial for a durable installation. Skipping this step leads to big problems later.
  • Moisture-Prone Areas: Bathrooms, showers, and other wet areas need proper waterproofing and attention to detail to prevent leaks and mold. This isn't an area to cut corners.
  • Diagnosing Existing Issues: If you have cracks, water damage, or other underlying structural problems, a professional can diagnose these issues and recommend the right solutions before tiling begins. They can save you from future headaches and costly repairs. For any of these situations, bringing in someone like HH Remodeling LLC ensures your investment is protected and your new tile or marble looks fantastic.
