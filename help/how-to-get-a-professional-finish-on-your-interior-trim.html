How to Get a Professional Finish on Your Interior Trim

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting that crisp, seamless look on your interior trim really makes a difference in a room. It's all about precision and a few key steps. Here's how to aim for a professional finish:

  • Measure and Cut Precisely: This is the foundation. Measure each piece multiple times before cutting. For corners, make sure your miter saw is set perfectly, and always cut a test piece first to check the angle. Even a tiny error will show.
  • Dry-Fit Everything: Before you reach for the nail gun, place all your trim pieces in position without fastening them. This allows you to check for gaps, inconsistencies, and makes sure all your corners meet perfectly. Adjust cuts as needed.
  • Fasten Securely and Thoughtfully: Use an appropriate nail gun with nails that are long enough to secure the trim to the wall studs, but not so long they pop through the other side! Avoid nailing too close to the edges of the trim, which can cause splitting.
  • Apply Glue to Mitered Joints: This is a pro secret! A little wood glue on those mitered corner joints dramatically increases their strength and helps prevent them from separating over time, keeping your trim looking tight and seamless.
  • Fill Holes and Caulk Gaps: Once everything is fastened, use wood filler to meticulously fill all nail holes. Let it dry, then sand it smooth. Finish by caulking any small gaps where the trim meets the wall or other trim pieces. This creates a smooth transition.

Following these steps will get you a finish you'll be proud of!
