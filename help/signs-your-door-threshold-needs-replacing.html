Signs Your Door Threshold Needs Replacing

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Your door threshold might seem like a small detail, but it plays a big role in keeping your home comfortable and protected from the elements. So, how do you know when it's time for a replacement? One of the clearest signs is visible damage like cracks, rot, or significant wear. If your threshold is crumbling or deteriorating, it's not doing its job anymore.

Another red flag is if you feel drafts coming from under your door or notice moisture seeping in, especially after rain or snow. A damaged or improperly sealed threshold can create gaps that compromise your door's weather seal, letting in cold air, water, and even pests. You might also notice that your door sweep isn't making good contact with the threshold, creating an obvious opening.

If your door isn't closing smoothly and consistently, or if it's binding against the threshold, that's another indicator. An old, warped, or improperly installed threshold can cause alignment issues, making your door difficult to operate. Over time, thresholds can also become uneven, creating a tripping hazard. If you're noticing any of these issues around your home in the Denver area, it's probably time to consider a new threshold.
