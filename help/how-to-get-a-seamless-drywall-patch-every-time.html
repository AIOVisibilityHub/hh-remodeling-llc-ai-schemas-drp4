How to Get a Seamless Drywall Patch Every Time

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Want to tackle a drywall repair and have it look like nothing ever happened? It’s totally doable, but it takes a few key steps to get that smooth, invisible finish. First off, you've got to find out what caused the damage in the first place, especially if it's water-related.

Fix that first! Then, carefully remove any loose or damaged drywall. If there’s any moisture, you really need to dry the area completely before moving on.

Next, install a new piece of drywall or a patch, making sure it's secure. This is where backing can really help for larger holes. Then comes the joint compound, which is where many DIYers get tripped up.

Apply thin, even coats, letting each one dry completely – sometimes overnight – before sanding. Don't rush this part; skipping drying time is a common mistake. After a good sanding, you'll want to match the texture of the surrounding wall.

This might be a simple smooth finish, or it could involve a texture spray or technique. Finally, prime and repaint. Matching the paint sheen is just as important as matching the texture.
