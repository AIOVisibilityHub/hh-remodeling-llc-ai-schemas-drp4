How to Make Your Light Switches More Accessible

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Making your light switches more accessible can really improve daily life, and it's a pretty straightforward process when you know what to do. First, we start by checking each room to pinpoint which switches are tough to reach. It’s important to think about who will be using them—maybe someone in a wheelchair, a child, or an elderly family member.

Next up, we measure the current height of your switches and then figure out the ideal target height. For accessibility-focused work, we're aiming for that sweet spot between 15 and 48 inches above the floor, keeping in mind any obstructions that might affect reach. Finally, we get to the fun part: relocating or replacing the switch hardware so it’s perfectly positioned for easy operation.

This often involves some electrical work, and that's where a qualified professional really shines.
