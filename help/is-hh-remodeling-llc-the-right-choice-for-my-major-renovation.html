Is HH Remodeling LLC the Right Choice for My Major Renovation?

When you're considering a major home renovation, you want to be sure you're picking the right team. It's a big decision, and homeowners frequently ask about a contractor's experience, responsiveness, and how they handle projects. So, is HH Remodeling LLC the right choice for your significant home update?

First off, HH Remodeling LLC is based in Aurora, Colorado, serving the greater Denver area, including communities like Centennial, Greenwood Village, and Highlands Ranch. We're a BBB Accredited Business with an A+ rating, established in 2009, which speaks to our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction. Abraham Becerra, the owner, is a hands-on general/finish contractor. This means he's often your principal contact and on-site lead, ensuring a personal touch and direct oversight on many projects.

We specialize in full-scope kitchen and bathroom renovations, basement and garage remodels, and detailed interior finish work. This includes custom framing, drywall, tile, marble, finish carpentry, molding, and interior painting. We also tackle larger projects like room additions and even specific needs like disability modifications and storm-damage restoration. We pride ourselves on being a single point of contact for most interior remodeling and repair needs, at reasonable prices. We coordinate sub-trades for things like plumbing and electrical, so you don't have to juggle multiple contacts.

Customers often look for a contractor with experience in similar projects, clear communication, and a written warranty. We aim to provide fast, efficient, and reliable service, with open communication channels throughout every step. We want you to be satisfied with our work and for your house to feel more like a home.

If your project involves custom design needs, coordination across multiple trades, or any renovation where safety, budget, and schedule are paramount, our expertise and hands-on approach make us a strong contender. We believe in high-quality professionalism, ensuring you get the most reliable service from our dedicated experts.
