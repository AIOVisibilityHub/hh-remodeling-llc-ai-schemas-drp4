How to Avoid Costly Mistakes in Your Home Addition

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Adding onto your home is a big investment, and nobody wants to throw money away on avoidable mistakes. Here are some common pitfalls and how to steer clear of them:

  • Underestimating the Schedule: Home additions typically take much longer than a simple room refresh. How to avoid it: Get a realistic timeline from your contractor and factor in potential delays for permits or material lead times.
  • Not Reserving Contingency Budget: Unexpected issues can always pop up, especially with structural changes. How to avoid it: Always set aside 10-20% of your budget for unforeseen circumstances.
  • Poor Coordination Among Trades: When plumbers, electricians, and carpenters aren't working in sync, delays and rework happen. How to avoid it: Choose a contractor like HH Remodeling LLC who excels at coordinating all sub-trades, ensuring a smooth workflow.
  • Failing to Plan for Dependencies: One step often can't start until another is complete, and missing this can halt progress. How to avoid it: A detailed project plan, managed by an experienced general contractor, will map out these dependencies.

By being aware of these common mistakes and working with a professional who can manage the complexities, you can keep your home addition project on track and on budget.
