When You Need a Pro for Storm Damage Repair

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It’s not always obvious when a storm-damaged interior calls for professional help versus a DIY fix. Here are some clear signs it’s time to pick up the phone and get an expert involved:

  • Significant Water Intrusion: If you have more than a small puddle, especially if it’s from a compromised roof or burst pipe, you need immediate professional attention to prevent further damage, mold, and structural issues.
  • Structural Damage: Visible cracks in walls, sagging ceilings, or shifted foundations are major red flags. These aren't just cosmetic; they can compromise the safety of your home.
  • Electrical Risks: Any damage near electrical outlets, wiring, or appliances, especially involving water, is extremely dangerous. Don't risk it; call a pro and potentially shut off your main power if you know how to do so safely.
  • Suspected Hidden Moisture or Mold: If you smell mildew or suspect water has gotten into walls or under flooring, hidden moisture can lead to serious mold problems. Professionals have tools to detect and properly remediate these issues.
  • Insurance Claim Involvement: If you're going to file an insurance claim, a qualified contractor with experience in storm damage can streamline the process, provide proper documentation, and work directly with your adjuster. We often coordinate with insurers to ensure your restoration is handled efficiently in areas across the greater Denver metro area.
