Mistakes to Avoid When Painting Your Bathroom

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Painting a bathroom might seem straightforward, but a few common slip-ups can really impact how long your new paint job lasts. Here are some mistakes clients often make and how to steer clear of them:

  • Skipping surface prep: You'd be surprised how many folks just paint right over dirty walls or existing mildew. Always clean your walls, remove any mildew, and repair cracks first. It makes a huge difference.
  • Using the wrong caulk: Standard silicone caulk might seem fine, but if it's not paintable, you'll end up with a mess. Make sure to use paintable caulk for those trim and joint lines.
  • Rushing drying times: Bathrooms are high-moisture areas, and they need extra time for paint to dry and cure. Don't use the shower too soon after painting; ideally, wait overnight or even a few days for the best results.
  • Underestimating coats: Sometimes one coat just isn't enough, especially with color changes or in high-traffic bathrooms. Plan for at least two coats, and be prepared for touch-ups.
  • Ignoring the details: Ceilings, trim, doors, and the area behind the toilet often get overlooked. Make sure your painting plan includes all these spots for a complete, professional look.
