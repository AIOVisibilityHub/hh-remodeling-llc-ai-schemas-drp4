What to Expect During Kitchen Drywall Installation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting new drywall in your kitchen might sound a bit messy, but knowing what to expect can help ease your mind. Generally, the process kicks off with our team carefully measuring and cutting the drywall panels to fit your kitchen's framework. This part requires precision, especially around windows, doors, and cabinet areas.

Once the panels are cut, we'll securely fasten them to the framing. After that, the real artistry begins with taping the seams. This is a crucial step for a smooth finish! We'll apply multiple coats of joint compound over the tape and screw heads, letting each coat dry completely before adding the next. This drying time is often where most of the project's duration comes in.

After all the coats are applied and thoroughly dry, we move on to sanding. This can create some dust, so we take precautions to minimize the spread and ensure thorough cleanup afterward. Finally, once the sanding is complete and the surface is perfectly smooth, the new drywall is ready for priming and painting. We always aim for a finish level that seamlessly blends with your existing walls, ensuring your satisfaction throughout the entire process. Communication is key, and we'll keep you informed every step of the way.
