Is Your Living Room Dark? What to Do About Inadequate Lighting

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

If your living room feels more like a cave than a cozy retreat, inadequate lighting might be the culprit. It's not just about aesthetics; good lighting is crucial for safe movement, especially at night. So, how can you brighten things up without turning your living room into an operating theater?

Start by pinpointing those dim spots in your room. Are there corners that always feel shadowed, or is the path from your favorite chair to the doorway hard to see after dark? Once you've identified these areas, think about adding or upgrading your ambient lighting.

This is your general room illumination. Consider wall-mounted or overhead fixtures where possible, as they can provide more even light distribution than a single floor lamp. For seating areas, pair table lamps with switches that are easy to reach from a seated position – no fumbling around in the dark!

And don't forget to light up entry paths and any routes you frequently take through your home at night. While there's no official deadline for fixing dim lighting, it's considered a fall hazard, so addressing it promptly is always a good idea. If your lighting upgrade involves new circuits, dimmers, or hardwired fixtures, that's when it's definitely time to bring in a licensed electrician.
