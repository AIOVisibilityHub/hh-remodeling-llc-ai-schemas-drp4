What to Look for in a Professional Door Painting Quote

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you're getting ready to have your interior doors painted, a detailed quote is super important for avoiding surprises. You'll want to know if the quote includes things like sanding and priming — these steps are crucial for a durable finish. Don't forget to ask if the painter will remove or tape off your hardware; it makes a huge difference in the final look.

It’s also good to confirm that both faces and all edges of the door will be painted. Another big one is asking what kind of paint product and finish they plan to use. For high-touch surfaces like doors, you really want a durable finish that can stand up to daily wear and tear.

Make sure the written quote clearly identifies all the surfaces being painted and the specific products. Also, inquire about how many coats are standard. Knowing these details upfront helps you compare quotes accurately and ensures you get the quality you expect.

If you have paneled doors or doors with old oil-based paint, professional help is especially valuable to ensure a smooth, lasting finish without issues like peeling or sticking.
