How to Plan a Glare-Free Lighting Upgrade for Your Denver Home

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Planning a non-glare lighting upgrade for your home in the Denver area involves more than just picking pretty fixtures. It's about creating a comfortable, functional, and visually pleasant environment. Here's a walkthrough of the typical process to ensure you get it right.

Step 1: Evaluate Your Current Lighting and Glare Sources. Before anything else, a lighting contractor will usually come in and evaluate your existing setup. They'll look at what's causing glare – is it exposed bulbs, too much brightness, or poor fixture placement? This initial assessment is crucial for understanding the problem areas and your current lighting needs.

Step 2: Select the Right Fixtures and Shielding. This is where the magic happens! Based on the evaluation, the contractor will help you choose fixtures that specifically reduce glare. This might mean lights with better optics, integrated shielding, or different lens types. They'll also consider color and beam control to ensure the light is directed where you need it without causing discomfort.

Step 3: Develop a Detailed Layout and Installation Plan. A good plan isn't just about what lights go where; it's about how they'll perform. The contractor will check the proposed plan against how you intend to use the area. For outdoor lighting, discussions about project length, maintenance, and warranty terms are typically covered upfront. This ensures the upgrade will provide enough usable light while effectively minimizing glare.

Step 4: Professional Installation and Testing. Once the plan is solid and fixtures are selected, the installation begins. For complex upgrades, this might involve rewiring or replacing existing units. After installation, thorough testing ensures everything works as intended, providing that comfortable, glare-free illumination you're looking for.
