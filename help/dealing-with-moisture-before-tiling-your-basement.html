Dealing with Moisture Before Tiling Your Basement

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting ready to tile your basement? That's awesome, but let's talk about moisture first. It's a huge factor in basement tiling, and it’s a frequent mistake to skip those all-important moisture checks.

You see, basements are prone to dampness, and if you don't address it, you could end up with a tile job that doesn't last. The key steps here start with inspecting and cleaning your slab. Look for any cracks and get those repaired.

Then, you absolutely need to check for moisture. It's not something you want to guess about! Based on those moisture readings, you'll choose the right moisture-resistant adhesive or thin-set, and possibly a suitable underlayment or isolation membrane.

These steps are critical for a long-lasting bond. Once everything is prepped and dry, then you can set your tile, grout, and seal it as needed. Remember, curing time matters; wait at least 24 hours before walking on new tile.
