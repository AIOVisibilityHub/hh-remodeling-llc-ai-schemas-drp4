Are Rocker Switches Right for Your Home?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about upgrading your light switches to rocker style? Many of our clients ask if they’re easier to use than traditional toggles and if they help with accessibility. The short answer is often yes!

Rocker switches are designed for easier activation; you just press the top or bottom of a flat panel rather than flipping a small lever. This can be a huge benefit for anyone with dexterity issues, or even just for convenience in a busy household. If you're looking for an accessibility upgrade, a rocker switch at the right height can make a world of difference.

However, simply installing a rocker switch without considering its location is a common mistake. The switch still needs to be within an accessible reach range, typically between 15 and 48 inches above the floor, especially if it's part of an overall accessibility project. We also need to think about the switch's feel, how easily it activates, and if it's compatible with your existing wiring or dimming needs.
