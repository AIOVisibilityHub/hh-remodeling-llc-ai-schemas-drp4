Key Steps for an Accessible Laundry Room Design

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Designing an accessible laundry room is all about smart planning to make daily chores easier for everyone. Let's break down the key steps to get it right. First, you've got to ensure routes are completely unobstructed.

This means thinking about how you enter the room, move between machines, and access detergents or baskets. Second, it’s crucial to provide enough clear approach space in front of each machine; imagine a 30 x 48-inch rectangle where someone could comfortably position themselves. Third, always reserve plenty of turning space, aiming for about 60 inches in diameter if possible, to allow for easy maneuverability with mobility devices.

Fourth, arrange storage and folding surfaces so they are within easy reach, not high up where you'd have to strain or stand on a stool. Lastly, don't forget the details like door swings, thresholds, and even the entry to the utility room. Planning for accessibility before construction starts is super important, as retrofitting these clearances later can be much more complex and expensive.

If you're dealing with structural changes or plumbing, definitely call in a professional to help.
