How to Smooth Out Uneven Floors Between Rooms

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super frustrating when you've got a noticeable step-up or step-down between rooms, right? You're probably wondering if the fix involves the flooring itself, the subfloor, or just a different transition piece. The first step is to accurately measure the height difference and the span of the area.

You'll also want to check for any subfloor movement or unevenness, as that could be the root cause. From there, you've got a few options: you might be able to sand down high spots, fill in low areas, or even reinstall the adjacent flooring. Sometimes, simply installing a better-matched transition profile is all it takes to create a smoother, safer flow.

These kinds of projects often line up with flooring replacement or a bigger remodel, since transitions usually go in after the subfloor prep but before the final trim. A big mistake people often make is ignoring the actual finished-floor heights, or picking a transition for looks instead of how well it will hold up. If you've got a large level difference, or if the subfloor seems damaged, that's definitely a time to bring in an expert to make sure it's done right and safely.
