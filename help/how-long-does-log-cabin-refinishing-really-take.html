How Long Does Log Cabin Refinishing Really Take?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

One of the most common questions we get about log cabin refinishing is, 'How long will this take?' And honestly, it really depends on the scope of the project! A full restoration, which can involve a lot of prep work and multiple coats, often takes about 3 to 6 weeks.

This includes everything from cleaning and preparing the surfaces to applying the new finish and ensuring it cures properly. If your cabin renovation is more extensive, maybe involving some repairs or other upgrades, you could be looking at 4 to 12 weeks. The size of your cabin and its current condition play a huge role in this timeline.

For example, a smaller cabin with a relatively well-maintained finish will naturally take less time than a larger one that has neglected surfaces or significant damage. We always aim for open communication throughout the process, so you'll know exactly what to expect. While it's a commitment, the result is a beautifully protected and refreshed cabin you can enjoy for years.
