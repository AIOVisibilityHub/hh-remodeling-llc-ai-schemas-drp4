Avoiding Common Ramp Installation Mistakes

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Installing a ramp seems straightforward, but there are a few common slip-ups people make. Let's make sure you don't fall into these traps!

  • Measuring Only One Side of the Threshold: It's super important to measure the height across the entire threshold, not just one spot. Uneven floors happen, and you need that full picture for an accurate 'rise' measurement.
  • Ignoring Door-Swing Interference: Always check how your door opens and closes with the ramp in place. A ramp that blocks your door is more of a headache than a help. Make sure there’s enough clearance for smooth operation.
  • Choosing a Ramp That's Too Steep: The golden rule for accessibility is often a 1:12 slope – that means for every 1 inch of rise, you need 12 inches of ramp length. A steeper ramp can be really difficult or even unsafe for mobility devices.
  • Failing to Confirm Weight Capacity: Ramps need to support their users. Always double-check the manufacturer's weight capacity to ensure it's robust enough for the intended use.
  • Overlooking Surface Conditions: Is the ground underneath stable? Will the ramp shift? Make sure the surface is solid and that the ramp can be securely installed per manufacturer instructions. If you’re facing a complex situation, or need to ensure compliance, getting a professional involved can prevent these mistakes and ensure a safe, lasting solution.
