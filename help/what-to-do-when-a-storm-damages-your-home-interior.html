What to Do When a Storm Damages Your Home Interior

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When a storm hits and your home's interior takes a beating, it's easy to feel overwhelmed. First things first, make sure everyone is safe! Once that's covered, your immediate priority should be to prevent any further damage.

This often means quick temporary fixes like tarping a leaky roof or removing standing water to stop things from getting worse. It's super important to document everything with photos and videos before you do any major cleanup or demolition. Think of it as building your case for the insurance company.

After you've got the initial damage controlled and documented, then you can start thinking about structural drying or demolition. Remember, getting a professional involved early, especially with water intrusion or potential structural issues, can make a huge difference. They can help navigate the next steps and ensure everything is handled correctly, preventing bigger headaches down the line.
