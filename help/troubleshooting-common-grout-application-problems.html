Troubleshooting Common Grout Application Problems

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Grouting might seem straightforward, but it's surprisingly easy to run into common problems that can affect the look and longevity of your tiled surfaces. One frequent mistake we see is using too much water during the cleaning phase. While a damp sponge is essential for wiping away excess grout and haze, an overly wet sponge can actually wash out the joints, leaving them shallow or uneven. This weakens the grout and can make it more susceptible to damage.

Another big no-no is applying grout sealer too early. Grout needs to be completely dry before sealing, which can take anywhere from 24 hours to 3 days, depending on the product. Sealing too soon traps moisture, which can lead to discoloration or even mildew growth.

And, of course, letting grout dry on the tile face before thoroughly removing the haze is a classic. That tough, cloudy film is a nightmare to get off once it's set.
