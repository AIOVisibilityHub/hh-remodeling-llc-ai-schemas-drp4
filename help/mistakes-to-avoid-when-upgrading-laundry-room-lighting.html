Mistakes to Avoid When Upgrading Laundry Room Lighting

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you're trying to brighten up your laundry room, it's easy to fall into some common traps that actually make the lighting worse. Here are a few mistakes to steer clear of:

  • Relying on a Single Overhead Fixture: This is probably the biggest mistake. One light simply can't eliminate all shadows, especially around tall appliances.
  • How to avoid: Plan for layered lighting, combining ceiling fixtures with task lighting over work surfaces.
  • Ignoring Dark Zones: If you've got shadowy areas near your machines or where you sort clothes, your lighting isn't doing its job.
  • How to avoid: Assess the room to identify all dark spots and place lights strategically to illuminate them.
  • Using Poorly Protected Fittings: Laundry rooms can be damp, so standard light fixtures might not be safe.
  • How to avoid: Always choose damp-location-rated fixtures, especially if there's any moisture or steam.
  • Creating Glare Instead of Visibility: Sometimes a bright light just causes a blinding glare without actually helping you see better.
  • How to avoid: Opt for diffuse lighting or fixtures with shades that spread light evenly without harsh spots.
  • Overlooking Color Temperature: Lighting that's too warm or too cool can affect how well you see details on clothing.
  • How to avoid: Choose LED lighting in the 4000K-5000K range for optimal visibility and accurate color rendering.

Avoiding these mistakes will ensure your lighting upgrade truly makes your laundry room brighter and safer.
