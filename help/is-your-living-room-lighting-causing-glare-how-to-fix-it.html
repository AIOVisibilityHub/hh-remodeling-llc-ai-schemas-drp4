Is Your Living Room Lighting Causing Glare? How to Fix It

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Glare in your living room lighting can be really annoying, making it hard to relax or even see clearly. It's often caused by light that's too bright or poorly placed, reflecting off shiny surfaces or even your TV screen. The good news is, you can totally fix this!

First, identify where the glare is coming from. Is it a bare bulb, a lamp in a bad spot, or light bouncing off a window or mirror? Once you know the source, you can start making changes.

Try using diffusers or lampshades on bare bulbs to soften the light. Positioning is key; avoid placing bright lights directly opposite reflective surfaces. You might also consider upgrading to bulbs with a lower lumen output if the current ones are just too intense for the space.

If you're relying on one main overhead light, try introducing more layered lighting with several smaller sources like table lamps and floor lamps. This helps distribute the light more evenly and reduces harsh contrasts that cause glare. While there aren't strict deadlines for these fixes, addressing glare promptly can significantly improve comfort and visibility.
