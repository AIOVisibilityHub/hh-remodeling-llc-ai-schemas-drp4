Why Your Ceiling Paint Looks Streaky and Uneven

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's a common and frustrating problem: you finish painting a ceiling, step back, and instead of a smooth, uniform surface, you see streaks, roller marks, or an uneven finish. What went wrong? Often, it comes down to a few key issues that are easy to overlook.

One major culprit is failing to adequately prep the ceiling surface. If there are existing stains, cracks, or nail pops, simply painting over them won't make them disappear; they'll likely show through. Another common mistake is not using primer when it's needed, especially over water stains or very dark previous colors, which can lead to blotchy coverage.

The application method also plays a big role; uneven roller pressure or not maintaining a wet edge can result in visible lap marks. Lastly, poor lighting during the painting process can make it hard to spot missed areas or inconsistencies until it's too late. Ensuring thorough prep, using the right primer, and paying attention to your application technique are vital for a smooth, streak-free ceiling.
