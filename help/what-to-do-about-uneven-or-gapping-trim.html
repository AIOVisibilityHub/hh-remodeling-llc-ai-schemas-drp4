What to Do About Uneven or Gapping Trim

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Uneven or gapping trim can really make a room feel unfinished, can't it? Whether it's around your doors, windows, or along the baseboards, those gaps and misalignments are just plain annoying. Often, people notice these issues after new flooring has been laid, or perhaps after some door work, because any changes to the surrounding structure can affect how the trim sits.

A common mistake is just trying to caulk over large gaps without understanding why they're there in the first place. Sometimes, the issue isn't the trim itself, but rather an uneven wall, an out-of-plumb door frame, or even slight shifts in your home's foundation. The process usually involves carefully removing the old trim, checking the underlying surfaces for level and plumb, and then cutting and fitting new trim precisely.

Getting those mitered corners just right and ensuring a flush fit against uneven surfaces is where the real skill comes in. If you're finding that your walls are really uneven, or if the trim problems seem connected to door movement or foundational issues, it’s usually a sign that this isn't just a cosmetic fix. That's when calling in a professional like us can make a huge difference in getting that perfectly finished look.
