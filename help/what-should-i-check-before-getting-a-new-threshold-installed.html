What Should I Check Before Getting a New Threshold Installed?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting a new threshold installed is a great way to improve your home's energy efficiency and appearance, but a little prep can make the whole process smoother. Here’s a quick checklist of things to consider before you start:

  • Measure Accurately: Grab your tape measure and get the exact width of your door opening from jamb to jamb, and also the height from the floor to where the door closes. These numbers are crucial for choosing the right threshold size.
  • Inspect the Sill and Framing: Take a good look at the area where the threshold will sit. Is there any rot, water damage, or signs of mold? If so, these underlying issues need to be addressed before a new threshold goes in to ensure a lasting repair.
  • Consider Your Door System: Think about how your door currently operates. Do you have a standard hinged door, or something more complex like a sliding or bifold door? The threshold choice will need to be compatible with its hardware and movement.
  • Evaluate Your Flooring: What kind of flooring do you have on both sides of the doorway? The new threshold needs to transition smoothly and securely with wood, tile, carpet, or concrete. Material compatibility is key for a seamless look and proper function.
  • Think About Drainage (Exterior Doors): For any exterior thresholds, remember that a slight slope away from the house is essential for water drainage. If your current setup doesn't have this, it's something to discuss with your installer.
