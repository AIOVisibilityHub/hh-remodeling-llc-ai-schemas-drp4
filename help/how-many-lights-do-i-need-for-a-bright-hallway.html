How Many Lights Do I Need for a Bright Hallway?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Figuring out the right number of lights for your hallway is all about balancing brightness and aesthetics. You want enough light to make the space feel inviting and safe, but not so much that it feels like an operating room! The key is to avoid dark spots, especially in longer hallways.

A good rule of thumb is to measure your hallway's length and then consider the type of fixture. For recessed lights, you might aim for one every 4-6 feet. For flush-mount or semi-flush-mount fixtures, you might space them a bit wider, perhaps every 6-8 feet, depending on their light output.

Think about the purpose of the hallway too – is it a main thoroughfare or a quiet passage? Ultimately, you're aiming for even illumination. If you're unsure about the exact number or placement, especially for new wiring, getting a pro involved can save you a lot of guesswork and ensure the end result is exactly what you envision.
