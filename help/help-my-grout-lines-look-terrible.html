Help! My Grout Lines Look Terrible!

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Uh oh, messy grout lines can definitely detract from a beautiful tile job. Let’s talk about some common culprits and how to avoid them. One major mistake is cleaning the grout too soon after it’s applied or using too much water during the cleaning process.

This can wash away the pigment or weaken the grout, leading to uneven colors and crumbling. Another issue can be uneven spacing of the tiles themselves; if the tiles aren't perfectly aligned and leveled during installation, no amount of perfect grouting will make them look right. Poor surface preparation before tiling can also contribute to problems.

If the subfloor isn't level, your tiles will be uneven, and that will show in the grout lines. Also, using the wrong type of trowel or not getting full mortar coverage under each tile can cause issues. For a flawless finish, it’s often best to trust professionals who know the nuances of tile setting and grouting.
