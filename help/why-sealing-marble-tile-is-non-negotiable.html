Why Sealing Marble Tile Is Non-Negotiable

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

If you're investing in the timeless beauty of marble tile, there's one step you absolutely cannot skip: sealing. It's truly non-negotiable for keeping your marble looking its best and protecting your investment. Marble is a porous natural stone, meaning it has tiny openings that can easily absorb liquids.

Without sealing, spills like coffee, wine, or even just water can penetrate the stone and leave permanent stains or discoloration. When we install marble, we make sure to seal it as part of the process. This creates a protective barrier, making the tile much more resistant to stains and easier to clean.

It's also super important that you don't use the tiled area until that initial sealer has fully cured. After installation, you'll also need to reseal your marble periodically, typically once a year or every other year, depending on how much wear and tear it gets. Think of it as essential maintenance for a luxurious surface.

Skipping this critical step is one of the most common mistakes people make, and it can really diminish the beauty and longevity of your marble. Always protect your beautiful marble!
