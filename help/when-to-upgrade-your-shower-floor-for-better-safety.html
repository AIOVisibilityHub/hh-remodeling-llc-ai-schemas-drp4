When to Upgrade Your Shower Floor for Better Safety

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

You might be wondering if your current shower floor is safe enough, especially as homes get older or if someone in the family has mobility concerns. Knowing when it's time to upgrade for better slip resistance can really make a difference in preventing accidents.

Here are some clear signs it's time to consider a shower floor upgrade

  • Your current floor is visibly smooth or glossy: If your shower tile has a shiny finish, it's likely very slippery when wet and soapy. This is a primary indicator for an upgrade.
  • Water pools in your shower: If you notice standing water long after you've turned off the shower, your drainage slope might be insufficient, creating a slip hazard even on a textured surface.
  • You're using bath mats in the shower: Relying on mats inside the shower means your current floor isn't cutting it. A permanent, integrated solution is much safer.
  • Someone in your household has accessibility needs: For seniors or those with limited mobility, a highly slip-resistant floor is crucial, often combined with grab bars and proper lighting.
  • Your existing grout is sparse or worn: If you have large-format tiles with minimal grout, or if the grout is deteriorating, you're losing valuable traction.
  • You've had near-slips or actual falls: This is the most critical sign. Don't wait for another incident.

If any of these sound familiar, it's definitely time to talk to a professional.
