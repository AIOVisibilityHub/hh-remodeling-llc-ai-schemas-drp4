Key Steps to a Smooth Finish Carpentry Project

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting finish carpentry right means following a clear process to ensure a beautiful outcome. Here’s a simple timeline to help you understand what's involved.

Phase 1: Initial Planning & Scope Review. This is where we discuss your vision, review the existing conditions of your home, and figure out exactly what work needs to be done. We'll look at things like trim, casing, baseboards, crown molding, and any built-ins you're dreaming of. It's crucial to confirm room-by-room measurements and identify any profiles we need to match or create.

Phase 2: Material Selection & Preparation. Once the scope is clear, we’ll help you select the right materials. This phase also involves preparing the space, making sure it’s ready for the detailed work ahead. Remember, finish carpentry typically happens after the main construction and messy trades are done to protect the delicate finished surfaces.

Phase 3: Installation & Fitting. This is where the magic happens! We’ll carefully install and scribe all the trim, doors, and casework. This includes setting and shimming pieces, fastening them securely, and ensuring everything is aligned perfectly. We pay close attention to consistent reveals around doors and clean, tight joints everywhere.

Phase 4: Finishing Touches & Inspection. After installation, we move to the final steps: filling nail holes, caulking seams, and light sanding to prepare for paint or stain. We'll perform a thorough check to ensure all reveals are consistent and the operation of any doors or built-ins is smooth.

Your satisfaction is our priority, so we’ll walk through the project with you to make sure every detail meets your expectations.
