Navigating Accessible Bathroom Renovations: A Complete Guide

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about an accessible bathroom renovation can feel a bit overwhelming, right? It's a big step towards making your home work better for you. The first thing you'll want to do is a really thorough needs assessment.

This means thinking about what activities are tough right now, whether you prefer a shower or tub, and where you'll need grab bars or space for medical supplies. Don't forget to consider future needs as your mobility might change over time! Once you've got a good handle on your needs, you can start planning the layout and clearances, making sure there's enough room to move around comfortably.

A crucial step is adding structural support inside the walls before you put up grab bars; skipping this can lead to unsafe situations later on. Finally, choosing slip-resistant surfaces and accessible hardware like lever handles will make a huge difference in day-to-day use. If you're tackling structural changes, plumbing, or waterproofing, that's when bringing in a remodeler with accessibility experience, like us at HH Remodeling LLC, becomes super important to ensure everything is safe and functional.
