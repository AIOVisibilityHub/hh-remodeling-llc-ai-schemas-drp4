Trim Repair: DIY or Call a Professional?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

So, you've got some trim that's seen better days, and you're weighing whether to tackle it yourself or call in a pro. It’s a common dilemma! For purely cosmetic issues like small dings, minor nail holes, or a bit of recaulking, a DIY approach can totally work.

If it's a simple refresh and you're comfortable with a caulk gun and some paint, go for it! However, things get a bit trickier when the trim is gapping because of uneven walls, if it just won’t sit flush, or if the damage is more extensive. We often see folks struggling with getting perfectly mitered corners or trying to make new trim match existing profiles seamlessly.

Another big red flag for calling in help is if the trim problems seem linked to underlying issues like a door frame that's out of square, uneven floors, or persistent door movement. If the trim isn't just a surface issue, but a symptom of something bigger, a professional can properly diagnose and address the root cause, ensuring the repair lasts. For precise cuts, complex fits, or when the trim is part of a larger structural repair, getting experienced help ensures a much cleaner, more durable finish.

Give us a call if you're not sure which route to take!
