Avoiding Common Mistakes in Log Cabin Staining

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When it comes to log and cabin finish upgrades, there are a few common pitfalls folks often stumble into. Knowing these can help you avoid costly mistakes and get the best results. First off, a big no-no is applying new stain directly over failing old coatings.

If your current finish is peeling or flaking, you absolutely need to remove it first. Trying to stain over it just won't give you a good, lasting finish. Another common error is using the wrong blasting media if you're going that route for removal; it can damage the wood if not done correctly.

People also sometimes miss the early signs of finish failure, like water no longer beading, color loss, or those gray, fuzzy wood fibers. Waiting too long just makes the job harder. Finally, not thinking about the long-term maintenance cycle of a finish system is a mistake.

Some finishes need more attention sooner, especially on walls facing south and west. By being aware of these, you're already ahead of the game. If you're unsure about any of these steps, or if your cabin has extensive peeling, it’s a good idea to bring in a professional.
