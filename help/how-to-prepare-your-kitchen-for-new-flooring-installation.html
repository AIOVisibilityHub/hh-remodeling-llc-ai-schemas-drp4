How to Prepare Your Kitchen for New Flooring Installation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting ready for new kitchen flooring can seem a bit overwhelming, but a little prep goes a long way to ensure a smooth installation. Here's a simple checklist to get you started:

  • Clear the Space: Remove all small appliances, kitchenware, and anything else on your countertops and in lower cabinets. This helps create a clear workspace for the installers.
  • Empty Your Fridge and Pantry: If your refrigerator or pantry cabinet needs to be moved, try to empty it as much as possible beforehand. Lighter appliances are easier and safer to move.
  • Protect Nearby Areas: Lay down old sheets or plastic coverings in adjacent rooms and walkways to minimize dust and protect your existing floors from traffic during the project. Drywall dust, especially, can travel.
  • Confirm Appliance Plans: Discuss with your installer who is responsible for disconnecting and reconnecting appliances like your dishwasher and refrigerator. HH Remodeling LLC can often handle these details, but it's good to confirm ahead of time.
  • Subfloor Check: Understand if any subfloor repairs or leveling are needed. This is a critical step for a successful installation and something a professional like us will assess.

Taking these steps helps ensure your project moves along efficiently and minimizes disruption.
