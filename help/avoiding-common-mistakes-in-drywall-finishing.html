Avoiding Common Mistakes in Drywall Finishing

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting that perfect, smooth drywall finish can be tricky, and it’s easy to make a few common mistakes that can really show up later. One big one is applying joint compound too thickly. You might think more is better, but it actually just leads to more sanding and a lumpy finish.

Thin, even coats are key, and let each one dry completely before you even think about the next. Another frequent error is sanding too aggressively. You can easily create dips or grooves that will be super obvious once paint goes on.

Be gentle and use fine-grit sandpaper. Skipping primer is also a big no-no! Primer helps the paint absorb evenly, preventing those blotchy spots and making sure your hard work looks great.

And don't rush the process; drywall finishing takes time because of all the drying required between coats. If you’re aiming for a super smooth, professional look, especially in a large basement area, sometimes it’s worth bringing in a pro like HH Remodeling LLC to make sure it’s done right.
