Key Steps for a Successful Accessibility Home Renovation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Undertaking an accessibility renovation can seem like a large project, but by breaking it down into manageable steps, you can ensure a successful and satisfying outcome. Here’s a typical timeline to guide you through the process:

Phase 1: Initial Assessment and Planning (Approx. 1-2 months)

This phase starts with understanding your specific needs. We’ll assess your current home layout, measure clearances for doorways and hallways, and check thresholds and stairs to identify all potential barriers. This is also where we prioritize changes based on your daily routines and future mobility considerations. It's critical to consider how you use each space and what features would enhance your independence and safety. During this time, we’ll also discuss potential design solutions and gather your ideas and preferences.

Phase 2: Design and Permitting (Approx. 2-6 months, varies by scope)

Once we have a clear understanding of your needs, we move into detailed design. This involves creating plans for features like ramps, roll-in showers, grab bars, and widened passages. For projects involving plumbing, electrical, structural changes, or lifts, securing necessary permits is a key part of this phase. We'll handle the permit applications, which can sometimes extend this timeline, especially if third-party funding or complex approvals are involved. For HH Remodeling LLC, open communication throughout this process is really important so you’re always in the loop.

Phase 3: Construction and Completion (Approx. 2-6 months, varies by scope)

With designs finalized and permits in hand, construction begins. Our team, led by Abraham Becerra, focuses on executing the plan efficiently and to the highest standards. This might involve custom framing, drywall, tile installation, or finish carpentry, depending on your project.

We coordinate all sub-trades, like plumbers and electricians, to ensure seamless progress. Throughout construction, we maintain open communication, addressing any questions or adjustments promptly. Upon completion, we’ll do a final walk-through to ensure every detail meets your expectations and provides the accessibility you need.

Remember, the entire process from consultation to completion can range from a few months to over a year for more extensive projects, especially when third-party funding is involved, so patience is key.
