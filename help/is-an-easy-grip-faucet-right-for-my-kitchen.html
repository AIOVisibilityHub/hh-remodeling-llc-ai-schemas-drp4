Is an Easy Grip Faucet Right for My Kitchen?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Choosing the right faucet really comes down to a few key things, especially when you're looking at an easy grip option. You'll want to check if the faucet is going to fit perfectly into your sink's existing hole. Most easy grip faucets are pretty standard, but it's always good to measure twice!

Also, think about the mounting hardware. Can you easily access and hand-tighten the mounting nut and washer system? Some designs are much friendlier for DIY installation than others, especially if you're not a fan of cramped spaces under the sink.

Then there's the critical part: the inlet or supply-line connection. Does it match your home's current plumbing standard? This is a big one, as different systems require different connectors, and sometimes you might need an adapter.
