Preventing Glare with Bathroom Night Lighting

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Nighttime trips to the bathroom shouldn’t involve being blasted by a bright light that jolts you awake. The whole point of bathroom night lighting is to provide enough visibility to navigate safely without creating harsh glare. It's a delicate balance, but totally achievable with a few smart strategies.

A frequent mistake we see is installing night lights that are simply too bright. Remember, the goal isn't to illuminate the entire room like it's daytime. You want a subtle glow. Another common error is placing night lights where they shine directly into your eyes, which defeats the purpose of gentle illumination. Think about where you walk and how the light will hit you at a low level.

Instead of a single overhead light, which can create strong shadows and glare, consider a low-level path light approach. This means choosing lights that direct their illumination downward or are subtly integrated into the design. Under-vanity lighting, for example, casts a soft glow on the floor, outlining your path without shining directly into your eyes.

Motion-activated lights can be incredibly effective here, as they only turn on when you need them and at a pre-set low brightness. When selecting your night lights, really focus on the brightness level. Look for options with adjustable settings or those specifically designed for low-level output.

Also, ensure that any light installed in moisture-exposed locations, like near a shower or tub, is appropriately damp- or wet-rated for safety.
