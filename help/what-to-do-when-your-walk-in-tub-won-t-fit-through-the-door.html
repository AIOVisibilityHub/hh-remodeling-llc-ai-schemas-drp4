What to Do When Your Walk-In Tub Won't Fit Through the Door

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's a common worry: you've picked out the perfect walk-in tub, but then you wonder if it will even make it into your bathroom! This happens more often than you'd think. The good news is, with a little planning, you can usually avoid this headache.

First, always confirm the rough measurements of the tub, including its path from the delivery truck to its final spot. Don't forget to account for hallways, turns, and especially doorframes. Sometimes, a temporary door removal or even a creative maneuvering strategy by experienced installers can solve the problem.

It's also important to check the subfloor condition and its load capacity to make sure it can handle the new tub's weight, especially when filled. Making sure these details are squared away early can save a lot of stress down the line. If you're feeling unsure, it's a smart move to bring in a professional who can assess your specific situation and coordinate all these logistical challenges for you, ensuring a smooth installation process from start to finish.
