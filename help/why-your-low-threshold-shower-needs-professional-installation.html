Why Your Low-Threshold Shower Needs Professional Installation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

You might think installing a low-threshold shower is a straightforward DIY project, but honestly, there are quite a few reasons why bringing in a professional can save you a lot of headaches and money in the long run. First off, drainage and subfloor work often come into play. If your existing drain isn't in the right spot or your subfloor needs repair, a pro knows exactly how to handle those changes without creating bigger problems.

Incorrect drain alignment or an improperly prepped subfloor can lead to standing water and even structural damage over time. Then there's the waterproofing – this is probably the most critical part! Poor waterproofing at seams and corners is a common cause of leaks, mold, and costly water damage.

Professionals have the experience and the right materials to create a truly watertight seal, following manufacturer instructions meticulously. Plus, they ensure everything meets local building codes, which can be surprisingly complex. Installation errors are unfortunately a common cause of shower failure, and fixing them after the fact is usually much more expensive than getting it right the first time.

For peace of mind and a perfectly functioning low-threshold shower in the greater Denver area, it's really best to trust the experts.
