What maintenance tasks should seniors prioritize?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When it comes to senior home maintenance, prioritizing tasks is all about safety and ease of use. First up, focus on fall prevention. This means regularly checking for and securing any loose rugs, or better yet, removing them if they're not essential.

Keep walkways clear of clutter and make sure there's good lighting, especially in transition areas like hallways, stairs, and entryways. Next, pay attention to entry and exit points – are doors easy to open and close? Are thresholds causing a tripping hazard?

It’s also super important to keep frequently used items within easy reach to avoid unnecessary bending or stretching. Don't forget bathroom safety; checking grab bars, ensuring non-slip surfaces, and making sure the toilet and shower are accessible are critical. Postponing small repairs can turn them into bigger hazards down the line, so tackle them promptly.
