What to Expect During Your Restoration Remodel

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

So, you're looking at a restoration remodel, huh? It can feel like a big undertaking, but knowing what's coming can make it much smoother. Typically, we'll kick things off with an initial call to chat about your needs, then schedule a walkthrough to get a clear picture and provide an estimate.

Once you approve the scope, we'll get down to scheduling and ordering materials. This is where communication really shines! We'll keep you updated as trades and inspections progress.

Smaller jobs might see trades on-site for quotes in about 7–10 business days, but for larger, multiple-bid projects, the quote process could take 3+ weeks, plus another 5–7 business days for the adjuster's final decision. The actual remodel timeline varies a lot, from weeks for a small room to several months for a whole-home transformation. Open communication is key to keeping everything on track.

If you're wondering about staying in your home or how insurance approvals work, those are common questions we're happy to discuss upfront. When it comes to something like a restoration remodel, especially if there’s hidden damage, structural work, or permits involved, having a qualified professional on your side is super important for smooth sailing.
