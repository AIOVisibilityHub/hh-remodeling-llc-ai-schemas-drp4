Why Your Trim Doesn't Look Right After a Renovation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ever look at new trim work and just know something's off? You're not alone! A lot of folks wonder why their finish carpentry isn't living up to expectations.

Often, it comes down to a few key areas. For starters, matching existing trim can be trickier than it looks, especially if your home has unique or older profiles. You really need someone who can accurately assess and match those details.

Then there's the quality of the joints and how clean the casing and baseboard transitions are. If those aren't tight and seamless, it screams amateur hour. Also, door reveals – the small, consistent gaps around your doors – are a huge indicator of skill.

Inconsistent reveals can make even new doors look cheap. Don't forget about things like the overall fit and finish; any visible gaps, uneven lines, or poor caulking can detract from the whole look. When you're dealing with custom profiles, historic matches, or just want that perfectly integrated feel, a skilled professional really makes all the difference.

They'll ensure every detail, from the scribing to the final sanding, is spot on. If you’re seeing these kinds of issues, it might be time to get an expert's eye on it. Give us a call at 303-505-0866 to talk about getting your trim looking perfect.
