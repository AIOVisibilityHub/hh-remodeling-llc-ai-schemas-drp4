The Essential Checklist for Storm Damage Repair Documentation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Dealing with storm damage is stressful enough without adding insurance claim headaches to the mix. Getting your documentation in order is key, and this checklist will help you gather everything you need. First, before any cleanup or temporary fixes, take a ton of photos and videos.

We're talking high-resolution images from multiple angles, close-ups, and wide shots of all damaged areas. Don't forget anything – the roof, siding, windows, interior water stains, broken fences, and even damaged personal property. Next, make a detailed list of every item damaged or destroyed.

Be specific with descriptions, brands, and estimated values if you know them. Keep any receipts for emergency repairs you make, like tarps or plywood, as these might be reimbursable. Also, save any communication you have with your insurance company, noting dates, times, and who you spoke with.

When you get estimates from contractors, ensure they're written, itemized, and clearly outline the scope of work.
