Why Won't My New Paint Last?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ever wonder why your freshly painted walls might not hold up as long as you'd hoped? It often comes down to a few key factors that can be easily overlooked. One common issue is skipping proper surface preparation.

If walls aren't cleaned, patched, and sanded correctly, the paint won't adhere well and can start peeling or flaking prematurely. Another big one is neglecting to prime, especially over patched areas or when changing drastically dark colors. Primer creates a smooth, uniform surface that helps the paint achieve its true color and provides better adhesion.

The quality and type of paint also play a huge role; using a cheap, low-grade paint might save money upfront but can cost you in durability and appearance over time. Lastly, not applying enough coats can lead to a less durable finish that wears down quicker.
