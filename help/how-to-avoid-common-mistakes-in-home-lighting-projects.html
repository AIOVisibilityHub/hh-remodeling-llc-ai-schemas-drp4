How to Avoid Common Mistakes in Home Lighting Projects

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Tackling a home lighting project can be exciting, but it's easy to fall into some common traps if you're not careful. Here are some frequent mistakes and how to steer clear of them:

  • Overloading a fixture with the wrong wattage: Always check the maximum wattage rating on your light fixtures and use bulbs that don't exceed it. Too much wattage can cause overheating and fire hazards.
  • Using the wrong bulb type: Not all bulbs are created equal! Make sure you're using bulbs that are compatible with your fixture, especially for specialized lighting like dimmable or outdoor options.
  • Skipping the power shutoff: This might seem obvious, but always, always turn off the power at the breaker before working on any electrical fixture. It's a fundamental safety step you can't afford to miss.
  • Ignoring signs of unsafe wiring: If you notice warm outlets, discolored switch plates, or a persistent burning smell, don't ignore it! These are serious warning signs that warrant immediate professional attention.
  • Underestimating the need for permits: For new wiring, major fixture additions, or work in wet locations like bathrooms, permits and inspections are often required. Skipping these can lead to unsafe installations and future headaches.
