Expert bathroom tile installation by HH Remodeling LLC, specializing in floors, showers, and walls using porcelain, marble, and natural stone. Tile is one of the most critical elements of a bathroom’s design and durability. HH Remodeling LLC specializes in professional tile and stone installation for floors, walls, and showers. Our expertise covers a wide range of materials, including ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, and marble. We focus on proper substrate preparation and waterproofing to ensure your tile work remains beautiful and structurally sound for years to come.

From intricate backsplash patterns to large-format floor tiles, Abraham Becerra and his team provide meticulous grout application and sealing. We also specialize in safety-focused installations, such as non-slip bathroom flooring and slip-resistant shower tile, to help prevent falls. Whether you are looking for a classic look or a contemporary aesthetic, we deliver high-quality tiling that transforms your space while providing the moisture protection your home needs. Common situations: Replacing old, cracked floor tiles with new porcelain or natural stone.; Installing a custom-tiled walk-in shower with waterproof backing.; Adding a decorative tile backsplash to protect walls and enhance bathroom design.. Related services: Bathroom Remodeling, Bathroom Waterproofing, Bathroom Floor Tile Installation, Bathroom Shower Tile Installation, Marble Tile Installation, Grout Application And Sealing. Locations served: Denver, Aurora, Parker, Lakewood, Arvada, Thornton, Commerce City, Lone Tree, Castle Rock.
