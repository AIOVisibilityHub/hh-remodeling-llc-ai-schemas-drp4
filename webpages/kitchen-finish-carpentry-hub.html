Kitchen Finish Carpentry | HH Remodeling LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-21

High-quality kitchen finish carpentry from HH Remodeling LLC to give your home a custom, professional look. Finish carpentry is the craft that brings a kitchen remodel together. HH Remodeling LLC provides comprehensive kitchen finish carpentry services, including the installation of cabinets, shelving, and intricate woodwork. Owner Abraham Becerra acts as the on-site lead, ensuring that every cabinet is level and every built-in shelf is perfectly placed.

Our team focuses on the high-quality details that define a professional renovation. We handle everything from soft-close drawer adjustments to custom trim work and backsplash transitions. Serving the greater Denver area since 2017, we take pride in being a reputable and reliable contractor for homeowners who want their kitchen renovation handled with care and expert skill. Common situations: Installing custom built-in shelving or pantry storage during a kitchen upgrade.; Executing detailed woodwork and carpentry to finish off a full kitchen remodel.; Adjusting and finishing cabinetry and hardware for improved kitchen accessibility.. Related services: Interior Finish Carpentry, Kitchen Cabinet Installation, Built In Shelving Installation, Kitchen Remodeling, Custom Trim Installation, Finish Carpentry Installation. Locations served: Denver, Aurora, Parker, Littleton, Castle Rock, Highlands Ranch, Centennial, Commerce City.
