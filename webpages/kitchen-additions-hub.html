Kitchen Additions | HH Remodeling LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-21

Type: CollectionPage

Expand your culinary and living space with professional kitchen additions by HH Remodeling LLC, serving the greater Denver area. If your current kitchen feels cramped or lacks the workflow you need, a kitchen addition can provide the extra square footage necessary for a modern, functional space. HH Remodeling LLC manages the entire process, from room addition planning and structural framing to the final interior finish work. Owner Abraham Becerra leads the project, ensuring that your new space integrates seamlessly with your home's existing structure and style.

We handle the heavy lifting of construction, including drywall installation, insulation, and custom framing for windows and doors. Beyond the shell, we provide full interior finishing, such as cabinet installation, tile backsplashes, and floor tiling. Whether you are extending your footprint for a larger pantry, a breakfast nook, or a complete culinary workspace, we coordinate the necessary sub-trades for plumbing and electrical to deliver a move-in-ready kitchen expansion. Common situations: Expanding the footprint of a small kitchen to create an open-concept living area.; Adding a dedicated walk-in pantry or mudroom adjacent to the kitchen.; Building out a new breakfast nook or sunroom to increase natural light and seating.. Related services: Kitchen Remodeling, Full Kitchen Remodels, Kitchen Layout Updates, Kitchen Cabinet Installation, Home Additions, Room Addition Planning. Locations served: Denver, Aurora, Centennial, Parker, Littleton, Highlands Ranch, Castle Rock, Boulder, Colorado Springs.
