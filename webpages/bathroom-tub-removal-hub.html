Start your bathroom renovation on the right foot with expert tub removal services from HH Remodeling LLC, your trusted partner for interior demolition and restoration in Colorado. Removing an old, bulky, or damaged bathtub is often the first step toward a more modern and functional bathroom layout. HH Remodeling LLC provides professional tub removal services, ensuring the old unit is carefully extracted without causing unnecessary damage to your surrounding walls or subflooring. Abraham Becerra personally oversees these projects, coordinating the necessary steps to prepare your space for whatever comes next, whether it is a new installation or a tub-to-shower conversion.

A tub removal often reveals hidden issues like water damage or outdated plumbing. Our crew is equipped to handle these challenges immediately, offering drywall repair and interior restoration to ensure your bathroom's structure is sound. We focus on efficiency and reliability, helping Denver-area homeowners clear the way for their ideal bathroom renovation while maintaining a clean and safe workspace. Common situations: Removing a damaged or cracked alcove tub that is leaking into the subfloor.; Clearing out an old garden tub to make room for a modern walk-in shower.; Prepping a bathroom for a full footprint reconfiguration during a remodel.. Related services: Bathroom Renovation, Full Bathroom Remodels, Tub To Shower Conversion, Bathroom Drywall Repair, Interior Damage Repair, Water Damage Interior Repair.
