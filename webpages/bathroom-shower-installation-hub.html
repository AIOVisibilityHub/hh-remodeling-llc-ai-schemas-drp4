Expert bathroom shower installation and tub-to-shower conversions by HH Remodeling LLC, providing durable, waterproof, and accessible bathing solutions. Upgrade your daily routine with a professional shower installation. Whether you are looking for a complete tub-to-shower conversion or a modern low-threshold walk-in shower, HH Remodeling LLC manages the process from start to finish. Owner Abraham Becerra oversees every detail, from initial shower removal and waterproofing to the final tile and stone placement. Our team ensures a leak-free, durable installation that balances aesthetic appeal with daily functionality.

We specialize in accessible modifications, including curbless shower conversions and the installation of safety features like grab bars and fold-down seats. By coordinating necessary sub-trades for plumbing, we act as your single point of contact for a streamlined renovation. From simple fiberglass units to custom-tiled enclosures, we provide high-quality workmanship at reasonable prices for homeowners across the Denver metro area. Common situations: Converting an old, unused bathtub into a modern walk-in shower.; Installing a low-threshold or curbless shower for aging-in-place safety.; Replacing a damaged or leaking shower stall with updated tile and fixtures.. Related services: Bathroom Remodeling, Bathroom Tub Installation, Bathroom Waterproofing, Bathroom Tile Installation, Tub To Shower Conversion, Accessible Bathroom Renovation. Locations served: Denver, Aurora, Centennial, Parker, Littleton, Highlands Ranch, Lakewood, Arvada, Thornton, Colorado Springs.
