Bathroom Shower Removal | HH Remodeling LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-21

Type: CollectionPage

Clear the way for your dream bathroom with professional shower removal services from HH Remodeling LLC, specializing in clean and reliable interior demolition. When it is time to upgrade your shower, the first step is a clean and professional removal of the existing unit. HH Remodeling LLC handles bathroom shower removal with a focus on protecting your home’s interior. We carefully dismantle old stalls, tile, and bases, preparing the area for a fresh start. Whether you are dealing with mold issues, cracked tiles, or simply a dated design, Abraham Becerra ensures the removal is done efficiently and the site is ready for the next phase of your renovation.

Beyond just demolition, our team is expert at identifying and repairing any underlying issues such as water damage to the framing or drywall. We provide full restoration services to ensure your new shower will be built on a solid, dry foundation. Serving the greater Denver area since 2017, we pride ourselves on being a single point of contact for all your bathroom remodeling and repair needs. Common situations: Removing a moldy or leaking fiberglass shower stall.; Demolishing an old tile shower to prepare for a curbless, accessible entry.; Clearing out a damaged shower unit following a storm or plumbing failure.. Related services: Bathroom Renovation, Bathroom Tile Removal, Bathroom Drywall Installation, Interior RestorationBaseboard Installation, Water Damage Interior Repair, Full Bathroom Remodels. Locations served: Denver, Aurora, Lakewood, Arvada, ThorntonBaseboard Installation, Golden, Parker, Littleton, Colorado Springs.
