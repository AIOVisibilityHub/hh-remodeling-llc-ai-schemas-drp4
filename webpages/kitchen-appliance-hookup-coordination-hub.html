Professional coordination of electrical and plumbing sub-trades for seamless kitchen appliance installation and hookup. Managing the final stages of a kitchen remodel requires precise coordination to ensure all appliances are functional and safe. HH Remodeling LLC provides kitchen appliance hookup coordination as part of our general contracting services. Abraham Becerra acts as the primary contact, managing the project and coordinating necessary sub-trades for plumbing and electrical work. This ensures that your dishwasher, oven, and refrigerator are connected correctly and meet all local safety standards.

We take the stress out of the remodeling process by handling the logistics of trade coordination and permit support. Whether we are finishing a basement kitchen or completing a full-scale home renovation, our crew remains focused on the carpentry, drywall, and finish work while ensuring that all mechanical connections are handled by qualified professionals. We serve a wide range of locations, from Colorado Springs to Thornton, providing reliable and efficient service for every client. Common situations: Coordinating electrical and plumbing trades to hook up appliances during a full kitchen remodel.; Ensuring proper connections for new appliances after a storm damage restoration project.; Managing appliance installation and hookups for a foreclosed property refresh.. Related services: General Contractor Project Management, Sub Trade Coordination For Plumbing, Sub Trade Coordination For Electrical, Residential General Contracting, Full Kitchen Remodels, Restoration Remodeling. Locations served: Denver, Aurora, Thornton, Colorado Springs monument, Centennial, Littleton, Lakewood, Arvada.
