Professional bathroom remodeling by HH Remodeling LLC brings quality craftsmanship and expert tile work to your Denver area home. HH Remodeling LLC offers comprehensive bathroom remodeling services designed to update your space with high-quality craftsmanship at reasonable prices. As an A+ rated BBB business, we focus on every detail, from initial demolition to the final coat of paint. Abraham Becerra and his crew specialize in transforming outdated bathrooms into functional retreats through expert tile and marble installation, vanity mounting, and fixture upgrades.

We handle all aspects of the remodel, including drywall repair, interior painting, and finish carpentry such as trim and molding installation. Our team is also highly experienced in accessibility modifications, including walk-in shower conversions and grab bar installations. We serve homeowners across the Denver metro area, providing open communication and reliable project management to ensure your renovation meets your specific needs. Common situations: Updating outdated fixtures and finishes to improve home value.; Converting an old bathtub into a modern walk-in shower.; Repairing water-damaged drywall and flooring during a bathroom refresh.. Related services: Full Bathroom Remodels, Bathroom Renovation, Bathroom Tile Installation, Bathroom Waterproofing, Accessible Bathroom Renovation, Vanity Installation. Locations served: Denver, Aurora, Lakewood, Arvada, Thornton, Westminster, Centennial, Englewood, Littleton.
