Professional tile and stone installation for kitchen backsplashes, floors, and walls, featuring expert craftsmanship and durable finishes. At HH Remodeling LLC, we provide professional kitchen tile installation to help you achieve a durable and beautiful finish in your home. Abraham Becerra and his crew handle every detail of the process, including backsplash tile installation, floor tile installation, and wall tiling. We work with a wide variety of materials, including ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, and marble, ensuring that each piece is set with precision and finished with expert grout application and sealing.

Whether you are looking for a classic subway tile backsplash or a sophisticated marble floor, our team focuses on high-quality craftsmanship and attention to detail. We serve homeowners across the greater Denver area, from Aurora to Boulder, providing reliable interior finishing that stands the test of time. As an owner-operated business, we take pride in delivering affordable prices and professional results for every kitchen renovation project. Common situations: Replacing an outdated or stained kitchen backsplash with modern porcelain or ceramic tile.; Installing new, slip-resistant floor tiling during a full kitchen remodel.; Updating kitchen walls with natural stone or marble for a high-end look.. Related services: Kitchen Backsplash Installation, Kitchen Flooring Installation, Ceramic Tile Installation, Marble Tile Installation, Kitchen Renovation, Interior Finishing. Locations served: Denver, Aurora, Centennial, Littleton, Highlands Ranch, Parker, Lakewood, Arvada, Thornton, Boulder, Colorado Springs.
