Kitchen Backsplash Installation | HH Remodeling LLC

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-23

Type: CollectionPage

Custom kitchen backsplash installation services to protect your walls and enhance your kitchen’s design. A new backsplash is a functional and aesthetic necessity for any modern kitchen. HH Remodeling LLC provides professional installation for a wide range of materials, including ceramic, porcelain, and natural stone. Abraham Becerra and his crew focus on the fine details of finish carpentry and tile work to ensure your backsplash perfectly complements your cabinetry and countertops.

We handle every step of the installation, from wall preparation and drywall patching to grout application and sealing. Whether you are looking for a simple protective surface or a custom design feature, our team provides the reliable service and open communication needed to bring your vision to life. We serve residential properties throughout the Denver metro area, offering expert craftsmanship at reasonable prices. Common situations: Adding a protective backsplash behind sinks and stoves during a renovation.; Updating the look of a kitchen with custom tile or stone work.; Installing new backsplashes in rental properties or foreclosed home refreshes.. Related services: Kitchen Backsplash Tile Installation, Ceramic Tile Installation, Porcelain Tile Installation, Kitchen Remodeling, Drywall Finishing, Grout Application And Sealing. Locations served: Denver, Centennial, Greenwood Village, Lone Tree, Cherry Hills Village, Arvada, Lakewood, Superior, Lafayette, Erie.
